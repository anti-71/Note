##### 原文

45. $\underline{\qquad}$

（1）The most fevered followers of Allen’s organizational methodology gather online.（2）Websites like gtdindex.marvelz.com parse Allen’s every utterance.（3）The 43Folders blog ran an eight-part pod-cast interview with him.（4）GTD enthusiasts like Frank Meeuwsen, on whatsthenextaction.com gather best practice techniques for implementing the book’s ideas.（5）More than 60 software tools have been built specifically to supplement Allen’s system.

##### 分析

先通篇略读，发现没有明显转折词，故先读段首句：

最狂热的对 A 的有组织的方法论的追随者在网上聚集

这题比较简单，一是 followers 对应选项 A 的 follow，二是 online 对应选项 A 的 on lines，故应选 A
