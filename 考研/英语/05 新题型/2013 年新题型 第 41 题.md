##### 原文

> [!question] 
> Yet this enormous resource is not contributing enough to today's global challenges, including climate change, security, sustainable development and health.（41）$\underline{\qquad}$ Humanity has the necessary agro-technological tools to eradicate hunger, from genetically engineered crops to artificial fertilizers. Here, too, the problems are social: the organization and distribution of food, wealth and prosperity.

##### 分析

读挖空的前一句：

有一个 yet，对前面内容的转折，对做题无用

including ... 是对 challenges 的举例，同义替换选项 E 和 F 中的 issues 和 problems，都可以选，待定

读挖空后一句：

好像在讲一句好的事，先放着

> [!note] 
> 没有明确依据，可以先做下一题

做完第 42 题，排除了 F，故第 41 题应选意思偏好的 E
