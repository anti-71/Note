##### 原文

44. $\underline{\qquad}$

（1）Few companies have embraced Allen’s philosophy as thoroughly as General Mills, the Minnesota-based maker of Cheerios and Lucky Charms.（2）Allen began at the company with a couple of private coaching sessions for top executives, who raved about his guidance.（3）Allen and his staff now hold six to eight two-day training sessions a year.（4）The company has already put more than 2,000 employees through GTD training and plans to expand it company-wide.（5）“Fads come and go,” says Kevin Wilde, General Mills’ CEO, “but this continues to work.”

##### 分析

先通篇略读，发现句内转折词 but，故先读第（5）句：

KW（GM 公司的 CEO）说：“潮流来和去，但是这一直起作用”

往选项上靠，猜想在说坚持一个什么东西，待选 C，再去看段首句：

很少有像 GM 这样的公司拥护 A 的哲学如此彻底

可以看出，确实是在将坚持，故确定 C
