##### 原文

> [!question] 
> The time for sharpening pencils，arranging your desk，and doing almost anything else instead of writing has ended. The first draft will appear on the page only if you stop avoiding the inevitable and sit，stand up，or lie down to write（41）$\underline{\qquad}$

##### 分析

先看挖空前一句：

看到 the first draft 和 write，分别对应[[2008 年新题型 选项#选项 G|选项 G]] 和[[2008 年新题型 选项#选项 D|选项 D]] 的复现，此时只能比较哪个选项的意思更通顺

体会原文，说坐着、站着或躺着写，明显在说怎么写，符合选项 D，故第 41 题应选 D
