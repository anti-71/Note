##### 原文

> [!question] 
> The problem is not necessarily the amount of available funding.（44）$\underline{\qquad}$ This is an adequate amount so long as it is aimed in the right direction. Social scientists who complain about a lack of funding should not expect more in today’s economic climate.

此题先看挖空后一句，因为明显的是：这是一个足够的数量

观察各个选项，只有[[2013 年新题型 选项#选项 G|选项 G]] 出现了大量的数字，故第 44 题应选 G
