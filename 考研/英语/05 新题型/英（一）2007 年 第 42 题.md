##### 原文

42. $\underline{\qquad}$

（1）Kids need a range of authentic role models—as opposed to members of their clique, pop stars and vaunted athletes.（2）Have regular dinner-table discussions about people the family knows and how they got where they are.（3）Discuss the joys and downsides of your own career and encourage your kids to form some ideas about their own future.（4）When asked what they want to do, they should be discouraged from saying “I have no idea.”（5）They can change their minds 200 times, **but** having only a foggy view of the future is of little good.

##### 分析

先通篇略读，发现转折词 but（句内转折也要考虑），故先读第（5）句：

他们可以改变想法 200 次，但是对未来抱有一个模糊的观点有点不好

对应选项，看到 future 同时在该句和选项 D 中出现，但不能确定选 D，而是要选项与原文 “双向奔赴”

选项中有 “讨论” 和 “定期”，简要扫一遍原文（不用仔细翻译），发现 Have regular dinner-table discussions 和 Discuss 都能与选项对应，最终确定选项 D
