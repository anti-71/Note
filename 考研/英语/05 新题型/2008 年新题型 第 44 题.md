##### 原文

> [!question] 
> If you are working on a word processor, you can take advantage of its capacity to make additions and deletions as well as move entire paragraphs by making just a few simple keyboard commands. Some software programs can also check spelling and certain grammatical elements in your writing.（44）$\underline{\qquad}$ These printouts are also easier to read than the screen when you work on revisions.

##### 分析

看到下一句有代词 these，先读挖空下一句：

these 后跟 printouts，观察哪个选项跟 print 有关

[[2008 年新题型 选项#选项 C|选项 C]] 中出现 printer 和 print，故第 44 题应选 C
