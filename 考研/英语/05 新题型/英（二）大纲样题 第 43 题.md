##### 原文

43. $\underline{\qquad}$

（1）When e-mails, phone calls and to-do lists are truly under control, Allen says, the real change begins.（2）You will finally be able to use your mind to dream up great ideas and enjoy your life rather than just occupy it with all the things you’ve got to do.（3）Allen himself, despite running a $5.5 million consulting practice, traveling 200 days a year and juggling a business that’s growing 40% every year, finds time to joyride in his Mini Cooper and sculpt bonsai plants.（4）Oh, and he has earned his black belt in karate.

##### 分析

先通篇略读，发现有句中转折词 despite，故先读第（3）句

但扫一眼，出现大量数字，如 $5.5、200、40%，大概率是举例子的部分，而不是核心内容，故还是回到段首句：

A 说：“当电子邮件、电话和待办清单确实在控制之下时，真正的改变开始了”

原文 “在控制之下” 可以对应选项 G 中的秩序，而选项中的效率是主题词，应是全文围绕的内容，故 G 符合
