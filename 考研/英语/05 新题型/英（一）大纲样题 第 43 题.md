##### 原文

43. $\underline{\qquad}$

（1）Plagiarism by accident, or oversight, sometimes is the result of the writer’s inability to decide or remember where the idea came from.（2）He may have read it long ago, heard it in a lecture since forgotten, or acquired it second-hand or third-hand from discussions with colleagues.（3）He may also have difficulty in deciding whether the idea is such common knowledge that no reference to the original source is needed.（4）Although this type of plagiarism must be guarded against, it is the least serious and, if lessons learned, can be exempt from being severely punished.

##### 分析

先通篇略读，发现转折词 “Although”，故先读第（4）句：

尽管这种剽窃必须被提防，但它并不严重并且如果能吸取教训，可以免受严重处罚

“可以免受处罚” 替换选项 D 的 “can be forgiven”
