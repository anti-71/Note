##### 原文

44. $\underline{\qquad}$

（1）Playing video games encourages immediate content.（2）And hours of watching TV shows with canned laughter only teaches kids to process information in a passive way.（3）At the same time, listening through earphones to the same monotonous beats for long stretches encourages kids to stay inside their bubble instead of pursuing other endeavors.（4）All these activities can prevent the growth of important communication and thinking skills and make it difficult for kids to develop the kind of sustained concentration they will need for most jobs.

##### 分析

先通篇略读，发现没有转折词，故先读段首句：

玩游戏鼓励孩子的即时满足

首先 “玩游戏” 可以与选项 C 的 “休闲活动” 对上，但是 “限制时间” 呢？

第（2）（3）句都涉及时间，但选项 E “应对的策略” 感觉也说得通

> [!note] 
> 不急，再看到下一题

做完第五题之后，排除了 E 选项，那只能选出最合理的 C 选项了
