##### 原文

（43）$\underline{\qquad}$ Your pages will be easier to keep track of that way, and, if you have to clip a paragraph to place it elsewhere, you will not lose any writing (on the other side.)

##### 分析

出现在段首，先读挖空下一句：

看到括号里 on the other side，马上联想到[[2008 年新题型 选项#选项 A|选项 A]] 的 one side，结合 will be easier，故第 43 题应选 A
