- 正常结构：`elect A as B` I elect A as B. 我选择 A 作为 B
- **宾语后置**：`elect as B A` I elect as B A. 我选择 A 作为 B

> [!question] 
> - 为什么会有宾语后置？宾语太长了，不符合英语表达习惯
> - 如何识别？发现动词和 as 紧挨着，考虑可能有宾语后置

##### 例 1 elect as B A

> [!question]
> What is an intellectual? I shall define him as an individual who has **elected as** (his primary duty and pleasure in life) (the activity of thinking in a Socratic way about moral problems).

主干：我将他定义为一个个体（define A as B 把 A 定义为 B）

**还原结构：** who has **elected** (the activity of thinking in a Socratic way about moral problems) **as** (his primary duty and pleasure in life)

| 成分 | 还原 |
|---|---|
| 绿色宾语（被后置） | （以苏格拉底方式来思考道德问题）→ 词性转变 |
| 黄色宾语补足语 | （他生活中的首要责任和快乐） |

**译文：** 我将他（知识分子）定义为一个个体，他把以苏格拉底方式来思考道德问题看作是他生活中的首要责任和快乐。

> [!tip] 翻译技巧：词性转变 / 调整语序（宾语后置）

##### 例 2 accept as B A

> [!question]
> On the other hand, he **did not accept as** well founded the charge made by some of his critics that, while he was a good observer, he had no power of reasoning.

- **结构还原：** accept A as B = accept as B A
- **还原：** he **did not accept** the charge made by some of his critics **as** well founded
