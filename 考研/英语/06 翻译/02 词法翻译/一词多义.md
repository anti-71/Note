**词无本义，义由境生** —— 在语境中选择意思

##### 分类

1. **词性不同，词义不同**
2. **词性相同，语境不同，词义不同**

> [!tip] 
> 1. 每个单词基本都不止一个意思，每句话都要考虑词义选择
> 2. 死背单个单词意思不能解决词义选择问题，解决方案是多看句子 / 多看搭配

##### 真题讲解

###### preserve

| 词性  | 词义               |
| --- | ---------------- |
| v.  | 保留，保存            |
| n.  | 保护区；**专属领域（特权）** |

> [!question]
> Traditionally, legal learning has been viewed in such institutions as the special **preserve** of lawyers, rather than a necessary part of the intellectual equipment of an educated person.

**词义选择：** preserve n. 专属领域

**译文：** 法律学习被视为律师的 **专属 / 特权**

> [!question]
> Time was when biologists somewhat overworked the evidence that these creatures **preserve** the health of game by killing the physically weak, or that they prey only on "worthless" species.

**词义选择：** preserve v. 保存 / 保留；game n. 猎物

**译文：** 这些生物 **保留 / 保护** 猎物的健康

###### intelligence

| 词义         |
| ---------- |
| n. 智力 / 智商 |
| n. 情报 / 信息 |

> [!question]
> The defining term of **intelligence** in humans still seems to be the IQ score, even though IQ tests are not given as often as they used to be.

**词义选择：** intelligence n. 智商

**译文：** 对人类 **智力 / 智商** 的定义

> [!question]
> All in all, this clearly seems to be a market in which big retailers could profitably apply their gigantic scale, existing infrastructure, and proven skills in the management of product ranges, logistics, and marketing **intelligence**.

**词义选择：** intelligence n. 信息 / 情报

**译文：** 在（产品范围、物流、和市场 **信息**）的管理方面
