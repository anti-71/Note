##### 题干

> [!question] 
> 36. What do American parents expect their children to acquire in **school** ?
> 37. We can learn from the text that Americans have a history of $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 38. The views of Ravitch and Emerson on **schooling** are $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 39. Emerson, according to the text, is probably $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 40. What does the author think of **intellect** ?

##### 题干串读

第 40 题问作者对 intellect（才智）的态度，那么这很有可能是全文的主题

且第 36、38 题中出现了 school 和 schooling，同样与 intellect 相关
