##### 题干

> [!question] 
> 15. What used to be **the danger in being a man** according to the first paragraph?
> 16. What does the example of India illustrate?
> 17. The author argues that **our bodies have stopped evolving** because $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 18. Which of the following would be the best title for the passage?

##### 题干串读

第 15 题提到了 the danger in being a man（作为一个人 / 男人的危险），第 17 题提到了 our bodies have stopped evolving（我们的身体已停止进化）

由此可判断本文主题应与 **人的身体** 有关
