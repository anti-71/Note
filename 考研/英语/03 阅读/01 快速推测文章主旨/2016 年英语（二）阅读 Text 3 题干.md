##### 题干

> [!question] 
> 31. The usual **time-management** techniques don't work because $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 32. The “empty bottles” metaphor illustrates that people feel a pressure to $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 33. Eberle would agree that **scheduling regular times** for **reading** helps $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 34. “**Carry a book** with you at all times” can work if $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 35. The best title for this text could be $\underline{\qquad}$.
>     
>     （A）How to Enjoy Easy Reading
>     
>     （B）How to Find Time to Read
>     
>     （C）How to Set Reading Goals
>     
>     （D）How to Read Extensively

##### 解题

第 31 题中的 time-management（时间管理）= 第 33 题中的 scheduling regular time（安排固定的时间），第 34 题中的 at all times（随时）也与时间有关

第 33 题中的 reading（阅读）= 第 34 题中的 Carry a book（带本书）

由此可见，这篇文章的主题应该与 “读书” 和 “时间” 相关

第 35 题的四个选项中，B 选项（How to Find Time to Read）的契合度最高，预判本题选 B
