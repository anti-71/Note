##### 题干

> [!question] 
> 31. According to Paragraph 1, the author's posts on Twitter $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 
> 32. What does the author say about the **Victorian portraits** he has collected?
> 
> 33. What might **have kept** the **Victorians from smiling** for **pictures** in the 1890s?
> 
> 34. Mark Twain is quoted to show that the **disapproval of smiles** in **pictures** was $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 
> 35. Which of the following questions does the text answer?
>     
>     （A）Why did most Victorians look stern in photographs?
>     
>     （B）When did the Victorians start to view photography differently?
> 	
> 	（C）What made photography develop slowly in the Victorian period?
> 	
> 	（D）How did smiling in photographs become a post-Victorian norm?

##### 解题

很明显，第 32 和第 33 题中都有专有名词 Victorian（维多利亚时代的；维多利亚人）

第 32 题中的 portraits（肖像照）与第 33、第 34 题中的 pictures（照片）是同义词

要特别注意的是，第 33 题中的 “have kept … from smiling” 意为 “让…… 不笑”，与第 34 题中的 disapproval of smiles（不赞同笑）表意相似

总结一下，题干中的重复信息是 “维多利亚人” “照片” “不笑”

第 35 题四个选项中，能与之匹配的只有 A 选项，其中 look stern（看起来很严肃）就是 “不笑” 的同义改写

通过以上几道题我们可以看出，正确答案并非原文信息的直接重复，而是经常会以 **同义改写** 的方式出现，并且对考生的词汇量有一定的要求
