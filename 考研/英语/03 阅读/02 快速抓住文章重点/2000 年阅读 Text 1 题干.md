##### 题干原文

> [!question] 
> 11. The U.S. achieved its **predominance** *after World War Ⅱ* because $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 12. The loss of U.S. **predominance** in the world **economy** *in the 1980s* is manifested in the fact that the American $\underline{\qquad}$.
> 13. What can be inferred from the passage?
> 14. The author seems to believe the revival of the U.S. **economy** *in the 1990s* can be attributed to the $\underline{\qquad}$.

##### 题干串读

第 11 和第 12 题中均出现了 predominance，第 12 和第 14 题中都出现了 economy

同时，题干中还有清晰的时间线索（after World War Ⅱ →i n the 1980s → in the 1990s）和事物发展标志（achieved its predominance → loss of … predominance → revival of the … economy）

由此可预判，文章的主题是 “**美国经济地位的变化**”

第 11 题中的 because 和第 14 题中的 be attributed to 表明这两道题考查原因，阅读过程中要重点关注体现原因的内容
