##### 题目

> [!question]
> Recent decades have seen science move into a convention where engagement in the subject can only be done through institutions such as university.

##### 一刷

最近几十年，科学走向一个惯例：项目研究只能由机构如大学来完成。

##### 评分

得分：1.5 / 2

- `Recent decades` → “最近几十年” ✓
- `science move into a convention` → “科学走向一个惯例” ✓
- `through institutions such as university` → “由机构如大学” ✓（“通过……机构”更贴切）
- `engagement in the subject` → “项目研究” 误译，应为 **“参与这一学科/对科学的参与”**（engagement = 参与，非"项目研究"）
