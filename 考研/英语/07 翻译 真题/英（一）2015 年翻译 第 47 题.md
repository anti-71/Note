##### 题目

> [!question]
> The United States is the product of two principal forces — the immigration of European peoples with their varied ideas, customs, and national characteristics and the impact of a new country which modified these traits.

##### 一刷

美国由两个主要因素产生 —— 一是欧洲人带着他们各式各样的想法、习俗和民族特质移民，二是新世纪的影响，这完善了这些特质。

##### 评分

得分：1.0 / 2

- `the product of two principal forces` → “由两个主要因素产生” ✓
- `immigration of European peoples` → “欧洲人……移民” ✓（"欧洲民族"更准确）
- `varied ideas, customs, and national characteristics` → “各式各样的想法、习俗和民族特质” ✓
- `a new country` → “新世纪” 误译，应为 **“一个新国家/新大陆”**
- `modified these traits` → “完善了这些特质” 不准确，`modified` 意为 **“改变/改造”**（中性），非“完善”（褒义）
