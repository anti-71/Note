##### 题目

> [!question]
> This seems a justification for neglect of those in need, and a rationalization of exploitation, of the superiority of those at the top and the inferiority of those at the bottom.

##### 一刷

这似乎证明忽视了那些有需要的人，并且合理化了剥削和社会阶级的两极分化。

##### 评分

得分：0.5 / 2

- `justification for neglect` → “证明忽视了” 不准确，应为 **“为……忽视而辩护”**
- `rationalization` → “合理化” 可接受，但 **“为……找理由/辩护”** 更准确
- `superiority of those at the top` → **“上层群体的优越”** 漏译
- `inferiority of those at the bottom` → **“底层群体的卑微”** 误译为“两极分化”
- 后半句 `of the superiority...and the inferiority...` 的并列结构完全丢失，整体信息缺失严重
