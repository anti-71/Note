##### 题目

> [!question]
> Before each of their revelations, many thinkers at the time had sustained more ancient ways of thinking, including the geocentric view that the Earth was at the centre of our universe.

##### 一刷

在他们的启示之前，许多思考者在那时保持着更古老的思考方式，包括地理观点：即地球是宇宙的中心。

##### 评分

得分：1.5 / 2

- `Before each of their revelations` → “在他们的启示之前” ✓
- `thinkers at the time` → “思考者在那时” ✓
- `Earth was at the centre of our universe` → “地球是宇宙的中心” ✓
- `geocentric view` → “地理观点” 误译，应为 **“地心说/以地球为中心的观点”**（geocentric ≠ geographic）
