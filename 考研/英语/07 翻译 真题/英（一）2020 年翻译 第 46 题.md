##### 题目

> [!question]
> With the Church’s teachings and ways of thinking being eclipsed by the Renaissance, the gap between the Medieval and modern periods had been bridged, leading to new and unexplored intellectual territories.

##### 一刷

随着教会的教育和思考方式因为文艺复兴黯然失色，中世纪和现代间的代际得以弥合，这导出了崭新的未探索的智力边界。

##### 评分

得分：1.5 / 2

- `being eclipsed by the Renaissance` → “因为文艺复兴黯然失色” ✓
- `had been bridged` → “得以弥合” ✓
- `teachings` → “教育” 误译，应为 **“教义”**
- `the gap between the Medieval and modern periods` → “代际” 不准确，`gap` 在此指 **“鸿沟/差距”**，非“代际”
- `intellectual territories` → “智力边界” 可接受，**“知识领域”** 更贴切
