> [!example]
> **It** was his duty to attend to the matter.
>
> （it 充当形式主语，真正主语为不定式 to attend to the matter）
>
> **It**'s no good waiting this morning. Come back Monday.
>
> （it 充当形式主语，真正主语为动名词 waiting this morning）
>
> **It** seems that he is rich.
>
> （it 充当形式主语， that 引导的主语从句作真正主语）
