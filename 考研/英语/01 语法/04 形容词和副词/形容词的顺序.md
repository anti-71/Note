I like a coat. (woollen / red / beautiful / Chinese / new)

He graduated from a school. (prestigious / American / law / old)

There is a pen. (plastic / black / new / British / nice )

**相对性排序**：

- 形容词之间相互比较，越是 **相对不变**（客观、属性、种类、特质）的越 **靠近** 名词！
- 越是 **相对易变**（主观、态度、情感、可有可无）的越 **远离** 名词！

I like a coat. (**beautiful new red Chinese woollen**)

He graduated from a school. (**prestigious old American law**)

There is a pen. (**nice new black British plastic**)
