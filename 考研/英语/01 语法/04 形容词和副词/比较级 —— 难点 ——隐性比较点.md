Your friend is kind.

Your friend is wise.

**kind** 和 **wise** 无法比，所以只能比程度（much）

$\implies$ Your friend is **much** kind.

Your friend is **much** wise.

$\implies$ Your friend is **more** kind **than** (your friend is) (**much**) wise.

---

His father left him money.

He can spend money.

**money** 和 **money** 都是名词无法比！但修饰名词的修饰语（多少）可以比！

$\implies$ His father left him **much** money.

He can spend **much** money.

$\implies$ His father left him **more** money **than** he can spend **much** money.
