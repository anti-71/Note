##### 1）形容词

My computer is expensive.

Your computer is expensive.

**less … than**

**not so … as**

**not as … as**

**程度副词** ... **从属化连词**

My computer is **less** expensive **than** your computer is expensive.

My computer is **less** **expensive** than your computer is **expensive**.

My computer is less expensive than your computer **is**.

My computer is less expensive than your computer **is**.

My computer is less expensive than your computer. 或 My computer is less expensive than **is** your computer.

My computer is less expensive than **yours**.

##### 2）副词

Tom works **hard**.

Jack works **hard**.

$\implies$ Tom **does not** work **as** hard **as** Jack works hard.

Tom **does not** work as hard as Jack works **hard**.

Tom **does not** work as hard as Jack works.

Tom **does not** work as hard as Jack works. 或 Tom **does not** work as hard as Jack **does**.

Tom **does not** work as hard as Jack. 或 Tom **does not** work as hard as **does** Jack.
