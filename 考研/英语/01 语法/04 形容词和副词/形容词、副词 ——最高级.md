**注意两点：**

1. 范围
2. 指定

---

1. **形容词最高级前通常用 “the”**

> [!example] 
> The Sahara is the biggest desert in the world.

2. **形容词 most 前面没有 the，不表示 “最”，只表示 “非常”**

> [!example] 
> It is a most important problem. = It is a very important problem.

3. **副词最高级前多数不加 the，可用于修饰动词**

> [!example] 
> I shall give a prize to the pupil who reads best.
> 
> 我将给朗读最好的学生发一份奖品。

---

**注意：使用最高级要注意将主语包括在比较范围内**

（错）Tom is the tallest of his three brothers.

（对）Tom is the tallest of the three brothers.
