##### 1）形容词

My feet are big.

Your feet are big.

**步骤：**

1. 写出两个简单句

2. 找出两个简单句中的 **比较点**（第一：**adj/adv**；第二：**相同**）

3. 用关联词把两个简单句连接成一个 **主从复合句**
   - more … than
   - as … as
   - less … than
   - not so … as
   - not as … as

（...前的是 **程度副词**，后的是 **从属化连词**）

4. 省略比较状语从句中的 **相同的比较点**

5. 美化：**省略**、**倒装**、**换代词**

My feet are **as** big **as** your feet are big.

My feet are as **big** as your feet are **big**.

My feet are as **big** as your feet are.

My feet are as **big** as your feet. 或 My feet are as **big** as **are** your feet.

My feet are as big as **yours**.

##### 2）副词

Bob speaks English **fast**.

Lucy speaks English **fast**.

$\implies$ Bob speaks English **as** fast **as** Lucy speaks English fast.

Bob speaks English as **fast** as Lucy speaks English **fast**.

Bob speaks English as **fast** as Lucy speaks English.

Bob speaks English as **fast** as Lucy speaks English.

Bob speaks English as **fast** as Lucy speaks. 或 Bob speaks English as **fast** as Lucy **does**.

Bob speaks English as fast as **Lucy**. 或 Bob speaks English as fast as **does** Lucy.
