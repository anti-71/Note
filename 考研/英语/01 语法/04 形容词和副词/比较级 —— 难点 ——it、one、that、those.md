Mountains in Beijing are high.

Mountains in Tibet are high.

北京的山没有西藏的山高。

$\implies$ Mountains in Beijing are not **as** high **as** mountains in Tibet are high.

Mountains in Beijing are **not as** high **as** mountains in Tibet are high.

Mountains in Beijing are **not as** **high** **as** mountains in Tibet are **high**.

Mountains in Beijing are **not as** **high** **as** are mountains in Tibet. 或 Mountains in Beijing are **not as** **high** **as** mountains in Tibet.

$\implies$ Mountains in Beijing are not as high as **those** in Tibet.

##### it, one, that 的区别和用法（均代替上文提过的名词）

1. **it**：特指之前提到的同一个事物（特指同物，可数名词单数）

> [!example] 
> This pen is mine. You can use **it**.（同一只笔）

表复数时：用 **they/them**

> [!example] 
> These pens are mine. You can use **them**.

2. **one**：泛指之前提到的同类中的任一个人 / 物（泛指同类不同物，可数名词单数）

> [!example] 
> I’ve lost my umbrella. I need to buy **one**.（不是同一把伞）

表复数时：用 **ones**

> [!example] 
> I have a new pen and some old **ones**.

3. **that**：特指之前提到的同类中的特定事物（特指同类不同物，可数名词单数 / 不可数名词）

> [!example] 
> The umbrella you bought is cheaper than **that** I bought.（与前面不同的一把伞）
> 
> The weather in Lanzhou is cooler than **that** in Sanya.（不同的天气，天气不可数）

表复数时：用 **those**

> [!example] 
> These pictures are more beautiful than **those**.
