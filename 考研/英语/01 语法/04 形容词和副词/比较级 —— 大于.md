##### 1）形容词

His hair is long.

My hair is long.

如何把上面两句话通过比较级合并成一句？

**步骤**：

1. 写出两个简单句

2. 找出两个简单句中的 **比较点**

3. 用下面的关联词把两个简单句连接成一个 **主从复合句**

4. 省略比较状语从句中的相同的比较点

   - more … than 大于
   - as … as 等于
   - more … than 小于
   - not so … as 小于
   - not as … as 小于

   （...前的是 **程度副词**，后的是 **从属化连词**）

5. 美化：**省略**、**倒装**、**换代词**

His hair is **more** long **than** my hair is long.

His hair is longer **than** my hair is long.

> [!note] 形容词比较级怎么变
>
> 1. 单音节：-er；-est
> 2. 多音节：more；most
> 3. 双音节：**看尾巴**：有 - ous/-ive/-ic/-al/ly，用 more/most；没有这些尾巴的都可

His hair is **longer** than my hair is **long**.

His hair is **longer** than my hair is.

His hair is **longer** than my hair. 或 His hair is **longer** than is my hair.

His **hair** is longer than my **hair**. / His **hair** is longer than **mine**.

##### 2）副词

He swims **well**.

I swim **well**.

$\implies$ He swims **more well** than I swim **well**.

He swims **better** than I swim.

He swims **better** than I do.

He swims **better** than do I. 或 He swims **better** than I.
