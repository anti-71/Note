1. 时间副词

常见的有 now, then, soon, ago, recently, lately, later, before, early, today, tomorrow, yesterday, tonight, suddenly, immediately, still, already, just 等

> [!example] 
> They are noting that the Victorians **suddenly** seem to become more human.
> 
> 他们注意到，维多利亚时代的人似乎变得更有人情味了。

2. 地点副词

常见的有 here, there, up, down, away, nearby, home, ahead, abroad, indoors, overseas, upstairs, downstairs 等。

> [!example] 
> The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more **abroad**.
> 
> 公园跑一开始由十几个朋友发起，后来在英国引发了 400 场活动，在国外就更多了。

3. 方式副词

表示行为方式的副词大多以 -ly 结尾，常见的有 well, fast, hard, quietly, heavily, warmly, carefully, happily, politely, angrily 等

> [!example] 
> These habits have helped companies earn billions of dollars when customers eat snacks or wipe counters almost without thinking, often in response to a **carefully** designed set of daily cues.
> 
> 消费者通常受到一系列精心设计的日常暗示的影响，他们几乎不假思索地吃着零食或者擦拭柜台，这些行为习惯已经帮助各公司赚取了数十亿美元的收益。

4. 频度副词

常见的有 always, usually, often, frequently, constantly, occasionally, sometimes, seldom, hardly, rarely, never 等

> [!example] 
> Those who jumped without a job haven’t **always** landed in top positions quickly.
> 
> 那些没有找好工作就跳槽的人并不总是很快就能找到最高层的职位。

5. 程度副词

常见的有 fairly, pretty, rather, quite, very, much, too, greatly, almost, nearly, half, highly, deeply, partly, perfectly, really 等

> [!example] 
> For the rest of the decade she apparently managed both roles without attracting **much** criticism.
> 
> 在此后九年时间里，她貌似成功地应付了两个角色，又没有招致太多非议。

6. 连接副词

这类副词有相当于并列连词的 therefore, besides, otherwise, however, moreover, furthermore, still, thus, meanwhile 等，也有引导名词性从句或不定式的 when, why, where, how 等

> [!example] 
> As the industry advances, **however**, other suits may have an even greater impact.
> 
> 但是，随着该行业的发展，其他案例可能会有更大的影响。

7. 关系副词

引导定语从句的 when, where, why 等

> [!example] 
> “We are just in a situation **where** there are no good theories explaining what consciousness actually is and how you could ever build a machine to get there.”
> 
> “我们正处于这样一种境地，即没有合理的理论可以解释意识到底是什么，以及如何创造一台拥有人类意识的机器。”

8. 疑问副词

用于引出特殊疑问句的 when, where, why, how 等

> [!example] 
> **When** will he be back?
> 
> 他什么时候回来？

9. 评论性副词

就是修饰整个句子，表达说话人的观点态度的副词

常见的有 frankly, honestly, obviously, evidently, generally, briefly, fortunately, unfortunately, luckily, naturally, hopefully 等

> [!example] 
> **Unfortunately**, the polyurethane foam he used is inherently unstable.
> 
> 不幸的是，他使用的聚氨酯泡沫本身就不稳定。
