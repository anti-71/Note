1. 情况：状语从句的 **主语** = 主句的 **主语**
2. 步骤：
    a. 自我确定 **是否省略** 状语从句的连接词（留下比较清楚，不留比较简洁）
    b. 将状语从句的 **主语** 省略
    c. **变状语从句的谓语动词**：
    - be + 其他 $\implies$ ~~be~~ + 其他
    - 情态动词 + do $\implies$ to do
    - 非 be 也非情态动词 + do $\implies$ doing

> [!example] 
> - When I returned home, I found that I had left my key in the office.
> 
> $\implies$ When returning home, I found that I had left my key in the office.
> 
> $\implies$ Returning home, I found that I had left my key in the office.
> 
> - Although he is a famous professor, he can not answer the question.
> 
> $\implies$ Although (being) a famous professor, he can not answer the question.
> 
> $\implies$ Although a famous professor, he can not answer the question.
> 
> - Although I was afraid of snakes, I stepped forward to pick up one.
> 
> $\implies$ Although afraid of snakes, I stepped forward to pick up one.
> 
> $\implies$ Afraid of snakes, I stepped forward to pick up one.
