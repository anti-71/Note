1. 条件：关系代词在定语从句中作主语
2. 步骤：
	1. 关系代词省略
	2. 变定语从句的谓语动词：
	    - be + 其他 $\implies$ ~~be~~ + 其他
	    - 情态动词 + do $\implies$ to do
	    - 非 be 也非情态动词 + do $\implies$ doing

> [!example] 
> - China is a developing country which belongs to the third world.
> 
> $\implies$ China is a developing country belonging to the third world.
> 
> - You are the only one that can understand me.
> 
> $\implies$ You are the only one to understand me.
> 
> - Books which are written in English are more expensive.
> 
> $\implies$ Books written in English are more expensive.

The Great Wall of China, which is the largest man-made structure on earth, no longer serves a military function.

$\implies$ The Great Wall of China, **the largest man-made structure on earth** , no longer serves a military function.

**同位语其实就是定语从句的名词补语**

> [!example] 
> Such standardized tests may not assess all the important elements **necessary** to succeed in school and in life, argues Robert J. Sternberg.
> 
> $\implies$ Such standardized tests may not assess all the important elements **which** are necessary to succeed in school and in life, argues Robert J. Sternberg.
