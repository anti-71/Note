##### 1）引用宾从

> [!example] 
> **Doug Fogg** , chief operating officer of Identigene, which makes the over-the-counter kits, **says** that more than 60,000 people have purchased the PTKs since they first became available without prescriptions last year.
> 
> More than 60,000 people have purchased the PTKs since they first became available without prescriptions last year, **says Doug Fogg** , chief operating officer of Identigene, which makes the over-the-counter kits.

##### 2）表时间或地点的词 / 短语

1. **介词短语**：in the small box

2. **地点 / 时间副词**：there /here /now /then

3. **介副词**：in /out /up /down /off /away /over /back

> [!example] 
> **In the small box** was the ring that he had given her twenty years before.
> 
> 那个小盒里面是那枚他二十年前送给她的戒指。
> 
> **Between the two buildings** stands a tall pine.
> 
> 两座楼之间有棵大松树。
> 
> **Here** comes the bus! 公交车来了！
> 
> **Here** it comes! 它来了！
> 
> **Now** comes your turn. 现在轮到你了。
> 
> **Out** went the children. 孩子们出去了。

##### 3）否定副词位于句首

**few** (很少几个)；**little** (很少)；**never** (从不)；**hardly** (几乎不)；

**seldom** (很少)；**in no way** (决不)；**in no case** (决不)；**at no time** (任何时候都不)；

**nowhere** (无处)；**not...until...** (直到…… 才)；**no sooner...than** (一…… 就)；**rarely** (很少)；**hardly...when** (一…… 就)

> [!example] 
> I will **never** trouble you again.
> 
> **Never will** I trouble you again.
> 
> 我再也不会麻烦你了
> 
> I did **not** know that he has got married until this morning.
> 
> **Not** until this morning **did** I know that he has got married.
> 
> 直到今天早晨我才知道他已经结过婚了。

##### 4）特殊形式

1. **“Only + 状语” 置于句首**

Only 强调范围

> [!example] 
> **Only in this way** can you solve this problem.
> 
> 只有用这种方法，你才可以解决这个问题。
> 
> **Only last week did** Jack go abroad.
> 
> 杰克只是上周出国了。
> 
> **Only** Jack went abroad last week.
> 
> 上周只有杰克出国了。

2. **There be 结构**

> [!example] 
> A book is there on the desk.
> 
> **There is** a book on the desk.
> 
> 桌子上有一本书。
