> [!example] 
> His favorite pastime is that he goes fishing on weekends.
> 
> $\implies$ His favorite pastime is going fishing on weekends.
> 
> Her wish is that she could lose weight in a short time.
> 
> $\implies$ Her wish is to lose weight in a short time.
