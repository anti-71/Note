> [!example] 
> She can’t decide whether she should go with him or stay home.
> 
> $\implies$ She can’t decide whether to go with him or stay home.
> 
> I don’t know what I should do.
> 
> $\implies$ I don’t know what to do.
> 
> Please tell me how I can get to the bus station.
> 
> $\implies$ Please tell me how to get to the bus station.
