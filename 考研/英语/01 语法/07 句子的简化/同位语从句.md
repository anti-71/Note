**“名词 + that 同位语从句”** 变成了一个动名词结构 **“名词 + of + 动名词的复合结构”**

> [!example] 
> There was no chance that Davy would come from the battle alive.
>  
> $\implies$ There was no chance of **Davy** coming from the battle alive.
> 
> 此处没有用所有格

 > [!question] 动名词复合结构中采用普通格的情况？
> 
> 1.  逻辑主语是无生命的事物时
>  
>  例如 “Is there any hope of our team winning the game?”（我们队有赢得比赛的希望吗？）
>  
>  这里 “our team” 是无生命的事物组合（从语法角度，团队在这里作为一个事物概念），可以用普通格 “our team” 加动名词 “winning” 的形式
>  
> 2. 逻辑主语是不定代词时
>  
>  “Everyone talking at the same time made the meeting very noisy.”（每个人都同时讲话让会议很嘈杂。）
>  
>  “everyone” 是不定代词，作为动名词 “talking” 的逻辑主语，采用普通格形式
>  
> 3. 逻辑主语为较长的名词短语时
>  
> “Several students from our class participating in the competition won prizes.”（我们班的几个学生参加比赛获奖了。）
>  
> “Several students from our class” 是比较长的名词短语，作为动名词 “participating” 的逻辑主语，用普通格
 
> [!example] 
> We were greatly encouraged by the news that China had launched another man-made satellite.
>  
> $\implies$ We were greatly encouraged by the news of China having launched another man-made satellite.
