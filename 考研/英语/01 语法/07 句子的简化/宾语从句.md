> [!example] 
> I hope that I can drive to work in my own car.
> 
> $\implies$ I hope to drive to work in my own car.
> 
> I would like that you can go to the concert with me.
> 
> $\implies$ I would like you to go to the concert with me.
> 
> I dislike that I am kept waiting.
> 
> $\implies$ I dislike being kept waiting.
