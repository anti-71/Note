##### 1）S. V. O. 改为被动语态

> [!example] 
> We **carried out** his instructions to the letter.（to the letter：完全地 / 准确地）
> 
> His instructions **were carried out** (by us) to the letter.
> 
> 我们彻底执行了他的指示。
> 
> Scientists say the compound **is likely to cause** cancer.
> 
> Scientists say cancer **is likely to be caused** by the compound.
> 
> 科学家表示，化合物有可能致癌。

##### 2）S. V. O. O. 改为被动语态

> [!example] 
> 
> They **gave** him a medal for his wonderful work.
> 
> He **was given** a medal for his wonderful work.
> 
> A medal **was given** (to) him for his wonderful work.
> 
> 他们授给他一枚奖章。
> 
> It **gives** us patience for ourselves and toward others as well as patience while driving, catching a fish, working on our car, or raising a child.
> 
> We **are given** patience for ourselves and toward others as well as patience while driving, catching a fish, working on our car, or raising a child (by it).
> 
> Patience for ourselves and toward others as well as patience while driving, catching a fish, working on our car, or raising a child **is given** (to) us (by it).
> 
> 它让我们对自己和他人更有耐心，也让我们在开车、钓鱼、修车或育儿时都更具耐性。

##### 3）S. V. O. C. 改为被动语态

> [!example] 
> 
> You don't **have to make** Peter work hard.
> 
> Peter doesn't **have to be made** to work hard.
> 
> 你没有必要逼彼得好好学习。
> 
> You **can call** their views stupid.
> 
> Their views **can be called** stupid.
> 
> 你可以说他们的观点很愚蠢。


##### 4）完成时的被动语态

> [!example] 
> 
> I **have asked** him to help you.
> 
> He **has been asked** to help you.
> 
> 我已经请他来帮助你了。
> 
> By offering on-trend items at dirt-cheap prices, Cline argues, these brands **have hijacked** fashion cycles.
> 
> By offering on-trend items at dirt-cheap prices, Cline argues, fashion cycles **have been hijacked** by these brands.
> 
> 克莱茵认为，这些品牌通过提供极其便宜的时尚单品，强行控制时尚周期。


##### 5）进行时的被动语态

> [!example] 
> 
> They **were carrying** the hurt player.
> 
> The hurt player **was being carried** by them.
> 
> 他们那会正在抬着受伤的运动员。
> 
> Imagine you **are pouring** your heart out to someone.
> 
> Imagine your heart **is being poured** out to someone by yourself.
> 
> 想象一下，你正在向某人倾吐心声。


##### 6）将来时的被动语态

> [!example] 
> They **will give** me a chance to play in this game again.
> 
> I **will be given** a chance to play in this game again.
> 
> 他们会给我一个再次参加这场比赛的机会。
> 
> It **will hold** another meeting soon.
> 
> Another meeting **will be held** soon.
> 
> 马上将召开另一场会议。
