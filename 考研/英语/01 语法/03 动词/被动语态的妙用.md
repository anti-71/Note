##### 1）连贯性

> [!example] 
> **Jack** was happy yesterday because **his teacher** praised him.
> 
> **Jack** was happy because **he** was praised by his teacher.
> 
> 杰克很高兴，因为他受到了老师的表扬。
> 
> **He** respects others and **others** respect him.
> 
> **He** respects others and is respected (by others).
> 
> 他尊重其他人，其他人也尊重他。
> 
> Because **oxytocin** promotes attachment in relationships, including that between mothers and their newborn babies, **some experts** often called it "the bonding hormone".
> 
> Because oxytocin promotes attachment in relationships, including that between mothers and their newborn babies, **it** was often called "the bonding hormone".
> 
> 催产素可以促进关系的依恋，包括母亲与新生儿之间的依恋，因此它通常被称为 “亲密荷尔蒙”。

##### 2）准确性

> [!example] 
> **They (People? Experts? Doctors?) divide** obesity, in turn, into moderately obese, severely obese, and very severely obese.
> 
> Obesity, in turn, **can be divided** into moderately obese, severely obese, and very severely obese.
> 
> 相应地，肥胖又可分为中度肥胖、重度肥胖和极度肥胖。
> 
> **They (Governments? Managers?) should have closed down** zoos as they prioritize moneymaking over animals’ well-being.
> 
> Zoos **should have been closed down** as they prioritize moneymaking over animals’ well-being.
> 
> 动物园应该被关闭，因为它们优先考虑赚钱而不是动物的福祉。

##### 3）简洁性

> [!example] 
> **Something** can destroy a man, but **nothing** can defeat him.
> 
> **A man** can be destroyed but not defeated.
> 
> 一个人可以被毁灭，却不能被打败。
> 
> **Lucy’s good behavior** deeply impressed me, so **I** gave her a big gift.
> 
> **(I was) Deeply impressed** by Lucy’s good behavior, so **I** gave her a big gift.
> 
> **Deeply impressed** by Lucy’s good behavior, **I** gave her a big gift.
> 
> 露西的良好表现给我留下了深刻的印象，所以我送给她一份大礼。
