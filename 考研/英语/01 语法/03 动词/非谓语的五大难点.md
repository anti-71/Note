##### 1. **分开理解** 还是 **整体理解**？

> [!example] 
> It **is interesting**.
> 
> I **am reading** a book.
> 
> He **is dressed** in blue.
> 
> A boy **was dressed** by his father this morning.
> 
> I can’t find my wallet. It **is gone**.
> 
> The leaves **are fallen**.

不能单纯的看成动词的语态变化，当成 **系 + 表** 结构更好

##### 2. 分词 (doing or done) 做形容词时如何区别？

> [!example] 
> Your job **is promising**.
> 
> He **looks excited** when hearing the good news.

doing or done 做形容词时：

- doing 主动 “令人……”
- done 被动 “感觉……”

##### 3. doing 现在分词还是动名词？

> [!example] 
> running shoes（1. shoes for running **n** 跑步的鞋；2. shoes are running **adj**）
> 
> a swimming pool 游泳池
> 
> developing countries 发展中国家
> 
> the flying bird ?
> 
> the running brook ?

尝试改写一下短语：

- 如果 + for，作现在分词
- 如果 + be 动词，作动名词

##### 4. 使役动词和感官动词后的动词不定式用动词原形？

① **使役动词** let/make/have/help “要”“使”“让”：强迫 / 操控 + 确定性语气（可省 to）

> [!example] 
> He **lets** them **stay** in the classroom.
> 
> The teacher **made** the naughty boy **stand** there for an hour.
> 
> I will **have** him **come and help** you.
> 
> The naughty boy **was made to stand** there for an hour.

变 **被动** 时，语气变化成 “**被迫**”，“**不得不**” 做，这时相当于 **must/have to = to do**

② **感官动词** see/watch/hear…… “看到”“听到”（确定发生了）：

**看到 / 听到 + 确定语气**

> [!example] 
> I saw him work in the garden yesterday. 昨天我看见他在花园里干活了。

##### 5. 什么时候跟 doing 或 to do sth？

> [!example] 
> enjoy **doing** sth. 喜爱做某事
> 
> ask sb **to do** sth. 要求做某事
> 
> remember **to do** sth. 记住要做某事
> 
> remember **doing** sth. 记住曾做过某事
> 
> Remember **to post** the letter for me.
> 
> I remember **seeing** him last night.

- doing 表示已经做过，事实性
- to do 表示还没做，表推测
