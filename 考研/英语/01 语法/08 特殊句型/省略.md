在英语中为了追求语言的 **简洁凝练**，在 **不产生歧义** 的情况下，常省略句中的一个或几个成分

##### 主语相同的省略

> [!example] 
> **He opened** the Bible, (he) **told** his father that the first line he saw would settle his fate, and (he) **read** the magical words.
> 
> 他打开了《圣经》，告诉他的父亲他读到的第一行文字将决定他的命运，并且读了这些有魔力的文字。
> 
> 因主语相同，所以省略了主语

##### 谓语相同的省略

> [!example] 
> She **is** a nurse and her husband (is) a doctor.
> 
> 她是护士，她丈夫是医生。
> 
> Some **die** of hunger, others (die) of eating too much.
> 
> 有些人死于饥饿，有些人死于吃得太多。

##### 主语和（部分）谓语相同的省略

> [!example] 
> **He will** come, and (he will) play at cards with me.
> 
> 他会来的，还会和我一起玩牌。
> 
> It is said that in England death is pressing, in Canada (**death is**) inevitable and in California (**death is**) optional.
> 
> 据说，在英国，死亡是紧迫的，在加拿大，死亡是不可避免的，而在加利福尼亚，死亡是可选择的。

##### 其他成分相同的省略

> [!example] 
> I **have got up** , and my roommate (has) **prepared** our breakfast.
> 
> 我已经起床了，我的室友已经准备好了我们的早餐
> 
> **She asked** the kids **to stay** , and (she asked) the adults (to stay) too.
> 
> 她请孩子们留下来，也请大人留下来。
> 
> writers who **are not read** to writers who **are** (read)
> 
> 不受读者欢迎的作家对受读者欢迎的作家
> 
> She **started** (the car) but **stopped the car** at once.
> 
> 她起动了这辆车，但又立即把它停住了。
> 
> Linda wore **the green dress** , but the red (dress/one) suits her better.
> 
> 琳达穿了件绿色的裙子，但那件红色的更适合她。
> 
> You can write your name **on** (the line), **above** (the line) or **below the line**.
> 
> 你可以把你的名字写在线上、线的上方或者线的下方。

##### 从句的省略

> [!example] 
> **When I returned** home, I found that I had left my key in the office.
> 
> **When returning** home, I found that I had left my key in the office.
> 
> **Returning** home, I found that I had left my key in the office.
> 
> 我回到家，我发现我把钥匙忘在办公室了
> 
> Although **I was** afraid of snakes, I stepped forward to pick up one.
> 
> Although afraid of snakes, I stepped forward to pick up one.
> 
> 虽然我怕蛇，但我还是上前捡了一条。
