##### 1）主语从句

- △ + V.（+ 其他）
    
    > [!example] 
> Where you went yesterday has nothing to do with us.
- △ + 系 V. + P.
    
    > [!example] 
> Where the girl lives is still unknown.

在含有主语从句的复合句中，有时为保持句子平衡，避免头重脚轻，常用 it 作形式主语，将真正的主语（较长的主语从句）后置

- It is n. /adj./done. + △.
    
    > [!example] 
> It is still unknown where the girl lives.

##### 2）表语从句

- S. + 系 V. + △.
    
    > [!example] 
> The question is where the girl lives.
>     
>    The boss scolded him. That was because he came late.

> [!warning] 注意
> 并非所有的系动词都能接从句
> 
> 能接表从的系 V. 主要是：be; seem; look; appear; sound; remain 等等

##### 3）宾语从句

- S. + Vt. + △.
    
    > [!example] 
> I don’t know where the girl lives.
- S. + V. + 介词 + △.
    
    > [!example] 
> This is different from what you said.
- S. + Vt. + O. + △.
    
    > [!example] 
> I tell you where the girl lives.（双宾语）
- S. + be + adj. + △.
    
    > [!example] 
> We are glad that you have come to join us.

believe，consider，feel，find，guess，hear，imagine，know，make，prove，think，understand 等动词后接 “宾语 + 宾语补足语” 时，用 **it** 作形式宾语，将 that 引导的真正的宾语从句后置

- S. +Vt. + **it** + OC. + △.
    
    > [!example] 
> I find it easy where the girl lives.

> [!warning] 注意
> 由于非谓语动词仍然具有 **动词** 的特性，可以有自己的宾语（逻辑宾语），因此其后 **也可以跟宾语从句**，构成非谓语动词短语

> [!example] 
> Not knowing where she lives, I have to call her to come over.
> 
> 由于不知道她住在哪，我不得不打电话让她过来。

##### 4）同位语从句

- S. + Vt. + O.(n.) + △.
    
    > [!example] 
> He has to admit the fact that he is no longer young.
- S.(n.) + △ + V.（+ 其他）.
    
    > [!example] 
> The fact that he succeeded in the experiment pleased everybody.
- S.(n.) + △ + 系 V. + P.
    
    > [!example] 
> The news that we are having a holiday tomorrow is not true.
