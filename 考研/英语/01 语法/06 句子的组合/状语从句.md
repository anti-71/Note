- **状语从句前置**：△ ，S. + V. + O.
    
    > [!example] 
> While his wife was cooking, he was watching TV.
    
- **状语从句后置**：S. + V. + O. △.
    
    > [!example] 
> She was absent from school the day before yesterday because she had a fever.
    
- **状语从句插入**：S. + △ + V. + O.
    
    > [!example] 
> Sleeping, as time goes by, becomes less of a problem.
    

> [!warning] 注意
> 完整的句子之后不 **定** 就 **状** $\implies$ S. + V. + O. △（定从）/ △（状从）
> 
> 与 V. 或主句有关
