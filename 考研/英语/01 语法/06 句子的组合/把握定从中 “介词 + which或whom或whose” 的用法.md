**选用介词**： “看前 (n)，看后 (v)，看句意 (从句和所修饰的 n)”

###

##### 看前 n.

> [!example] 
> I remember the day **on which** I first came to this school.
> 
> I remember the year **in which** I first visited Beijing.

##### 看后 v.

> [!example] 
> This is the book **on which** I spent 10 yuan.
> 
> This is the book **for which** I paid 10 yuan.

##### 看句意

> [!example] 
> You must pay your bill
> 
> - **on** the day. 在这一天还账
> - **before** the day. 在这一天之前还账
> - **by** the day. 截止到这一天还账
> 
> Sep.30 is the day **by/on/before which** you must pay your bill.
> 
> 根据句子语境选择实际介词
