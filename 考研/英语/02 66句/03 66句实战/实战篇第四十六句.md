> [!question] 
> This success, coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not genetically determined, led Ericsson to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one.

##### 1）S, $\underline{\quad\quad}$ ,V 或 S— $\underline{\quad\quad}$ —V 中间部分通常为插入语

句中 coupled with … determined 位于主语 This success 和谓语 led 中间，是 **插入语**

##### 2）区分 did/done 同体

谓语动词（did），表示主动含义

过去分词（done），表示被动完成态，可译为 “被”“已经” 和 “已经被”

“This success” 是动作 “led” 的执行者，两者构成主动关系，led 为 **谓语动词**

##### 3）S + V + O to do sth. 区分 to do sth. 作什么成分

见 [[实战篇第六句#2）S + V + O to do sth. 区分 to do sth. 作什么成分|66-6]]

本句动词不定式短语 to conclude that … one 的动作由宾语 Ericsson 发出，在句中作 **宾语补足语**

且 that 引导的从句 that the act … one 作 to conclude 的逻辑宾语，所以此处 that 引导 **宾语从句**

##### 4）代词 one 的还原

one 泛指上文提到的 **同类不同物**，指代可数名词单数

本句中 intuitive exercise 和 cognitive exercise 二者属于同类不同物，为避免重复，用 one 指代前面提到的 exercise

> [!note] it, one, that 的区别和用法（代替上文提过的名词时）
> 1. it：
> 
> 特指之前提到的同一个事物（特指同物，可数名词单数）
> 
> > [!example] 
> > This pen is mine. You can use it.（同一支笔）
> 
> 表复数时：用 they/them
> 
> > [!example] 
> > These pens are mine. You can use them.
> 
> 2. one：
> 
> 泛指之前提到的同类中的任一个人 / 物（泛指同类不同物，可数名词单数）
> 
> > [!example] 
> > I’ve lost my umbrella. I need to buy one.（不是同一把伞）
> 
> 表复数时：用 ones
> 
> > [!example] 
> > I have a new pen and some old ones.
> 
> 3. that：
> 
> 特指之前提到的同类中的特定事物（特指同类不同物，可数名词单数 / 不可数名词）
> 
> > [!example] 
> > - The umbrella you bought is cheaper than that I bought.（与前面不同的一把伞）
> > 
> >  -The weather in Lanzhou is cooler than that in Sanya.（不同的天气，天气不可数）
> 
> 表复数时：用 those
> 
> > [!example] 
> > These pictures are more beautiful than those.

##### 5）many of 与 much of 辨析

见 [[实战篇第三十二句#2）many of 与 much of 辨析|66-32]]

…the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one 中

more 是 much 或者 many 的比较级

但结构为 “more of a/an + 可数名词单数”，故 more 只能是 much 的比较级（“much of a/an + 单数名词”）

此处意为 “在很大程度上”

##### 6）常见比较级结构

见 [[实战篇第二十三句#2）常见比较级结构|66-23]]

本句 the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one 可还原为以下两个简单句：

句 1：The act of memorizing is much of a cognitive exercise.

句 2：The act of memorizing is much of an intuitive exercise.

演变过程：

1. 连接

句 1 作主干，句 2 作比较状语从句

$\implies$ The act of memorizing is **more**（**much 的比较级**）of a cognitive exercise **than** the act of memorizing is **much of** an intuitive exercise.

2. 找比较点，将从句中比较点及重复内容删除

注意：删除重复内容时不应产生歧义

$\implies$ The act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than ~~the act of memorizing is much of~~ an intuitive exercise.

3. 美化（省略、倒装、换代词、换代动词）

$\implies$ The act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive **one**.

##### 7）more of A than B

表示 “与其说是 B，不如说是 A”

该结构主要用于比较两个事物，强调某事物在某种性质或特征上更偏向于前者而非后者

the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one 中

the act of memorizing 更偏向于 a cognitive exercise，而非 an intuitive one

可译为：

记忆行为 **与其说是** 一种直觉练习，**倒不如说是** 一种认知练习

##### 8）插入语的理解

思路 1：**非限制性定语从句的简化**

见 [[实战篇第六句#8）定语从句的简化|66-6]]

插入语 coupled with later research showing … determined 是 **过去分词短语**

可理解成非限制性定语从句（**which was**）coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not genetically determined 的 **简化**，补充说明 This success

其中 **现在分词短语** showing that memory … determined 是 research 的后置定语

由定语从句 **which/that** shows that memory itself is not genetically determined **简化** 而来

思路 2：**句子组合**

见 [[实战篇第十八句#2）句子组合的方式|66-18]]

1. This success led Ericsson to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one.
2. This success was coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not genetically determined.

1 + 2：

$\implies$ This success, **coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not genetically determined** , led Ericsson to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one.

##### 参考译文

这一成功，加上后来的研究（该研究表明记忆本身不是由基因决定的），使得埃里克森（Ericsson）得出结论：记忆行为与其说是一种直觉行为，倒不如说是一种认知行为。
