> [!question] 
> Left, until now, to odd, low-level IT staff to put right, and seen as a concern only of data-rich industries such as banking, telecoms and air travel, information protection is now high on the boss’s agenda in businesses of every variety.

##### 1）until 用法集锦

until *prep.* & *conj.* 到…… 时；直到…… 为止

1. *prep.*

表示动作或状态持续到某个时间点或事件发生为止，后接 **名词** 或 **时间点**

> [!example] 
> - He worked **until** midnight. 他一直工作到午夜。
> 
> - **Until** now I have always lived alone. 直到现在，我一直独自生活。

2. *conj.*

引导 **时间状语从句**，表示主句动作持续到从句动作发生时为止

> [!example] 
> She stayed until the meeting ended.
> 
> 她一直待到会议结束。

3. 肯定句与否定句的用法差异

- 肯定句：

主句谓语动词需为 **延续性动词**（如 live, study），表示动作持续到 until 所指的时间

> [!example] 
> I will study until you call me.
> 
> 我会学习到你打电话给我为止。

- 否定句（not...until...）：

主句谓语动词为 **非延续性动词**（如 leave, die），表示 “直到…… 才……”

> [!example] 
> She didn’t leave until the rain stopped.
> 
> 直到雨停她才离开。

##### 2）句子组合的方式

见 [[实战篇第十八句#2）句子组合的方式|66-18]]

本句整体结构为：过去分词短语 1，and 并列过去分词短语 2，主干

- 过去分词短语 1：Left... right
- 过去分词短语 2：seen... air travel
- 主干：information protection is now high ...variety

过去分词短语 1 和过去分词短语 2 的逻辑主语与主干主语（information protection）一致

1. 过去分词短语 1（Left... right）可还原为：

$\implies$ **Information protection was** left, until now, to odd, low-level IT staff to put right.

leave sth. to sb.（主动）$\implies$ sth. be left to sb.（被动）

2. 过去分词短语（seen... air travel）可还原为：

$\implies$ **Information protection was** seen as a concern only of data-rich industries such as banking, telecoms and air travel.

see A as B（主动）$\implies$ A be seen as B（被动）

本句的简化过程：

1. Information protection（主语）was left（谓语），until now, to odd, low-level IT staff to put right.

be + 其他 $\implies$ 其他，即 was left $\implies$ left

2. Information protection（主语）was seen（谓语）as a concern only of data-rich industries such as banking, telecoms and air travel.

be + 其他 $\implies$ 其他，即 was seen $\implies$ seen

3. Information protection is now high on the boss’s agenda in businesses of every variety.

1 和 2 均简化成 **过去分词短语**，3 作 **主干**

$\implies$ Left, until now, to odd, low-level IT staff to put right, and seen as a concern only of data-rich industries such as banking, telecoms and air travel, information protection is now high on the boss’s agenda in businesses of every variety.

##### 3）插入语

until now 表示 “直到现在”，在句中作 **插入语**，是 and 并列的两个过去分词短语共用的 **时间状语**

##### 4）“把 A 看作 B” 的常见表达

见 [[实战篇第三句#7）“把 A 看作 B” 的常见表达|66-3]]

本句为 see A as B 的被动形式

##### 5）列举性同位语

见 [[实战篇第十句#2）列举性同位语|66-10]]

...only of data-rich industries such as banking, telecoms and air travel...

such as banking, telecoms and air travel 既可看作 **列举性同位语** 补充说明 data-rich industries，也可看作 **后置定语** 修饰 data-rich industries

##### 6）翻译的语境化处理

air travel 本意为 “航空旅行”，但句中 and 并列连接 banking, telecoms and air travel，均是对 data-rich industries（数据密集型行业）的举例

所以该语境下，air travel 可理解为 “**航空业**”
