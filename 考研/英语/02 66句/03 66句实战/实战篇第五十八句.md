> [!question] 
> Several massive leakages of customer and employee data this year —— from organizations as diverse as Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley —— have left managers hurriedly peering into their intricate IT systems and business processes in search of potential vulnerabilities.

##### 1）名词词组作状语的常见情况

见 [[实战篇第十二句#4）名词短语作状语的常见情况|66-12]]

本句中 **名词词组** this year 作 **时间状语**

##### 2）定语从句的简化

见 [[实战篇第六句#8）定语从句的简化|66-6]]

在 as diverse as Time Warner,..., Berkeley 中

第一个 as 为 **副词**，diverse 为 **形容词**，第二个 as 引导比较结构

紧跟名词 organizations 之后，该部分整体可理解为形容词短语作后置定语修饰 organizations

可还原为 **定语从句**：

...**which are** as diverse as Time Warner,..., Berkeley.

##### 3）常见比较级结构 —— 同级比较

见 [[实战篇第二十三句#2）常见比较级结构|66-23]]

演变过程 —— 同级比较：as...as...

表示 “等于”：as（副词，修饰形容词或副词）...as（从属连词，引导比较状语从句）...

- 句 1: Organizations **are diverse**.
- 句 2: Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley **are diverse**.

1. 连接句子，句 1 作主干，句 2 作比较状语从句

Organizations are **as** diverse **as** Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley are diverse.

2. 找比较点，将从句中比较点及重复内容删除（谓语动词先保留）

注意：省略重复内容时不应产生歧义

Organizations are as diverse as Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley are ~~diverse~~.

3. 美化（省略、倒装、换代词、换代动词）

- 省略：

Organizations are as diverse as Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley (**are**) .

- 倒装：

Organizations are as diverse as **are** Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley .

4. 定语从句的简化

$\implies$ Organizations (which are as diverse as Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley)

$\implies$ Organizations as diverse as Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley.

##### 4）后置定语的翻译 —— 五九原则

见 [[实战篇第二句#7）后置定语 （介词短语 / 定语从句 / 不定式短语 / 分词短语 / 形容词短语）的翻译|66-2]]

as diverse as..., Berkeley 这一 **后置定语** 修饰 organizations

含有 as...as... **同级比较** 结构，表示：和…… 一样

可直译为：

和时代华纳公司、美国国防承包商科学应用国际公司，甚至加州大学伯克利分校一样多样的机构。

该部分用于列举，强调牵涉机构多，且该后置定语信息量较大，可单独译，调整为：

牵涉多类机构，像时代华纳公司、美国国防承包商科学应用国际公司，甚至还涉及加州大学伯克利分校。

##### 参考译文

今年发生的几起客户和雇员资料的重大泄露事件 —— 牵涉多类机构，像时代华纳公司、美国国防承包商科学应用国际公司，甚至还涉及加州大学伯克利分校 —— 使管理者匆忙地细查复杂的 IT（信息技术）系统和业务流程，以便找出潜在的漏洞。
