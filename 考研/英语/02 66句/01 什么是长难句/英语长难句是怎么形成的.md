to do sth is difficult.

- It is difficult to do sth.
- It is difficult **for sb** to do sth.
- It is difficult for the average reader **to imagine a time**.

> [!warning] 
> 定冠词 **the** 用于单数可数名词前，表示一类人或事物
> 
> > [!example] 
> > The cat is kind to people.
> 
> 句中 the average reader 指的是广大普通读者

- It is difficult for the average reader **under the age of forty** to imagine a time.
- It is difficult for the average reader under the age of forty to imagine a time **when high-quality arts criticism could be found in most big-city newspapers**.
- It is difficult **to the point of impossibility** for the average reader under the age of forty to imagine a time when high-quality arts criticism could be found in most big-city newspapers.
