- **can** / may / must / shall / should / would / will / might **do** sth
- be able to do sth
- be willing to do sth
- be likely to do sth
- have to do sth
- ought to do sth

> [!example] 
> The bodies playing major professional sports have dramatically changed over the years, and managers **have been** more than **willing to adjust** team uniforms to fit the growing numbers of bigger, longer frames.
> 
> 这些年来，职业运动员的体格发生了显著变化。教练们非常乐意调整队服以适应越来越多身材更加魁梧的运动员。
