### （二）连接句子的连词

> [!warning] 
> 只数连接句子的连词

> [!question] 怎么理解连接句子的从属化连词和并列连词？
> 1. I came into the woods.
> 2. I saw many birds.
> 
> I came into the woods, I saw many birds.（❌）
> 
> $\implies$ I came into the woods **and** I saw many birds.（主句 + 主句）
> 
> $\implies$ **When** I came into the woods, I saw many birds.（从句 + 主句）
