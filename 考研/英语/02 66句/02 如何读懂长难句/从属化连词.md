##### 从属化连词一览

| 名从连词                                                                                                                     | 定从连词                                                                                                         | 状从连词                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| that<br><br>whether; if（宾从）;<br><br>as if（表从）;<br><br>what; who; whom -ever;<br><br>where; when; why; how<br><br>特殊疑问词都可 | **无 what、无 how**<br><br>that; which;<br><br>who; whom;<br><br>whose（所有格）;<br><br>where; when;<br><br>why; as | **九大状语从句**，连词较多，都要认识<br><br>如（原因状从）：<br><br>because; since; as; in that;<br><br>now that; considering that;<br><br>seeing that… |

**从句常见连词**：wh-; that; whether; because; if...

> [!example] 
> More recently, **while** examining housing construction, the researchers discovered that illiterate, non-English-speaking Mexican workers in Houston, Texas, consistently met best-practice labor productivity standards despite the complexity of the building industry's work.
