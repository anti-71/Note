1. I think **(that)** he is fit for his office.
2. Tom is reading the book **(that/which)** he bought last week.
3. It is obvious **(that)** he is wrong.
4. **That** he likes books of this kind is very interesting.

以上句子有些省略了连词，而有些不可省（如第四句）

为了语言的简洁性，在省略了不产生歧义的情况下，通常省略句子中的一些成分，此时要具体分析

> [!example] 
> The researchers wanted to know if the optimism and inclination for risk-taking that come with happiness would change the way **(that)** companies invested.
> 
> 研究人员想知道伴随幸福感而来的乐观主义和冒险倾向是否会改变公司的投资方式。
