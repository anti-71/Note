##### 1）添加范畴词

- in evolution　在进化 **的过程中**

- report the finding　报道发现 **的成果**

- He discussed greatness and excellence. 他探讨了伟大和卓越 **的含义**。

##### 2）添加动词

- After the basketball match, the chairman still has an important meeting.

在观看篮球比赛之后，主席还要 **参加** 一个重要会议。

- While comment and reaction from lawyers may enhance stories, it is preferable for journalists to rely on their own notions of significance and make their own judgments.

尽管律师 **发表** 的评论和 **做出** 的反应可能会提高报道的质量，但是新闻记者依赖自己对重要性的理解并做出判断则更加难能可贵。

##### 3）添加解释性词汇

- During that course I realised that I was not good enough in this area.

在课程 **学习** 期间，我意识到自己在这个领域不够优秀。

- His function is analogous to that of a judge, who must accept the obligation of revealing in as obvious a manner as possible the course of reasoning which led him to his decision.

**知识分子** 的职责与法官 **的职责** 相似，他必须承担一种义务：用尽可能清楚的方式展示使其做出决定的推理过程。

##### 4）添加量词

- Flowers bloom.　**百** 花齐放

- The United States is the product of two principal forces—the immigration of European peoples with their varied ideas, customs and national characteristics and the impact of a new country which modified these traits.

如今的美国是两股主要力量的产物：**第一**，欧洲各国移民带来了不同观念、风俗习惯和民族特点；**第二**，改变融合了这些欧洲文化特点的新建国家所带来的影响。
