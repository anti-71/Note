> [!question]
>
> 设 3 阶矩阵 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 的特征值为 $\lambda_1=1,\lambda_2=2,\lambda_3=3$，对应的[[考研/数学/02 线性代数/05 特征值与特征向量/特征值与特征向量的定义|特征向量]]依次为
>
> $$
> \boldsymbol{\xi}_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix},\quad
> \boldsymbol{\xi}_2=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\4\end{pmatrix},\quad
> \boldsymbol{\xi}_3=\begin{pmatrix}1\\3\\9\end{pmatrix}
> $$
>
> 又向量 $\boldsymbol{\beta}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\3\end{pmatrix}$
> 
> （2）求 $\boldsymbol{A}^n\boldsymbol{\beta}$（$n$ 为自然数）

（2）由（1）可得 $\boldsymbol{\beta}=2\boldsymbol{\xi}_1-2\boldsymbol{\xi}_2+\boldsymbol{\xi}_3$

$\because \boldsymbol{\xi}_1,\boldsymbol{\xi}_2,\boldsymbol{\xi}_3$ 分别为 $\boldsymbol{A}$ 对应于 $\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3$ 的特征向量

$\therefore \boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{\xi}_1=\lambda_1\boldsymbol{\xi}_1=\boldsymbol{\xi}_1,\ \boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{\xi}_2=\lambda_2\boldsymbol{\xi}_2=2\boldsymbol{\xi}_2,\ \boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{\xi}_3=\lambda_3\boldsymbol{\xi}_3=3\boldsymbol{\xi}_3$

进而有 $\boldsymbol{A}^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_i=\lambda_i^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_i,\ i=1,2,3$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\boldsymbol{A}^n\boldsymbol{\beta} &= \boldsymbol{A}^n(2\boldsymbol{\xi}_1-2\boldsymbol{\xi}_2+\boldsymbol{\xi}_3) \\[4pt]
&= 2\boldsymbol{A}^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_1 - 2\boldsymbol{A}^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_2 + \boldsymbol{A}^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_3 \\[4pt]
&= 2\cdot 1^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_1 - 2\cdot 2^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_2 + 3^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_3 \\[4pt]
&= 2\boldsymbol{\xi}_1 - 2^{n+1}\boldsymbol{\xi}_2 + 3^n\boldsymbol{\xi}_3 \\[4pt]
&= 2\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix} - 2^{n+1}\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\4\end{pmatrix} + 3^n\begin{pmatrix}1\\3\\9\end{pmatrix} \\[4pt]
&= \begin{pmatrix}
2-2^{n+1}+3^n \\
2-2^{n+2}+3^{n+1} \\
2-2^{n+3}+3^{n+2}
\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$
