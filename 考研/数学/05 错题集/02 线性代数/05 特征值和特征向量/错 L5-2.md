> [!question]
>
> 已知 $\boldsymbol{A} \sim \boldsymbol{B}$，其中 $\boldsymbol{B} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{bmatrix}$，则 $|\boldsymbol{A}+2\boldsymbol{E}| = \underline{\qquad}$

**法一**

$\because \boldsymbol{A} \sim \boldsymbol{B} \quad \therefore \boldsymbol{A}+2\boldsymbol{E} \sim \boldsymbol{B}+2\boldsymbol{E}$

根据**[[考研/数学/02 线性代数/05 特征值与特征向量/相似的性质|相似]]的性质**，

$$
|\boldsymbol{A}+2\boldsymbol{E}| = |\boldsymbol{B}+2\boldsymbol{E}| = \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 5 \end{vmatrix} = 8
$$

**法二**

$\because \boldsymbol{A} \sim \boldsymbol{B} \quad \therefore \boldsymbol{A}$、$\boldsymbol{B}$ [[考研/数学/02 线性代数/05 特征值与特征向量/特征值与特征向量的定义|特征值]]相同

$$
|\lambda\boldsymbol{E}-\boldsymbol{B}| = \begin{vmatrix} \lambda & -1 \\ -2 & \lambda-3 \end{vmatrix} = \lambda^2-3\lambda-2=0 \implies
\begin{cases}
\lambda_1\lambda_2 = -2\\
\lambda_1+\lambda_2 = 3
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\therefore |\boldsymbol{A}+2\boldsymbol{E}| = (\lambda_1+2)(\lambda_2+2) = \lambda_1\lambda_2+2(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)+4 = 8
$$

**法三**

填空题，$\because \boldsymbol{A} \sim \boldsymbol{B} \quad \therefore$ 取 $\boldsymbol{A}=\boldsymbol{B}$，后续步骤同法一
