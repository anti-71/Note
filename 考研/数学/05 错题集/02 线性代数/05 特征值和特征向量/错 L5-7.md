> [!question]
>
> 设 $\boldsymbol{A}=\begin{pmatrix}2&1&1\\1&0&1\\1&1&0\end{pmatrix}$，$\boldsymbol{B}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&1\\1&2&1\\1&1&0\end{pmatrix}$，求可逆矩阵 $\boldsymbol{C}$，使 $\boldsymbol{A}=\boldsymbol{C}^\mathrm{T}\boldsymbol{B}\boldsymbol{C}$

**法一**

$\boldsymbol{A}$、$\boldsymbol{B}$ 形式接近，考虑初等变换

$$
E_{12}\boldsymbol{B}=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&1\\0&1&1\\1&1&0\end{pmatrix}
$$

$E_{12}\boldsymbol{B}E_{12}=\boldsymbol{A}$ 即 $\boldsymbol{A}=E_{12}^\mathrm{T}\boldsymbol{B}E_{12}$

$\implies \boldsymbol{C}=E_{12}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}$

**法二**

$\because \boldsymbol{A}$、$\boldsymbol{B}$ 为[[考研/数学/02 线性代数/05 特征值与特征向量/实对称矩阵（Aᵀ = A）的性质与对角化|实对称矩阵]]

$\therefore$ 若 $\boldsymbol{A}\sim\boldsymbol{B}$，$\boldsymbol{Q}_1^\mathrm{T}\boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{Q}_1=\boldsymbol{\Lambda}=\boldsymbol{Q}_2^\mathrm{T}\boldsymbol{B}\boldsymbol{Q}_2 \implies \boldsymbol{A}=\boldsymbol{Q}_1\boldsymbol{Q}_2^\mathrm{T}\boldsymbol{B}\boldsymbol{Q}_2\boldsymbol{Q}_1^\mathrm{T}=(\boldsymbol{Q}_2\boldsymbol{Q}_1^\mathrm{T})^\mathrm{T}\boldsymbol{B}(\boldsymbol{Q}_2\boldsymbol{Q}_1^\mathrm{T})$

即 $\boldsymbol{C}=\boldsymbol{Q}_2\boldsymbol{Q}_1^\mathrm{T}$

现要证 $\boldsymbol{A}\sim\boldsymbol{B}$，仅需证 $\lambda_A=\lambda_B$

计算得 $\boldsymbol{A}$：$\lambda_1=-1,\lambda_2=0,\lambda_3=3$

$\boldsymbol{B}$：$\lambda_1'=-1,\lambda_2'=0,\lambda_3'=3$ $\implies \boldsymbol{A}\sim\boldsymbol{B}$

求解 $(\boldsymbol{A}-\lambda_i\boldsymbol{E})\boldsymbol{x}=\boldsymbol{0} \implies \boldsymbol{\alpha}_1=(0,-1,1)^\mathrm{T},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2=(-1,1,1)^\mathrm{T},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3=(2,1,1)^\mathrm{T}$

$\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3$ 单位化，得 $\boldsymbol{Q}_1=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}&\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{6}}\\[6pt]-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\\[6pt]\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\end{pmatrix}$

同理，对于 $\boldsymbol{B}$，$\boldsymbol{Q}_2=\begin{pmatrix}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\\[6pt]0&-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}&\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{6}}\\[6pt]\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}&\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{6}}\end{pmatrix}$

$\therefore \boldsymbol{C}=\boldsymbol{Q}_2\boldsymbol{Q}_1^\mathrm{T}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}$
