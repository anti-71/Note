> [!question]
>
> 设 $Y_1 \sim P(\lambda_1)$ , $Y_2 \sim P(\lambda_2)$，且 $Y_1,Y_2$ [[考研/数学/03 概率论与数理统计/03 二维随机变量及其分布/随机变量独立|相互独立]]，求证：$Y_1+Y_2 \sim P(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)$

**证明**：
$$
P\{Y_1=m\} = \frac{\lambda_1^m}{m!}e^{-\lambda_1},\quad P\{Y_2=n\} = \frac{\lambda_2^n}{n!}e^{-\lambda_2},\quad m,n=0,1,2,\dots
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore P\{Y_1+Y_2=k\}
&= \sum_{i=0}^{k}P\{Y_1=i,Y_2=k-i\} \\[4pt]
&= \sum_{i=0}^{k}\frac{\lambda_1^i}{i!}e^{-\lambda_1}\cdot\frac{\lambda_2^{k-i}}{(k-i)!}e^{-\lambda_2} \\[4pt]
&= e^{-(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)}\cdot\frac{1}{k!}\sum_{i=0}^{k}\frac{k!}{i!(k-i)!}\lambda_1^i\lambda_2^{k-i} \\[4pt]
&= \frac{1}{k!}e^{-(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)}\sum_{i=0}^{k}\mathrm{C}_k^i \lambda_1^i\lambda_2^{k-i} \\[4pt]
&= \frac{(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)^k}{k!}e^{-(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore Y_1+Y_2 \sim P(\lambda_1+\lambda_2)
$$

证毕
