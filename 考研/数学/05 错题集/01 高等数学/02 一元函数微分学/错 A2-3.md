> [!question] 
> 设 $f(x) = (e^x - 1) \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1 - x^2 + x^3}{1 + x - x^3}}$，求 $f'(0)$

直接求导较复杂，用[[导数的概念|定义]]


$$
f'(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x}
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(e^x - 1) \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1 - x^2 + x^3}{1 + x - x^3}}}{x}
$$

$$
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{x} \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1 - x^2 + x^3}{1 + x - x^3}}
= 1 \cdot 1 = \boxed{1}
$$
