> [!question] 
> $y = e^x \cos x$，求 $y^{(n)}$

逐次求导找规律


$$
y' = e^x(\cos x - \sin x) = \sqrt{2} \, e^x \cos\!\left(x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right)
$$

$$
y'' = (\sqrt{2})^2 \, e^x \cos\!\left(x + \frac{2\pi}{4}\right)
$$

$$
y''' = (\sqrt{2})^3 \, e^x \cos\!\left(x + \frac{3\pi}{4}\right)
$$

由数学归纳法得通项


$$
\boxed{y^{(n)} = (\sqrt{2})^n \, e^x \cos\!\left(x + \frac{n\pi}{4}\right)}
$$

> [!note] 合角公式
> $\cos x \pm \sin x = \sqrt{2}\cos\!\left(x \mp \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)$，迭代得 $n$ 阶公式
