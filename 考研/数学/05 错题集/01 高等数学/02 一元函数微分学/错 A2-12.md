> [!question] 
> 设 $f(x)$ 在 $(-\infty, +\infty)$ 上有定义，$f'(0) = 2$，对任意的 $x,y$ 有
> $$
> f(x+y) = e^x f(y) + e^y f(x)
> $$
> 求 $f(x)$

由函数方程形式，用[[导数的概念|导数定义]]


令 $x = y = 0$ 得 $f(0) = 2f(0) \implies f(0) = 0$

$$
f'(x) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x+\Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x}
$$

代入函数方程

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{e^x f(\Delta x) + e^{\Delta x} f(x) - f(x)}{\Delta x}\\
&= e^x \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(\Delta x)}{\Delta x} + f(x) \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{e^{\Delta x} - 1}{\Delta x}\\
&= e^x f'(0) + f(x) \cdot 1\\
&= 2e^x + f(x)
\end{aligned}
$$

得一阶线性微分方程 $f'(x) - f(x) = 2e^x$

通解

$$
f(x) = e^{\int dx} \left( \int 2e^x e^{-\int dx} dx + C \right)
= e^x \left( \int 2 dx + C \right)
= 2x e^x + C e^x
$$

代入 $f(0) = 0$ 得 $C = 0$

故 $\boxed{f(x) = 2x e^x}$
