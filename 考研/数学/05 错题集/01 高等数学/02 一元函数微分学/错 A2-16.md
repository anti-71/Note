> [!question] 
> 已知 $f(x)$ 二阶可导，其反函数为 $g(x)$，且 $f(1) = 3$，$f'(1) = -2$，$f''(1) = 1$，求 $g'(3)$，$g''(3)$ 的值

由反函数概念：$f[g(x)] = x$，两边求导

$$
f'[g(x)] \cdot g'(x) = 1 \quad\Rightarrow\quad g'(x) = \frac{1}{f'[g(x)]}
$$

再求导：
$$
g''(x) = -\frac{f''[g(x)] \cdot g'(x)}{\{f'[g(x)]\}^2}
= -\frac{f''[g(x)]}{\{f'[g(x)]\}^3}
$$

代入 $x = 3$，由 $f(1) = 3$ 知 $g(3) = 1$：

$$
g'(3) = \frac{1}{f'[g(3)]} = \frac{1}{f'(1)} = -\frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
g''(3) = -\frac{f''[g(3)]}{\{f'[g(3)]\}^3} = -\frac{f''(1)}{\{f'(1)\}^3}
= -\frac{1}{(-2)^3} = \frac{1}{8}
$$

故 $\boxed{g'(3) = -\dfrac{1}{2},\; g''(3) = \dfrac{1}{8}}$
