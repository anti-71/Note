> [!question] 
> 设 $x \geqslant 0$，由中值定理，存在 $\xi \in (x, x+1)$ 使
> $$
> \sqrt{x+1} - \sqrt{x} = \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{\xi}}
> $$
> 记 $\theta(x) = \xi - x$，则下列正确的是
> 
> （A）$\theta(x)$ 单调递增，$\lim\limits_{x\to0^+}\theta(x)=0$，$\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\theta(x)=\dfrac14$
> 
> （B）$\theta(x)$ 单调递减，$\lim\limits_{x\to0^+}\theta(x)=\dfrac14$，$\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\theta(x)=0$
> 
> （C）$\theta(x)$ 单调递增，$\lim\limits_{x\to0^+}\theta(x)=\dfrac14$，$\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\theta(x)=\dfrac12$
> 
> （D）$\theta(x)$ 单调递减，$\lim\limits_{x\to0^+}\theta(x)=\dfrac12$，$\lim\limits_{x\to+\infty}\theta(x)=\dfrac14$

由 $\sqrt{x+1} - \sqrt{x} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x+1} + \sqrt{x}} = \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x+\theta(x)}}$ 得

$$
\sqrt{x+1} + \sqrt{x} = 2\sqrt{x+\theta(x)}
$$

平方化简：
$$
\theta(x) = \dfrac{1}{4}\bigl(1 - 2x + 2\sqrt{x^2+x}\bigr)
$$

求导：
$$
\theta'(x) = -\dfrac12 + \dfrac{2x+1}{4\sqrt{x^2+x}}
$$

比较 $(2x+1)^2 = 4x^2+4x+1 > 4x^2+4x = (2\sqrt{x^2+x})^2$

$\therefore 2x+1 > 2\sqrt{x^2+x}$，故 $\theta'(x) > 0$，$\theta(x)$ **严格递增**

极限：
- $x\to0^+$：$\theta(x)\to \dfrac14$
- $x\to+\infty$：$\sqrt{x^2+x} = x\sqrt{1+\dfrac1x} = x + \dfrac12 - \dfrac1{8x}+o(\dfrac1x)$

$$
\theta(x) = \dfrac14\bigl(1 - 2x + 2x + 1 - \dfrac1{4x} + \cdots\bigr) = \dfrac12 - \dfrac1{16x} + \cdots \to \dfrac12
$$

故选 **$\boxed{\text{C}}$**
