> [!question] 
> $\displaystyle\dfrac{d}{dt}\int_{t^2}^{t} x f(t^2 - x^2) \, dx$

被积函数含 $t$，需先换元再求导

令 $u = t^2 - x^2$，则 $du = -2x \, dx$，$x \, dx = -\dfrac{1}{2} du$

$$
\int_{t^2}^{t} x f(t^2 - x^2) \, dx
= -\dfrac{1}{2} \int_{t^2 - t^4}^{0} f(u) \, du
$$

$$
\text{原式} = -\dfrac{1}{2} \dfrac{d}{dt} \int_{t^2 - t^4}^{0} f(u) \, du
$$

由 [[导数的概念|变限积分求导公式]]：
$$
= -\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot (-1) \cdot (2t - 4t^3) \cdot f(t^2 - t^4)
= \boxed{(t - 2t^3) \, f(t^2 - t^4)}
$$
