> [!question] 
> 已知函数 $y = f(x)$ 由参数方程
> $$
> \begin{cases}
> x = \sqrt{3}\, t - 1 \\
> t e^y - y = 0
> \end{cases}
> $$
> 确定，求 $\left. \dfrac{dy}{dx} \right|_{x = -1}$ 和 $\left. \dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} \right|_{x = -1}$

$\dfrac{dx}{dt} = \sqrt{3}$

对 $t e^y - y = 0$ 两边对 $t$ 求导：
$$
e^y (1 + t y') - y' = 0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad y' = \frac{e^y}{1 - t e^y}
$$

当 $x = -1$ 时，$\sqrt{3}\,t - 1 = -1 \Rightarrow t = 0$，代入得 $y = 0$

$$
\left. \frac{dy}{dx} \right|_{t=0} = \frac{y'|_{t=0}}{dx/dt}
= \frac{\dfrac{e^0}{1 - 0}}{ \sqrt{3}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \boxed{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}}
$$

二阶导：
$$
\frac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \frac{d\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)/dt}{dx/dt}
= \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot \frac{d}{dt}\!\left(\frac{e^y}{\sqrt{3}(1 - t e^y)}\right)
$$

代 $t = 0,\; y = 0,\; y' = 1$ 得：
$$
\boxed{\left. \frac{d^2y}{dx^2} \right|_{t=0} = \frac{2}{3}}
$$
