> [!question] 
> 设 $f(x)$ 在 $(-1,1)$ 内二阶可导，$f''(0) \neq 0$
> 对任意 $x \in (-1,0) \cup (0,1)$，存在 $\theta(x)$ 介于 $0$ 与 $x$ 之间，且满足
> $$
> f(x) - f(0) = x f'[\theta(x)x]
> $$
> 则 $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \theta(x) =$
> 
> （A）$-1$　（B）$-\dfrac12$　（C）$\dfrac12$　（D）$1$

将 $f(x)$ 在 $x=0$ 处[[泰勒定理#皮亚诺型余项泰勒公式|泰勒展开]]

$$
f(x) = f(0) + f'(0)x + \dfrac{f''(0)}{2}x^2 + o(x^2)
$$

将 $f'(x)$ 在 $x=0$ 处展开
$$
f'(x) = f'(0) + f''(0)x + o(x)
$$

代入原式左边
$$
f(x) - f(0) = f'(0)x + \dfrac{f''(0)}{2}x^2 + o(x^2)
$$

右边
$$
\begin{aligned}
x f'[\theta(x)x] &= x\bigl[f'(0) + f''(0)\theta(x)x + o(\theta(x)x)\bigr] \\
&= f'(0)x + f''(0)\theta(x)x^2 + o(x^2)
\end{aligned}
$$

两边消去 $f'(0)x$
$$
\dfrac{f''(0)}{2}x^2 + o(x^2) = f''(0)\theta(x)x^2 + o(x^2)
$$

同除以 $x^2$ 并取极限 $x \to 0$
$$
\dfrac{f''(0)}{2} = f''(0)\lim_{x\to0}\theta(x)
$$

由 $f''(0) \neq 0$ 得 $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\theta(x) = \boxed{\dfrac12}$
