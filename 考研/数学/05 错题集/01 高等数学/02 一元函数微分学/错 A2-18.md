> [!question] 
> 设 $y = y(x)$ 由
> $$
> \begin{cases}
> x = t^3 + 2t + 1, \\
> t - \displaystyle\int_1^{y+t} e^{-u^2} \, du = 0
> \end{cases}
> $$
> 确定，求 $\left.\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right|_{t=0}$，$\left.\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}\right|_{t=0}$

$\dfrac{dx}{dt} = 3t^2 + 2$

对 $t - \displaystyle\int_1^{y+t} e^{-u^2} \, du = 0$ 两边对 $t$ 求导：
$$
1 = \left(\dfrac{dy}{dt} + 1\right) e^{-(y+t)^2} \;\implies\; \dfrac{dy}{dt} = e^{(y+t)^2} - 1
$$

由[[求导法则|参数方程求导法]]：
$$
\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{dy/dt}{dx/dt} = \dfrac{e^{(y+t)^2} - 1}{3t^2 + 2}
$$

当 $t = 0$ 时，$x = 1$，代入原方程得 $y = 1$

$$
\left.\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right|_{t=0} = \dfrac{e - 1}{2}
$$

二阶导：
$$
\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2} = \dfrac{d}{dt}\!\left(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\right) \Big/ \dfrac{dx}{dt}
= \dfrac{2e^{(y+t)^2}(y+t)\left(\dfrac{dy}{dt} + 1\right)(3t^2+2) - 6t\left[e^{(y+t)^2} - 1\right]}{(3t^2+2)^3}
$$

代 $t = 0,\; y = 1,\; \dfrac{dy}{dt} = e-1$ 得：
$$
\left.\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}\right|_{t=0} = \dfrac{2e \cdot 1 \cdot e \cdot 2}{8} = \boxed{\dfrac{e^2}{2}}
$$
