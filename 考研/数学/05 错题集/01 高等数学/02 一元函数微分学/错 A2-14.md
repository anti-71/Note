> [!question] 
> $y = x^{x^a} + x^{a^x} + a^{x^x}$（$a > 0,\; x > 0$），求 $y'$

三项分别用[[求导法则|对数求导法]]（$u^v$ 型幂指函数 $\to e^{v\ln u}$）


原式 $= \left(e^{x^a \ln x}\right)' + \left(e^{a^x \ln x}\right)' + \left(e^{x^x \ln a}\right)'$

逐项求导

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\left(e^{x^a \ln x}\right)' = x^{x^a} \left(a x^{a-1} \ln x + \frac{x^a}{x}\right) \\
&\left(e^{a^x \ln x}\right)' = x^{a^x} \left(a^x \ln a \ln x + \frac{a^x}{x}\right) \\
&\left(e^{x^x \ln a}\right)' = a^{x^x} \cdot x^x (\ln x + 1) \ln a
\end{aligned}
$$

合并得

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
y' =& \; x^{x^a + a - 1} (a \ln x + 1) \\
&+ x^{a^x} \cdot a^x \left(\ln a \ln x + \frac{1}{x}\right) \\
&+ a^{x^x} \, x^x \ln a \, (\ln x + 1)
\end{aligned}}
$$
