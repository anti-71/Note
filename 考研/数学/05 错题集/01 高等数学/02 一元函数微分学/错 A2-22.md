> [!question] 
> 求函数 $y = \sqrt{4x^2 + x} \ln\!\left(2 + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)$ 的[[斜渐近线]]方程

**情况一：$x \to +\infty$**

$$
k_1 = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \dfrac{y}{x}
= \lim_{x \to +\infty} \sqrt{4 + \dfrac{1}{x}} \ln\!\left(2 + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)
= 2\ln 2
$$

$$
b_1 = \lim_{x \to +\infty} (y - k_1 x)
= \lim_{t \to 0^+} \dfrac{\sqrt{4 + t} \ln(2 + t) - 2\ln 2}{t}
$$

泰勒展开：$\sqrt{4 + t} = 2 + \dfrac{t}{4} + o(t)$，$\ln(2 + t) = \ln 2 + \dfrac{t}{2} + o(t)$

$$
\sqrt{4 + t}\ln(2 + t) = 2\ln 2 + \left(1 + \dfrac{\ln 2}{4}\right)t + o(t)
$$

$$
b_1 = 1 + \dfrac{\ln 2}{4}
$$

渐近线：$\boxed{y = 2\ln 2 \cdot x + 1 + \dfrac{\ln 2}{4}}$

**情况二：$x \to -\infty$**

此时 $\sqrt{4x^2 + x} = -x\sqrt{4 + \dfrac{1}{x}}$

$$
k_2 = \lim_{x \to -\infty} \dfrac{y}{x}
= \lim_{x \to -\infty} \left[-\sqrt{4 + \dfrac{1}{x}} \ln\!\left(2 + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)\right]
= -2\ln 2
$$

$$
b_2 = \lim_{x \to -\infty} (y - k_2 x)
= \lim_{t \to 0^-} \dfrac{2\ln 2 - \sqrt{4 + t}\ln(2 + t)}{t}
= -\left(1 + \dfrac{\ln 2}{4}\right)
$$

渐近线：$\boxed{y = -2\ln 2 \cdot x - 1 - \dfrac{\ln 2}{4}}$
