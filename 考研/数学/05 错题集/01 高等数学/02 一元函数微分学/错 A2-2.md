> [!question] 
> 若 $f(x) = \begin{cases} g(x)\cos\dfrac{1}{x}, & x \ne 0 \\ 0, & x = 0 \end{cases}$，而 $g(0) = g'(0) = 0$，则 $f'(0) = ?$

**分段函数在分段点的导数必须用[[导数的概念|定义]]求**


$$
f'(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0}
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{g(x)}{x} \cos\frac{1}{x}
$$

$$
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{g(x) - g(0)}{x - 0} \cdot \cos\frac{1}{x}
= g'(0) \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} \cos\frac{1}{x}
$$

$g'(0) = 0$，$\cos\frac{1}{x}$ 有界，由无穷小 $\times$ 有界量 $= 0$：

$$
\boxed{f'(0) = 0}
$$
