> [!question]
>
> 设 $D=\left\{(x,y)\;\big|\;x^2+y^2\leqslant \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right\}$，求
> $$
> \iint\limits_D \frac{\tan(x^2)}{1-\tan(x^2)\tan(y^2)}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y
> $$

$D$ 为圆域，由轮换对称性，分子中也引入 $\tan(y^2)$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \frac12 \iint\limits_D \frac{\tan(x^2)+\tan(y^2)}{1-\tan(x^2)\tan(y^2)}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y \\
&= \frac12 \iint\limits_D \tan(x^2+y^2)\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y \quad (\text{两角和公式}) \\
&= \frac12 \int_{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}} \tan(r^2) \cdot r\,\mathrm{d}r \quad (\text{极坐标}) \\
&= \frac{\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}} \tan(r^2)\,\mathrm{d}(r^2) \\
&= -\frac{\pi}{2} \left.\ln\cos(r^2)\right|_{0}^{\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}}} \\
&= \frac{\pi}{4}\ln 2
\end{aligned}
$$
