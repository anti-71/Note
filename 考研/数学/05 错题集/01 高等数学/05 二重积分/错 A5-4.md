> [!question]
>
> 计算二重积分 $\displaystyle\iint\limits_D \frac{\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}}{\sqrt{4a^2 - x^2 - y^2}} \,\mathrm{d}\sigma$，其中 $D$ 是由曲线 $y = -a + \sqrt{a^2 - x^2}\ (a > 0)$ 和直线 $y = -x$ 围成的区域

曲线 $y = -a + \sqrt{a^2 - x^2}$ 即 $x^2 + (y + a)^2 = a^2$ 的上半圆
与直线 $y = -x$ 的交点为 $(0,0)$ 和 $(a,-a)$

![](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/7a2a1d70e9a84ad1872e3966611da263.png)

极坐标变换：$\begin{cases}x = r\cos\theta \\ y = r\sin\theta\end{cases}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{0} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{-2a\sin\theta} \frac{r^2}{\sqrt{4a^2 - r^2}} \,\mathrm{d}r
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $r = 2a\sin t$，则 $\mathrm{d}r = 2a\cos t \,\mathrm{d}t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{0} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{-\theta} \frac{(2a\sin t)^2}{2a\cos t} \cdot 2a\cos t \,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{0} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{-\theta} 4a^2\sin^2 t \,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{0} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{-\theta} 4a^2 \cdot \frac{1 - \cos 2t}{2} \,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{0} 2a^2 \left.\left(t - \frac{\sin 2t}{2}\right)\right|_{0}^{-\theta} \mathrm{d}\theta \\
&= \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{0} 2a^2 \left(-\theta + \frac{\sin 2\theta}{2}\right) \mathrm{d}\theta \\
&= 2a^2 \left.\left(-\frac{\theta^2}{2} - \frac{1}{4}\cos 2\theta\right)\right|_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{0} \\
&= a^2 \left(\frac{\pi^2}{16} - \frac{1}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
