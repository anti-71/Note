> [!question]
>
> 设 $D=\left\{(x,y)\,\bigg|\, 2\leqslant \dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2}\leqslant 4,\ 2\leqslant \dfrac{y}{x^2+y^2}\leqslant 4\right\}$，则 $\displaystyle\iint\limits_D \dfrac{1}{xy}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=\underline{\qquad}$

画出积分区域 D

![区域 D 示意图|885x467](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/4586597b82d24021b0374ffc6d5b6a05.png)

[[利用极坐标计算|极坐标变换]] $x=r\cos\theta$，$y=r\sin\theta$，则

$$
\dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2}=\dfrac{\cos\theta}{r},\qquad
\dfrac{y}{x^2+y^2}=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{r}
$$

$$
2\leqslant\dfrac{\cos\theta}{r}\leqslant4
\implies \dfrac{\cos\theta}{4}\leqslant r\leqslant\dfrac{\cos\theta}{2}
$$

$$
2\leqslant\dfrac{\sin\theta}{r}\leqslant4
\implies \dfrac{\sin\theta}{4}\leqslant r\leqslant\dfrac{\sin\theta}{2}
$$

$D$ 关于 $y=x$ 轴对称，且被积函数满足 $f(x,y)=f(y,x)$，故只需计算 $y=x$ 一侧的积分，再乘以 2

在 $y=x$ 下方一侧即 $\theta\in\left[0,\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right]$，有 $\cos\theta\geqslant\sin\theta$，故

$$
r\in\left[\max\left\{\dfrac{\cos\theta}{4},\dfrac{\sin\theta}{4}\right\},\ \min\left\{\dfrac{\cos\theta}{2},\dfrac{\sin\theta}{2}\right\}\right]
=\left[\dfrac{\cos\theta}{4},\ \dfrac{\sin\theta}{2}\right]
$$

又需 $\dfrac{\cos\theta}{4}\leqslant\dfrac{\sin\theta}{2}\implies\tan\theta\geqslant\dfrac12$，联立边界

$$
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2}=4\\[4pt]
\dfrac{y}{x^2+y^2}=2
\end{cases}
\implies \dfrac{y}{x}=\dfrac12 \implies \theta=\arctan\dfrac12
$$

故 $\theta\in\left[\arctan\dfrac12,\ \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right]$，$r\in\left[\dfrac{1}{4\cos\theta},\ \dfrac{1}{2\sin\theta}\right]$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= 2\cdot\displaystyle\int_{\arctan\dfrac12}^{\dfrac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{d}\theta
     \displaystyle\int_{\dfrac{1}{4\cos\theta}}^{\dfrac{1}{2\sin\theta}}
     \dfrac{r\,\mathrm{d}r}{r^2\sin\theta\cos\theta} \\[4pt]
  &= 2\cdot\displaystyle\int_{\arctan\dfrac12}^{\dfrac{\pi}{4}}
     \dfrac{\ln(2\tan\theta)}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}\,\mathrm{d}\theta
   = 2\cdot\displaystyle\int_{\arctan\dfrac12}^{\dfrac{\pi}{4}}
     \dfrac{\ln(2\tan\theta)}{\tan\theta}\,\mathrm{d}\tan\theta
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $t=2\tan\theta$，则 $\tan\theta\in\left[\dfrac12,1\right]\implies t\in[1,2]$，且 $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}\tan\theta}{\tan\theta}=\dfrac{\mathrm{d}t}{t}$

$$
I = 2\cdot\displaystyle\int_{1}^{2}\dfrac{\ln t}{t}\,\mathrm{d}t
= \Bigl[(\ln t)^2\Bigr]_{1}^{2}
= \boxed{(\ln 2)^2}
$$
