> [!question]
>
> 求 $\displaystyle \iint\limits_D \frac{2xy}{(x^2+y^2)^2}\,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$，其中 $D$ 是曲线 $x^2+y^2=\sqrt{xy}$，$x^2+y^2=1$，$x=0$，$y=0$ 在第一象限围成的区域

令 $x=r\cos\theta,\ y=r\sin\theta$

$$
\text{曲线 }x^2+y^2=\sqrt{xy}
\;\Longrightarrow\; r^2 = \sqrt{r^2\sin\theta\cos\theta} = r\sqrt{\sin\theta\cos\theta}
$$

积分区域 $D$：$\theta\in[0,\dfrac\pi2]$，$r\in\bigl[\sqrt{\sin\theta\cos\theta},\,1\bigr]$

被积函数 $\displaystyle\frac{2xy}{(x^2+y^2)^2} = \frac{2\,r^2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{r^4} = \frac{2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{r^2}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \int_{0}^{\frac\pi2}\!\mathrm{d}\theta \int_{\sqrt{\sin\theta\cos\theta}}^{1}
     \frac{2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{r^2}\cdot r\,\mathrm{d}r \\[4pt]
  &= \int_{0}^{\frac\pi2} 2\sin\theta\cos\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta
     \int_{\sqrt{\sin\theta\cos\theta}}^{1} \frac{1}{r}\,\mathrm{d}r \\[4pt]
  &= \int_{0}^{\frac\pi2} 2\sin\theta\cos\theta\,
     \Bigl[\ln r\Bigr]_{\sqrt{\sin\theta\cos\theta}}^{1}\,\mathrm{d}\theta \\[4pt]
  &= -\int_{0}^{\frac\pi2} \sin\theta\cos\theta\,
     \ln(\sin\theta\cos\theta)\,\mathrm{d}\theta
\end{aligned}
$$

利用 $\sin\theta\cos\theta = \dfrac12\sin2\theta$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= -\int_{0}^{\frac\pi2} \frac12\sin2\theta\,
     \ln\!\left(\frac12\sin2\theta\right)\mathrm{d}\theta \\[4pt]
  &= -\frac12\int_{0}^{\frac\pi2} \sin2\theta\,
     \bigl(\ln\sin2\theta - \ln2\bigr)\,\mathrm{d}\theta
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $t = 2\theta$，$\mathrm{d}\theta = \dfrac12\mathrm{d}t$

$$
I = -\frac14\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin t\,
     \bigl(\ln\sin t - \ln2\bigr)\,\mathrm{d}t
$$

由[[利用奇偶性和周期性|对称性]]，$\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi}\sin t\ln\sin t\,\mathrm{d}t = 2\int_{0}^{\frac\pi2}\sin t\ln\sin t\,\mathrm{d}t$

$$
I = -\frac12\int_{0}^{\frac\pi2}\sin t\ln\sin t\,\mathrm{d}t + \frac12\ln2 \tag{*}
$$

记 $\displaystyle J = \int_{0}^{\frac\pi2}\ln\sin t\,\mathrm{d}(1-\cos t)$，[[定积分-分部积分法|分部积分]]

> [!warning] 注意
> 若仅仅凑微分为 $\mathrm{d}(-\cos t)$，$\ln\sin t$ 在 $0^+$ 和 $\dfrac\pi2$ 都趋于无穷，故要从 d 入手，使带入 t 时整项为 0

$$
\begin{aligned}
J &= (1-\cos t)\ln\sin t\Big|_{0^+}^{\frac\pi2}
     - \int_{0}^{\frac\pi2} (1-\cos t)\,\frac{\cos t}{\sin t}\,\mathrm{d}t \\[4pt]
  &= 0 - \int_{0}^{\frac\pi2} \frac{(1-\cos t)\cos t}{\sin t}\,\mathrm{d}t \\[4pt]
\end{aligned}
$$

上下同乘 $1+\cos t$

$$
= -\int_{0}^{\frac\pi2} \frac{\sin t\cos t}{1+\cos t}\,\mathrm{d}t
$$

令 $u = \cos t$，$\mathrm{d}u = -\sin t\,\mathrm{d}t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
J &= \int_{1}^{0} \frac{u}{1+u}\,\mathrm{d}u
   = \int_{0}^{1} \frac{u}{1+u}\,\mathrm{d}u \\[4pt]
  &= \int_{0}^{1} \!\left(1 - \frac{1}{1+u}\right)\!\mathrm{d}u
   = \bigl[u - \ln(1+u)\bigr]_{0}^{1} \\[4pt]
  &= 1 - \ln2
\end{aligned}
$$

即 $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\frac\pi2}\sin t\ln\sin t\,\mathrm{d}t = -J = \ln2 - 1$，代入\*式

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= -\frac12(\ln2 - 1) + \frac12\ln2
   = \boxed{\frac12}
\end{aligned}
$$
