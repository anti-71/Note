> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}y\int_{\sqrt{y}}^{1}\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^{x^3}+1}\,\mathrm{d}x = \underline{\qquad}$

交换积分次序：

![](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/ffde24d09e4c4d6fbcef4a474c80dbcb.png)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{0}^{x^2}\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^{x^3}+1}\,\mathrm{d}y \\
&= \int_{0}^{1}x^2\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^{x^3}+1}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac{1}{3}\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^{x^3}+1}\,\mathrm{d}(x^3) \\
&\xlongequal{t=x^3} \frac{1}{3}\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^{t}+1}\,\mathrm{d}t \\
&\xlongequal{u=\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^{t}+1}} \frac{1}{3}\int_{\sqrt{2}}^{\sqrt{\mathrm{e}+1}} \frac{2u^2}{u^2-1}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= \frac{2}{3}\int_{\sqrt{2}}^{\sqrt{\mathrm{e}+1}} \frac{u^2-1+1}{u^2-1}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= \frac{2}{3}\left.\left(u+\frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{u-1}{u+1}\right|\right)\right|_{\sqrt{2}}^{\sqrt{\mathrm{e}+1}} \\
&= \frac{2}{3}\left(\sqrt{\mathrm{e}+1}-\sqrt{2}+\ln\frac{\sqrt{\mathrm{e}+1}-1}{\sqrt{2}-1}-\frac{1}{2}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
