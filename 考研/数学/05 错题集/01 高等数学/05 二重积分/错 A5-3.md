> [!question]
>
> 设区域 $D: x^2 + y^2 \leqslant ay\ (a > 0)$，则二重积分
> $$
> I = \iint\limits_D \bigl( xy + y\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} + x^3\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \bigr) \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \frac{4}{15}a^4
> $$

$\because$ 区域 $D$ 关于 $y$ 轴对称，且 $xy$、$x^3\sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ 均为关于 $x$ 的奇函数

$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore I &= \iint\limits_D y\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \,\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y \\
&= \int_{0}^{\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{a\sin\theta} r\sin\theta \cdot r \cdot \mathrm{d}r \\
&= \frac{a^4}{4} \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin^5\theta \,\mathrm{d}\theta \\
&= \frac{a^4}{2} \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^5\theta \,\mathrm{d}\theta \\
&= \frac{a^4}{2} \times \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{2}{3} \\
&= \frac{4}{15}a^4
\end{aligned}
$$
