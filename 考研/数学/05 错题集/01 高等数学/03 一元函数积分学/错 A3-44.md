> $\displaystyle\int_{3}^{+\infty} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x-1)^4 \sqrt{x^2-2x}} = \underline{\qquad}$

$$
\text{原式} = \int_{3}^{+\infty} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x-1)^4\sqrt{(x-1)^2-1}}
$$

令$\ x-1=\sec t \implies \mathrm{d}x=\sec t \tan t \,\mathrm{d}t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \int_{\frac{\pi}{3}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sec t \tan t}{\sec^4 t \cdot |\tan t|}\,\mathrm{d}t = \int_{\frac{\pi}{3}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^3 t \,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \int_{\frac{\pi}{3}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} (1-\sin^2 t) \,\mathrm{d}\sin t = \left.\sin t\right|_{\frac{\pi}{3}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} - \left.\frac{1}{3}\sin^3 t\right|_{\frac{\pi}{3}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \\
&= \left(1-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) - \frac{1}{3}\left(1-\frac{3}{8}\sqrt{3}\right) \\
&= \frac{2}{3}-\frac{3}{8}\sqrt{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
