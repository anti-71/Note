> $\displaystyle\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1+\sin x+\cos x}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{1}{2\sin\frac{x}{2}\cos\frac{x}{2}+2\cos^2\frac{x}{2}}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac12\int \frac{1}{\tan\frac{x}{2}+1}\,\mathrm{d}\left(\tan\frac{x}{2}+1\right) \\
&= \frac12\ln\left|\tan\frac{x}{2}+1\right|+C
\end{aligned}
$$
