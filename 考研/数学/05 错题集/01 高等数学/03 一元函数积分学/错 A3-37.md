> $\displaystyle\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt[3]{(x+1)^2(x-1)^4}}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{1}{\sqrt[3]{\frac{(x+1)^2}{(x-1)^2}\cdot (x-1)^6}}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int \frac{1}{(x-1)^2\cdot \left(\frac{x+1}{x-1}\right)^{\frac23}}\,\mathrm{d}x
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{x+1}{x-1}} = u \implies x = \dfrac{u^3+1}{u^3-1},\ \mathrm{d}x = \dfrac{-6u^2}{(u^3-1)^2}\,\mathrm{d}u$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{1}{\frac{4}{(u^3-1)^2}\cdot u^2}\cdot \frac{-6u^2}{(u^3-1)^2}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= -\frac32 u + C \\
&= -\frac32 \sqrt[3]{\frac{x+1}{x-1}} + C
\end{aligned}
$$
