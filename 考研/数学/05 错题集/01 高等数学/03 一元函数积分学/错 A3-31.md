> $\displaystyle\int x\sin^2 x\,\mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \frac12\int x(1-\cos2x)\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac14x^2-\frac12\int x\,\mathrm{d}\sin2x\cdot\frac12 \\
&= \frac14x^2-\frac14x\sin2x+\frac14\int \sin2x\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac14x^2-\frac14x\sin2x-\frac18\cos2x+C
\end{aligned}
$$
