> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi} \sqrt{1-\sin x} \,\mathrm{d}x$

对被开方项变形：

$$
1-\sin x = \sin^2\frac{x}{2} + \cos^2\frac{x}{2} - 2\sin\frac{x}{2}\cos\frac{x}{2}
= \left(\sin\frac{x}{2} - \cos\frac{x}{2}\right)^2
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int_{0}^{\pi} \left|\sin\frac{x}{2} - \cos\frac{x}{2}\right| \mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\int_{0}^{\pi/2} \left(\sin\frac{x}{2} - \cos\frac{x}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}x
+ \int_{\pi/2}^{\pi} \left(\sin\frac{x}{2} - \cos\frac{x}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \left.2\cos\frac{x}{2}\right|_{0}^{\pi/2}
+ \left.2\sin\frac{x}{2}\right|_{0}^{\pi/2}
- \left.2\cos\frac{x}{2}\right|_{\pi/2}^{\pi}
- \left.2\sin\frac{x}{2}\right|_{\pi/2}^{\pi} \\
&= 4\sqrt{2} - 4 = 4(\sqrt{2} - 1)
\end{aligned}
$$
