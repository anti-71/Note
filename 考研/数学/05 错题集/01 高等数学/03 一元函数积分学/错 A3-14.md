> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin^n x}{\cos^n x + \sin^n x}\,\mathrm{d}x$，$n \geqslant 1$

**区间再现换元**：令 $t = \dfrac{\pi}{2} - x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &\xlongequal{t=\frac{\pi}{2}-x}
\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos^n t}{\sin^n t + \cos^n t}\,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos^n x}{\sin^n x + \cos^n x}\,\mathrm{d}x
\end{aligned}
$$

两式相加：

$$
\begin{aligned}
2I &= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin^n x}{\cos^n x + \sin^n x}\,\mathrm{d}x
+ \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos^n x}{\sin^n x + \cos^n x}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d}x = \frac{\pi}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

所以 $I = \dfrac{\pi}{4}$
