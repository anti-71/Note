> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int \frac{\cos x}{2\sin x + \cos x}\,\mathrm{d}x$

**法一** 万能代换

令 $\tan x = t$，则 $x = \arctan t$，$\mathrm{d}x = \dfrac{1}{1+t^2}\mathrm{d}t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{1}{2t+1}\cdot\frac{1}{1+t^2}\,\mathrm{d}t \\
&\xlongequal{\text{待定系数}} \frac15\int\left( \frac{4}{2t+1} - \frac{2t-1}{1+t^2} \right)\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \frac45\int \frac{1}{2t+1}\,\mathrm{d}t - \frac15\int \frac{2t}{1+t^2}\,\mathrm{d}t + \frac15\int \frac{1}{1+t^2}\,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \frac25\ln|2t+1| - \frac15\ln(1+t^2) + \frac15\arctan t + C \\
&= \frac25\ln|2\tan x+1| - \frac15\ln(1+\tan^2 x) + \frac15 x + C
\end{aligned}
$$

**法二**

凑 $(2\sin x + \cos x)' = 2\cos x - \sin x$，设 $\cos x = A(2\sin x+\cos x) + B(2\cos x-\sin x)$

解得 $A = \dfrac15$，$B = \dfrac25$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{A(2\sin x+\cos x) + B(2\cos x-\sin x)}{2\sin x+\cos x}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int A\,\mathrm{d}x + \int B\,\frac{\mathrm{d}(2\sin x+\cos x)}{2\sin x+\cos x} \\
&= \frac15 x + \frac25\ln|2\sin x+\cos x| + C
\end{aligned}
$$

两种结果等价：$\dfrac25\ln|2\tan x+1| - \dfrac15\ln(1+\tan^2 x) = \dfrac25\ln|2\sin x+\cos x|$
