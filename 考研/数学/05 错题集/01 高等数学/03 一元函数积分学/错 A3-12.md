> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int_{\frac12}^{\frac32} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{|x-x^2|}}$

去绝对值，分段积分：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int_{\frac12}^{1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x-x^2}} \mathrm{d}x
+ \int_{1}^{\frac32} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2-x}} \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int_{\frac12}^{1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\frac14-(x-\frac12)^2}} \mathrm{d}x
+ \int_{1}^{\frac32} \frac{1}{\sqrt{(x-\frac12)^2-\frac14}} \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \left.\arcsin\frac{x-\frac12}{\frac12}\right|_{\frac12}^{1}
+ \left.\ln\left|x-\frac12+\sqrt{(x-\frac12)^2-\frac14}\right|\right|_{1}^{\frac32} \\
&= \left(\frac{\pi}{2}-0\right) + \left(\ln\frac{2+\sqrt{3}}{2}-\ln\frac12\right) \\
&= \frac{\pi}{2} + \ln(2+\sqrt{3})
\end{aligned}
$$
