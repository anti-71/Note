> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi} \left(\mathrm{e}^{2\cos x} - \mathrm{e}^{-2\cos x}\right)\mathrm{d}x$

**区间再现公式**：$\displaystyle\int_{a}^{b} f(x)\mathrm{d}x = \int_{a}^{b} f(a+b-x)\mathrm{d}x$

令 $x \to \pi - x$：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int_{0}^{\pi} \left(\mathrm{e}^{2\cos(\pi-x)} - \mathrm{e}^{-2\cos(\pi-x)}\right)\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int_{0}^{\pi} \left(\mathrm{e}^{-2\cos x} - \mathrm{e}^{2\cos x}\right)\mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\int_{0}^{\pi} \left(\mathrm{e}^{2\cos x} - \mathrm{e}^{-2\cos x}\right)\mathrm{d}x = -(\text{原式})
\end{aligned}
$$

故原式 $= 0$
