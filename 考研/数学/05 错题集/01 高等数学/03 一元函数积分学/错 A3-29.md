> $\displaystyle\int \frac{2\sin x+3\cos x}{5\sin x+7\cos x}\,\mathrm{d}x$

设 $2\sin x+3\cos x = A(5\sin x+7\cos x)' + B(5\sin x+7\cos x)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= (5A+7B)\cos x + (5B-7A)\sin x
\end{aligned}
$$

联立方程组：

$$
\begin{cases}
5B-7A=2 \\
5A+7B=3
\end{cases}
\implies
\begin{cases}
A=\displaystyle\frac{1}{74} \\
B=\displaystyle\frac{31}{74}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\therefore \text{原式} &= \frac{1}{74}\int \frac{1}{5\sin x+7\cos x}\,\mathrm{d}(5\sin x+7\cos x) + \frac{31}{74}\int \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac{31}{74}x + \frac{1}{74}\ln|5\sin x+7\cos x| + C
\end{aligned}
$$
