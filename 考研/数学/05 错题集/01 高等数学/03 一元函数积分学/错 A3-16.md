> $\displaystyle\int \frac{\ln(\sin x)}{\sin^2 x} \mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \ln(\sin x) \csc^2 x \,\mathrm{d}x = -\int \ln(\sin x) \,\mathrm{d}(\cot x) \\
&= -\cot x \ln(\sin x) + \int \cot x \cdot \frac{\cos x}{\sin x} \,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\cot x \ln(\sin x) + \int \cot^2 x \,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\cot x \ln(\sin x) + \int (\csc^2 x - 1) \,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\cot x \ln(\sin x) - \cot x - x + C
\end{aligned}
$$
