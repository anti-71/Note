> $\displaystyle\int x\ln(1+x^2)\arctan x \,\mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \frac12 \int \ln(1+x^2)\arctan x \,\mathrm{d}(1+x^2)
\end{aligned}
$$

先算辅助积分：$\displaystyle\int \ln(1+x^2)\,\mathrm{d}(1+x^2) \xlongequal{1+x^2=u} \int \ln u\,\mathrm{d}u = u\ln u - u = (1+x^2)\ln(1+x^2) - (1+x^2)$

分部积分

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \frac12 \int \arctan x \,\mathrm{d}\big[(1+x^2)\ln(1+x^2) - (1+x^2)\big] \\
&= \frac12\Big\{(1+x^2)\ln(1+x^2)\arctan x - (1+x^2)\arctan x - \int\big[\ln(1+x^2)-1\big]\mathrm{d}x\Big\}
\end{aligned}
$$

记 $I = \displaystyle\int\big[\ln(1+x^2)-1\big]\mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= -x\ln(1+x^2) + 2\int \frac{x^2+1-1}{1+x^2}\,\mathrm{d}x + x \\
&= -x\ln(1+x^2) + 3x - 2\arctan x
\end{aligned}
$$

代入得

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \frac12\Big[(1+x^2)\ln(1+x^2)\arctan x - (3+x^2)\arctan x \\
&\qquad\qquad - x\ln(1+x^2) + 3x\Big] + C
\end{aligned}
$$
