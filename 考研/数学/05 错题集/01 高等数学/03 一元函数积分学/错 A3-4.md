> [!question]
>
> 曲线 $y = \cos x\ (x \in [0,\dfrac{\pi}{2}])$ 与 $x$ 轴、$y$ 轴所围面积被曲线 $y = a\sin x$ 等分，则 $a =$
>
> （A）$\dfrac{2}{5}$
>
> （B）$\dfrac{3}{5}$
>
> （C）$\dfrac{3}{4}$
>
> （D）$\dfrac{1}{2}$

画出图像：

![](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/493e0fcbc343420dbb852781849c6743.png)

总面积 $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2} \cos x\,dx = 1$，设交点 $(x_0, y_0)$

由 $\cos x_0 = a\sin x_0 \implies \tan x_0 = \dfrac{1}{a}$

根据等分条件，上方面积 $= \dfrac{1}{2}$：

$$
\int_0^{x_0} (\cos x - a\sin x)dx = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\sin x_0 + a\cos x_0 - a = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\frac{1}{\sqrt{a^2 + 1}} + \frac{a^2}{\sqrt{a^2 + 1}} - a = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\sqrt{a^2 + 1} = \frac{1}{2} + a
$$

平方得 $a^2 + 1 = a^2 + a + \dfrac{1}{4} \implies a = \dfrac{3}{4}$，选 C
