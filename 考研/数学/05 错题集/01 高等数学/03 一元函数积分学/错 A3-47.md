> [!question]
>
> $$
> \int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{\arctan x}{(1+x)^3} \,\mathrm{d}x
> $$

利用[[定积分-分部积分法|分部积分]]，$\dfrac{\mathrm{d}x}{(1+x)^3} = -\dfrac12\,\mathrm{d}\dfrac1{(1+x)^2}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= -\frac12 \int_{0}^{+\infty} \arctan x \;\mathrm{d}\frac1{(1+x)^2} \\[4pt]
  &= -\frac12 \left.\frac{\arctan x}{(1+x)^2}\right|_{0}^{+\infty}
     + \frac12 \int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{1}{(1+x)^2(1+x^2)}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
  &= \frac12 \int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{1}{(1+x)^2(1+x^2)}\,\mathrm{d}x    
\end{aligned}
$$

倒代换，令 $x = \dfrac1t$，$\mathrm{d}x = -\dfrac1{t^2}\mathrm{d}t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
J &= \frac12 \int_{+\infty}^{0}
     \frac{1}{\bigl(1+\dfrac1t\bigr)^2\bigl(1+\dfrac1{t^2}\bigr)}
     \left(-\frac1{t^2}\right)\mathrm{d}t \\[4pt]
  &= \frac12 \int_{0}^{+\infty}
     \frac{t^2}{(t+1)^2(t^2+1)}\,\mathrm{d}t
     = \frac12 \int_{0}^{+\infty}
     \frac{x^2}{(x+1)^2(x^2+1)}\,\mathrm{d}x
\end{aligned}
$$

将 I 与 J 相加

$$
\begin{aligned}
2I &= \frac12 \int_{0}^{+\infty}
     \frac{1+x^2}{(x+1)^2(x^2+1)}\,\mathrm{d}x \\[4pt]
   &= \frac12 \int_{0}^{+\infty}
     \frac{1}{(x+1)^2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\[4pt]
   &= \frac12 \left[-\frac{1}{x+1}\right]_{0}^{+\infty}
     = \frac12 (0 + 1) = \frac12
\end{aligned}
$$

故 $\displaystyle I = \boxed{\frac14}$
