> $\displaystyle\int e^{2x}(\tan x+1)^2 \,\mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int e^{2x}(\tan^2x + 2\tan x + 1)\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int e^{2x}(\sec^2x + 2\tan x)\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= e^{2x}\tan x + C
\end{aligned}
$$
