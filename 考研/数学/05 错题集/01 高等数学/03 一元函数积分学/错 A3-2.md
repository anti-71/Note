> [!question]
>
> 设 $f(x)$ 连续，且 $\lim\limits_{x \to 0} \dfrac{f(x)}{x} = 2$，$\psi(x) = \displaystyle\int_0^1 f(xt)dt$，则 $\psi'(x) = \underline{\qquad}$

由 $\lim\limits_{x \to 0} \dfrac{f(x)}{x} = 2$ 可知 $f(0) = 0$，$f'(0) = 2$

令 $u = xt$，则 $dt = \dfrac{du}{x}$

当 $x = 0$ 时，$\psi(0) = 0$

当 $x \ne 0$ 时，$\psi(x) = \dfrac{\displaystyle\int_0^x f(u)du}{x}$

由 [[变上限积分|变上限积分]] 求导公式：

$$
\psi'(x) = \dfrac{x f(x) - \displaystyle\int_0^x f(u)du}{x^2}
$$

当 $x = 0$ 时用定义

$$
\psi'(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\psi(x)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\displaystyle\int_0^x f(u)du}{x^2}
$$

使用[[利用洛必达法则|洛必达法则]]：

$$
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{2x} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2 = 1
$$

综上

$$
\psi'(x) =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x f(x) - \displaystyle\int_0^x f(u)du}{x^2}, & x \neq 0 \\\\
1, & x = 0
\end{cases}
$$
