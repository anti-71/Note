> $\displaystyle\int \frac{\sin x \cos x}{\sin x + \cos x}\,\mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{\frac12(\sin x+\cos x)^2-\frac12}{\sin x + \cos x}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\frac12\cos x + \frac12\sin x - \frac12\int \frac{1}{\sin x+\cos x}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\frac12\cos x + \frac12\sin x - \frac12\int \csc\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\,\mathrm{d}\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right) \\
&= -\frac12\cos x + \frac12\sin x - \frac12\ln\left| \csc\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+\cot\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right) \right|+C
\end{aligned}
$$
