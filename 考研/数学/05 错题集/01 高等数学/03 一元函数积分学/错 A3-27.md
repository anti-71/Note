> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{a^2\sin^2 x+b^2\cos^2 x}\,\mathrm{d}x$，其中 $a,b$ 是不全为 0 的非负数

分类讨论

1. $a=0,b>0$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \frac{1}{b^2}\int \frac{1}{\cos^2 x}\,\mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{b^2}\tan x + C
\end{aligned}
$$

2. $a>0,b=0$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \frac{1}{a^2}\int \frac{1}{\sin^2 x}\,\mathrm{d}x = -\frac{1}{a^2}\cot x + C
\end{aligned}
$$

3. $a>0,b>0$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{\sec^2 x}{a^2\tan^2 x+b^2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac{1}{b^2}\int \frac{1}{\left(\frac{a}{b}\tan x\right)^2+1}\,\mathrm{d}(\tan x) \\
&= \frac{1}{ab}\int \frac{1}{\left(\frac{a}{b}\tan x\right)^2+1}\,\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{a}{b}\tan x\right) \\
&= \frac{1}{ab}\arctan\left(\frac{a}{b}\tan x\right)+C
\end{aligned}
$$
