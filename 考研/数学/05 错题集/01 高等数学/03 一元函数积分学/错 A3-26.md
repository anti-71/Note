> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int x^2\sqrt{x^2+a^2}\,\mathrm{d}x$

**法一** 三角换元

令 $x=a\tan t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int a^2\tan^2 t \cdot a\sec t \cdot a\sec^2 t\,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= a^4\left( \int \sec^5 t\,\mathrm{d}t - \int \sec^3 t\,\mathrm{d}t \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

$\int \sec^n t\,\mathrm{d}t$ 递推公式：$\displaystyle\int \sec^n t\,\mathrm{d}t = \frac{1}{n-1}\sec^{n-2} t\tan t + \frac{n-2}{n-1}\int \sec^{n-2} t\,\mathrm{d}t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= a^4\left( \frac14\sec^3 t\tan t - \frac18\sec t\tan t - \frac18\int \sec t\,\mathrm{d}t \right) \\
&= \frac{a^4}{8}\left( 2\sec^3 t\tan t - \sec t\tan t - \ln|\sec t+\tan t| \right)+C \\
&\xlongequal{\text{代回}} \frac14 x(x^2+a^2)^{\frac32} - \frac{a^2}{8}x(x^2+a^2)^{\frac12} - \frac{a^4}{8}\ln\left|x+\sqrt{x^2+a^2}\right|+C
\end{aligned}
$$

**法二** 分部积分

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int x\ \mathrm{d}\left( \frac13(x^2+a^2)^{\frac32} \right) \\
&= \frac13 x(x^2+a^2)^{\frac32} - \frac13\int (x^2+a^2)\sqrt{x^2+a^2}\,\mathrm{d}x
\end{aligned}
$$

记 $A=\displaystyle\int x^2\sqrt{x^2+a^2}\,\mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{4}{3}A &= \frac13 x(x^2+a^2)^{\frac32} - \frac{a^2}{3}\int \sqrt{x^2+a^2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
A &= \frac14 x(x^2+a^2)^{\frac32} - \frac{a^2}{4}\int \sqrt{x^2+a^2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac14 x(x^2+a^2)^{\frac32} - \frac{a^2}{8}\left( x\sqrt{x^2+a^2} + \ln\left|x+\sqrt{x^2+a^2}\right| \right)+C
\end{aligned}
$$
