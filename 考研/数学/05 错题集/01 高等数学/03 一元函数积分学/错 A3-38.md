> $\displaystyle\int \left( \frac{f(x)}{f'(x)} - \frac{f^2(x) f''(x)}{\left[f'(x)\right]^3} \right) \mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{f(x)}{f'(x)} \left\{ 1 - \frac{f(x)\cdot f''(x)}{\left[f'(x)\right]^2} \right\} \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int \frac{f(x)}{f'(x)} \cdot \frac{\left[f'(x)\right]^2 - f(x)\cdot f''(x)}{\left[f'(x)\right]^2} \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int \frac{f(x)}{f'(x)} \,\mathrm{d}\left( \frac{f(x)}{f'(x)} \right) \\
&= \frac12 \left[ \frac{f(x)}{f'(x)} \right]^2 + C
\end{aligned}
$$
