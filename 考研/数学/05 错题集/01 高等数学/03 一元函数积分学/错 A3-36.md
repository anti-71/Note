> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int \sqrt{\frac{1-x}{1+x}}\cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x}$

**法一** 根式代换

令 $\sqrt{\dfrac{1-x}{1+x}} = u \implies x = \dfrac{1-u^2}{1+u^2} \implies \mathrm{d}x = \dfrac{-4u}{(1+u^2)^2}\,\mathrm{d}u$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int u\cdot \frac{1+u^2}{1-u^2}\cdot \frac{-4u}{(1+u^2)^2}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= \int \frac{4u^2}{(u^2-1)(u^2+1)}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= 2\int \frac{1}{u^2-1}\,\mathrm{d}u + 2\int \frac{1}{u^2+1}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= \ln\left|\frac{u-1}{u+1}\right| + 2\arctan u + C \\
&= \ln\left|\frac{\sqrt{1-x}-\sqrt{1+x}}{\sqrt{1-x}+\sqrt{1+x}}\right| + 2\arctan\sqrt{\frac{1-x}{1+x}} + C
\end{aligned}
$$

**法二** 有理化

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{1-x}{x\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int \frac{1}{x\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,\mathrm{d}x - \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \ln\left|\frac{1-\sqrt{1-x^2}}{x}\right| - \arcsin x + C
\end{aligned}
$$
