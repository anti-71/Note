> $\displaystyle\int \sqrt{1-\sin2x}\,\mathrm{d}x$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \sqrt{\sin^2x+\cos^2x-2\sin x\cos x}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int |\cos x-\sin x|\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \sqrt{2}\int \left|\cos\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right|\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \sqrt{2}\int \left|\cos\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right|\,\mathrm{d}\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right) \\
&\xlongequal{x+\frac{\pi}{4}=u} \sqrt{2}\int |\cos u|\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= \sqrt{2}\left\{\sin\left[(k+1)\pi+u\right]+2k+C_0\right\},\ u\in\left[\frac{\pi}{2}+k\pi,\frac{3}{2}\pi+k\pi\right) \\
&= \sqrt{2}\sin\left[(k+1)\pi+x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right]+2\sqrt{2}k+C,\ x\in\left[\frac{\pi}{4}+k\pi,\frac{5}{4}\pi+k\pi\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
