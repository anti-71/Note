> $\displaystyle\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{4-16x^2}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \frac14\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1-(2x)^2}
= \frac18\int \left( \frac{1}{1-2x} + \frac{1}{1+2x} \right)\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac18\left[ -\frac12\int \frac{1}{1-2x}\,\mathrm{d}(1-2x) + \frac12\int \frac{1}{1+2x}\,\mathrm{d}(1+2x) \right] \\
&= \frac1{16}\big( \ln|1+2x| - \ln|1-2x| \big) + C \\
&= \frac1{16}\ln\left| \frac{1+2x}{1-2x} \right| + C
\end{aligned}
$$
