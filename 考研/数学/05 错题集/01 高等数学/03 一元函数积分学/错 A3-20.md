> 16\. $\displaystyle\int \frac{x^2 \mathrm{e}^x}{(x+2)^2}\,\mathrm{d}x = \underline{\qquad}$

**法一** 直接凑 $\mathrm{d}\dfrac{1}{x+2}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= -\int x^2\mathrm{e}^x \,\mathrm{d}\frac{1}{x+2} \\
&= -\frac{x^2\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2} + \int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x(x^2+2x)}{x+2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\frac{x^2\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2} + \int x\mathrm{e}^x\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= -\frac{x^2\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2} + x\mathrm{e}^x - \mathrm{e}^x + C
\end{aligned}
$$

**法二** 凑配分子

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{[(x+2)(x-2)+4]\mathrm{e}^x}{(x+2)^2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int \frac{(x+2-4)\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2}\,\mathrm{d}x + 4\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{(x+2)^2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \mathrm{e}^x - 4\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2}\,\mathrm{d}x + 4\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{(x+2)^2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \mathrm{e}^x - 4\int \mathrm{e}^x \,\mathrm{d}\frac{1}{x+2} - 4\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \mathrm{e}^x - \frac{4\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2} + 4\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2}\,\mathrm{d}x - 4\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \mathrm{e}^x - \frac{4\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2} + C
\end{aligned}
$$

两种方法结果等价：$-\dfrac{x^2\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2} + x\mathrm{e}^x - \mathrm{e}^x = \mathrm{e}^x - \dfrac{4\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2} = \dfrac{(x-2)\mathrm{e}^x}{x+2}$
