>
> $\displaystyle\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1+\sin x}$

**法一** 有理化

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{1-\sin x}{(1+\sin x)(1-\sin x)}\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int \frac{1-\sin x}{\cos^2 x}\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int (1-\sin x)\mathrm{d}\tan x \\
&= \tan x - \tan x\cdot\sin x + \int \sin x\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \tan x - \frac{\sin^2 x}{\cos x} - \cos x + C \\
&= \tan x - \frac{1}{\cos x} + C
\end{aligned}
$$

**法二** 半角公式

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sin^2\frac{x}{2}+\cos^2\frac{x}{2}+2\sin\frac{x}{2}\cos\frac{x}{2}} \\
&= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\left(\sin\frac{x}{2}+\cos\frac{x}{2}\right)^2} \\
&= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{2\sin^2\left(\frac{x}{2}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)} \\
&= \int \csc^2\left(\frac{x}{2}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{x}{2}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right) \\
&= -\cot\left(\frac{x}{2}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+C
\end{aligned}
$$
