> 61\. 设平面域 $D$ 由曲线 $r = (1+\cos\theta)$ 所围成，试求
> （2）区域 $D$ 绕极轴旋转一周所得旋转体的体积

画出 $D$ 在极轴上的图像（心形线）

![](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/214edcd4972e48fb83c5f1f11d2454dd.png)

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= 2\pi\iint\limits_{D_1} y \mathrm{d}\sigma= 2\pi \int_{0}^{\pi} \mathrm{d}\theta \int_{0}^{1+\cos\theta} r\sin\theta \cdot r \mathrm{d}r\\
&= 2\pi\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin\theta \cdot \left.\frac{1}{3}r^3\right|_{0}^{1+\cos\theta} \mathrm{d}\theta\\
&= -\frac{2}{3}\pi \int_{0}^{\pi} (1+\cos\theta)^3 \mathrm{d}(\cos\theta+1)\\
&= -\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{\pi}{4} \left.(1+\cos\theta)^4\right|_{0}^{\pi}\\
&= -\frac{\pi}{6} \left[0-2^4\right]= \frac{8}{3}\pi
\end{aligned}
$$
