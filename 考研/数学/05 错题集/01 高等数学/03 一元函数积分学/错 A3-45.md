> $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{4} \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{4+x}+\sqrt{4-x}+4} = \underline{\qquad}$

令 $x = 2t$，$\mathrm{d}x = 2\mathrm{d}t$

$$
I = \int_{0}^{2} \frac{2\mathrm{d}t}{\sqrt{4+2t}+\sqrt{4-2t}+4}
= \int_{0}^{2} \frac{2\mathrm{d}t}{\sqrt{2}(\sqrt{2+t}+\sqrt{2-t})+4}
$$

令 $t = 2\sin\phi$，$\mathrm{d}t = 2\cos\phi \,\mathrm{d}\phi$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{2+t}+\sqrt{2-t}
&= \sqrt{2+2\sin\phi}+\sqrt{2-2\sin\phi} \\
&= \sqrt{2}\left(\sin\frac{\phi}{2}+\cos\frac{\phi}{2}\right)
   +\sqrt{2}\left(\cos\frac{\phi}{2}-\sin\frac{\phi}{2}\right) \\
&= 2\sqrt{2}\cos\frac{\phi}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{4\cos\phi}
        {4\cos\frac{\phi}{2} + 4} \,\mathrm{d}\phi
    = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos\phi}{\cos\frac{\phi}{2} + 1} \,\mathrm{d}\phi \\
  &= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{2\cos^{2}\frac{\phi}{2} - 1}{\cos\frac{\phi}{2} + 1} \,\mathrm{d}\phi
\end{aligned}
$$

令 $u = \dfrac{\phi}{2}$，$\mathrm{d}\phi = 2\mathrm{d}u$

$$
I = 2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{2\cos^{2}u - 1}{\cos u + 1} \,\mathrm{d}u
$$

将分子除以分母

$$
\frac{2\cos^{2}u - 1}{\cos u + 1} = 2\cos u - 2 + \frac{1}{\cos u + 1}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
I &= 2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \left(2\cos u - 2 + \frac{1}{\cos u + 1}\right) \mathrm{d}u \\
  &= 4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \cos u \,\mathrm{d}u
     - 4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \mathrm{d}u
     + 2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\mathrm{d}u}{\cos u + 1} \\
  &= 4\left.\sin u\right|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}
     - 4\cdot\frac{\pi}{4}
     + 2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{1}{2}\sec^{2}\frac{u}{2} \,\mathrm{d}u \\
  &= 2\sqrt{2} - \pi + \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec^{2}\frac{u}{2} \,\mathrm{d}u
\end{aligned}
$$

再令 $v = \dfrac{u}{2}$，$\mathrm{d}u = 2\mathrm{d}v$

$$
\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec^{2}\frac{u}{2} \,\mathrm{d}u
= 2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{8}} \sec^{2}v \,\mathrm{d}v
= 2\left.\tan v\right|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{8}}
= 2\tan\frac{\pi}{8} = 2(\sqrt{2} - 1)
$$

故

$$
I = 2\sqrt{2} - \pi + 2\sqrt{2} - 2 = \boxed{4\sqrt{2} - 2 - \pi}
$$
