> [!question]
>
> $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x(1+x)}}\,\mathrm{d}x$

**法一** 配方法

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+x+\frac14-\frac14}}\,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{\left(x+\frac12\right)^2-\frac14}}\,\mathrm{d}\left(x+\frac12\right) \\
&\xlongequal{x+\frac12=u} \ln\left|\sqrt{u^2-\frac14}+u\right|+C \\
&= \ln\left|\sqrt{x^2+x}+x+\frac12\right|+C
\end{aligned}
$$

**法二** 根式代换

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= 2\int \frac{\mathrm{d}\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt{(\sqrt{x})^2+1}} \\
&\xlongequal{\sqrt{x}=u} 2\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{u^2+1}}\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= 2\ln\left(\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt{x}\right)+C
\end{aligned}
$$
