> $\displaystyle\int x^3\sqrt{9-x^2}\,\mathrm{d}x$

令 $x=3\sin u$，则 $\mathrm{d}x=3\cos u\,\mathrm{d}u$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int 27\sin^3u \cdot 3\cos u \cdot 3\cos u\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= 243\int \cos^2u\sin^3u\,\mathrm{d}u \\
&= -243\int \cos^2u(1-\cos^2u)\,\mathrm{d}\cos u \\
&= \frac{243}{5}\cos^5u - \frac{243}{3}\cos^3u + C \\
&= \frac{1}{5}(9-x^2)^{\frac{5}{2}} - 3(9-x^2)^{\frac{3}{2}} + C
\end{aligned}
$$
