> [!question]
>
> 设 $f(x)$ 为非负连续函数，且
> $$
> f(x)\int_0^x f(x-t)dt = \sin^4 x
> $$
> 求 $f(x)$ 在 $\left[0,\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$ 上的平均值 $\overline{f(x)}$

令 $u = x - t$，则 $\displaystyle\int_0^x f(x-t)dt = \int_0^x f(u)du$

原式化为 $f(x)\displaystyle\int_0^x f(u)du = \sin^4 x$

直接求 $f(x)$ 解不出，改对两边在 $[0, \pi/2]$ 上积分：

$$
\int_0^{\pi/2} f(x)\int_0^x f(u)du \, dx = \int_0^{\pi/2} \sin^4 x \, dx = \frac{3}{16}\pi
$$

左边是 $y\cdot y'$ 形式（记 $y = \displaystyle\int_0^x f(u)du$）：

$$
= \int_0^{\pi/2} y \cdot y'\,dx = \int_0^{\pi/2} y\,dy = \frac{1}{2}y^2\Big|_0^{\pi/2}
= \frac{1}{2}\left(\int_0^{\pi/2} f(u)du\right)^2
$$

$$
\frac{1}{2}\left(\int_0^{\pi/2} f(u)du\right)^2 = \frac{3}{16}\pi
\implies \int_0^{\pi/2} f(u)du = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\frac{3}{2}\pi} \quad (f(x) \geqslant 0)
$$

$$
\overline{f(x)} = \frac{\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2} f(x)dx}{\pi/2}
= \frac{\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}\pi}}{\pi/2}
= \sqrt{\frac{3}{2\pi}}
$$
