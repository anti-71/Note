> $\displaystyle\int_{0}^{1} \frac{\arctan x}{(1+x^2)^2} \mathrm{d}x$

令 $x = \tan t$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{t}{\sec^4 t} \cdot \sec^2 t \,\mathrm{d}t
= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} t\cos^2 t \,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \frac12 \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} t(1+\cos 2t) \,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \frac{\pi^2}{64} + \frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} t\cos 2t \,\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \frac{\pi^2}{64} + \frac{1}{4}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} t\,\mathrm{d}(\sin 2t) \\
&= \frac{\pi^2}{64} + \frac{1}{4}\left( \left.t\sin 2t\right|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} - \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sin 2t \,\mathrm{d}t \right) \\
&= \frac{\pi^2}{64} + \frac{1}{4}\left( \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{1}{2}\left.\cos 2t\right|_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \right) \\
&= \frac{\pi^2}{64} + \frac{\pi}{16} - \frac{1}{8}
\end{aligned}
$$
