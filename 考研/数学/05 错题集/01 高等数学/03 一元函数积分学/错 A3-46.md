> [!question]
>
> 设 $\displaystyle f(x) = x^2\arcsin x - \int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x)\,\mathrm{d}x$，则 $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x)\,\mathrm{d}x = \underline{\qquad}$

令 $A = \displaystyle\int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x)\,\mathrm{d}x$（常数），则

$$
f(x) = x^2\arcsin x - A\implies f(\sin x) = \sin^2 x \cdot \arcsin(\sin x) - A
$$

两边在 $[0,\pi]$ 上积分

$$
A = \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin^2 x \cdot \arcsin(\sin x)\,\mathrm{d}x - A\cdot\pi
\;\Longrightarrow\;
A(1+\pi) = \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin^2 x \cdot \arcsin(\sin x)\,\mathrm{d}x
$$

由于 $\arcsin(\sin x)$ 是分段函数

$$
\arcsin(\sin x) =
\begin{cases}
x, & 0 \leqslant x \leqslant \dfrac{\pi}{2} \\[6pt]
\pi - x, & \dfrac{\pi}{2} < x \leqslant \pi
\end{cases}
$$

故

$$
A(1+\pi) = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x\sin^2 x\,\mathrm{d}x
     + \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} (\pi - x)\sin^2 x\,\mathrm{d}x
$$

对第二项令 $t = \pi - x$，$\mathrm{d}x = -\mathrm{d}t$

$$
\int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\pi} (\pi - x)\sin^2 x\,\mathrm{d}x
= \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{0} t\sin^2(\pi - t)(-\mathrm{d}t)
= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} t\sin^2 t\,\mathrm{d}t
$$

因此两项相等

$$
A(1+\pi) = 2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x\sin^2 x\,\mathrm{d}x
\;\Longrightarrow\;
A = \frac{2}{1+\pi}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x\sin^2 x\,\mathrm{d}x
$$

> [!note] 另解
> 也可以根据 $\sin x$ 关于 $x=\dfrac\pi2$ 对称得
> 
> $$\int_{0}^{\pi}f(\sin x)\mathrm{d}x=2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}f(\sin x)\mathrm{d}x$$

计算 $J = \displaystyle\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x\sin^2 x\,\mathrm{d}x$，利用 $\sin^2 x = \dfrac{1 - \cos 2x}{2}$ 及[[定积分-分部积分法|分部积分]]

$$
\begin{aligned}
J &= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x\cdot\frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2}\,\mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x\,\mathrm{d}x
     - \frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} x\cos 2x\,\mathrm{d}x \\[4pt]
  &= \frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{(\pi/2)^2}{2}
     - \frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{x}{2}\sin 2x + \frac{1}{4}\cos 2x\right]_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} = \frac{\pi^2}{16} - \frac{1}{2}\left[\left(0 - \frac{1}{4}\right) - \left(0 + \frac{1}{4}\right)\right] \\[4pt]
  &= \frac{\pi^2}{16} + \frac{1}{4}
\end{aligned}
$$

故

$$
\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\pi} f(\sin x)\,\mathrm{d}x
= A = \frac{2}{1+\pi}\cdot J
= \frac{2}{1+\pi}\cdot\frac{\pi^2+4}{16}
= \boxed{\frac{\pi^2+4}{8(1+\pi)}}
$$
