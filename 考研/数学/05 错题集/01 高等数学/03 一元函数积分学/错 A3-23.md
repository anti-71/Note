> $\displaystyle\int \sqrt{\dfrac{1-x}{1+x}}\,\mathrm{d}x$

原式 = $\displaystyle\int \frac{|1-x|}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\mathrm{d}x$

$$
\because \frac{1-x}{1+x} \geqslant 0 \implies -1 < x \leqslant 1
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \int \frac{1-x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\mathrm{d}x + \frac12\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\mathrm{d}(1-x^2) \\
&= \arcsin x + \sqrt{1-x^2} + C
\end{aligned}
$$
