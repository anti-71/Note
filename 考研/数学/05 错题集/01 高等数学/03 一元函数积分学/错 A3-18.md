> $\displaystyle\int \arctan(x^2) \,\mathrm{d}x$

分部积分

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= x\arctan x^2 - \int \frac{2x^2}{1+x^4} \,\mathrm{d}x \\
&= x\arctan x^2 - \int \frac{x^2+1}{1+x^4}\,\mathrm{d}x - \int \frac{x^2-1}{1+x^4}\,\mathrm{d}x
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \frac{x^2+1}{1+x^4}\,\mathrm{d}x
&= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}\left(x-\frac1x\right)}{\left(x-\frac1x\right)^2+2}
= \frac1{\sqrt{2}}\arctan\frac{x-\frac1x}{\sqrt{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \frac{x^2-1}{1+x^4}\,\mathrm{d}x
&= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}\left(x+\frac1x\right)}{\left(x+\frac1x\right)^2-2}
= \frac1{2\sqrt{2}}\ln\left|\frac{x+\frac1x-\sqrt{2}}{x+\frac1x+\sqrt{2}}\right|
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore\ \text{原式} = x\arctan x^2 - \frac1{\sqrt{2}}\arctan\frac{x-\frac1x}{\sqrt{2}}
- \frac1{2\sqrt{2}}\ln\left|\frac{x+\frac1x-\sqrt{2}}{x+\frac1x+\sqrt{2}}\right| + C
$$
