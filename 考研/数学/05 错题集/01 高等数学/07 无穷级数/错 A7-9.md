> 求级数 $\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(n^2-1)2^n}$ 的和

设 $S(x)=\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n^2-1}
      =\frac12\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\left(\frac1{n-1}-\frac1{n+1}\right)x^n,\ |x|<1$

$\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac1{n-1}x^n
 =x\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac1{n-1}x^{n-1}
 =x\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1n x^n
 =xS_1(x)$

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_1'(x) &= \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^{n-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n=\frac1{1-x} \\[4pt]
\int_{0}^{x}S_1'(t)\,\mathrm{d}t &= S_1(x)-S_1(0)=S_1(x)
      =\int_{0}^{x}\frac1{1-t}\,\mathrm{d}t
      =-\ln(1-t)\Big|_{0}^{x}=-\ln(1-x)
\end{aligned}
$$

当 $x\neq0$，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac1{n+1}x^n
&=\frac1x\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\frac1{n+1}x^{n+1}
 =\frac1x\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1n x^n-x-\frac12x^2\right) \\[4pt]
&=\frac1x\left[-\ln(1-x)-x-\frac12x^2\right]
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore S(x)=-\frac12x\ln(1-x)+\frac1{2x}\ln(1-x)+\frac12+\frac14x
$$

原级数 $=S\left(\dfrac12\right)
         =\dfrac14\ln2-\ln2+\dfrac12+\dfrac18
         =\dfrac58-\dfrac34\ln2$
