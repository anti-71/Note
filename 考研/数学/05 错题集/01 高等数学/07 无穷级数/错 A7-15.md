> 将函数 $f(x)=x-1\ (0\leqslant x\leqslant 2)$ 展开成周期为 $4$ 的余弦级数

将 $f(x)$ 偶延拓，再作 $T=4$ 的周期延拓

$$
f(x)=\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n\cos\frac{n\pi}{2}x
$$

$$
a_0=\frac{2}{2}\int_{0}^{2}f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x
   =\left.\frac12(x-1)^2\right|_{0}^{2}=0
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_n &=\frac{2}{2}\int_{0}^{2}f(x)\cos\frac{n\pi}{2}x\,\mathrm{d}x
      =\frac{2}{n\pi}\int_{0}^{2}(x-1)\,\mathrm{d}\!\left(\sin\frac{n\pi}{2}x\right) \\[4pt]
    &=\left.\frac{2}{n\pi}x\sin\frac{n\pi}{2}x\right|_{0}^{2}
      +\frac{4}{n^2\pi^2}\int_{0}^{2}\mathrm{d}\!\left(\cos\frac{n\pi}{2}x\right)
      -\left.\frac{2}{n\pi}\sin\frac{n\pi}{2}x\right|_{0}^{2} \\[4pt]
    &=\left.\frac{4}{n^2\pi^2}\cos\frac{n\pi}{2}x\right|_{0}^{2} \\[4pt]
    &=\frac{4}{n^2\pi^2}\bigl[(-1)^n-1\bigr]
      =\begin{cases}
        \displaystyle\frac{-8}{(2k-1)^2\pi^2}, & n=2k-1 \\[8pt]
        0, & n=2k
        \end{cases},\quad k=1,2,3,\dots
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore f(x)=-\frac{8}{\pi^2}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(2n-1)^2}\cos\frac{(2n-1)\pi}{2}x
$$
