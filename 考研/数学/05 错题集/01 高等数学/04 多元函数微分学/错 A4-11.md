> [!question]
> 设 $f(x,y)$ 二阶偏导连续，且 $f''_{xx}(x,y)=f''_{yy}(x,y)$，$f(x,2x)=x^2$，$f'_x(x,2x)=x$，求 $f''_{xx}(x,2x)$ 和 $f''_{xy}(x,2x)$

记 $f_1 = f'_x$，$f_2 = f'_y$，$f_{11}=f''_{xx}$，$f_{12}=f''_{xy}$，$f_{22}=f''_{yy}$

由 $f(x,2x)=x^2$ 两边对 $x$ 求导

$$
f_1(x,2x)\cdot 1 + f_2(x,2x)\cdot 2 = 2x \tag{1}
$$

已知 $f_1(x,2x)=x$，代入 1 式

$$
x + 2f_2(x,2x) = 2x \;\Longrightarrow\; f_2(x,2x) = \frac{x}{2}
$$

对 1 式两边再次对 $x$ 求导

$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigl[f_1(x,2x)\bigr] + 2\,\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigl[f_2(x,2x)\bigr] = 2
$$

其中

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigl[f_1(x,2x)\bigr] &= f_{11}(x,2x)\cdot1 + f_{12}(x,2x)\cdot2 \\[4pt]
\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\bigl[f_2(x,2x)\bigr] &= f_{21}(x,2x)\cdot1 + f_{22}(x,2x)\cdot2
\end{aligned}
$$

由二阶偏导连续知 $f_{12}=f_{21}$，又已知 $f_{11}=f_{22}$，代入得

$$
\bigl(f_{11}+2f_{12}\bigr) + 2\bigl(f_{12}+2f_{11}\bigr) = 2
\;\Longrightarrow\; 5f_{11} + 4f_{12} = 2 \tag{2}
$$

再由 $f_1(x,2x)=x$ 两边对 $x$ 求导

$$
f_{11}(x,2x) + 2f_{12}(x,2x) = 1 \tag{3}
$$

联立 2、3 式

$$
\begin{cases}
5f_{11} + 4f_{12} = 2 \\[4pt]
f_{11} + 2f_{12} = 1
\end{cases}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\begin{cases}
f_{11} = 0 \\[4pt]
f_{12} = \dfrac12
\end{cases}
$$

故 $f''_{xx}(x,2x)=\boxed{0}$，$f''_{xy}(x,2x)=\boxed{\frac12}$
