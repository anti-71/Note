> 方程 $\left(1+\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x}{y}}\right)y\mathrm{d}x+(y-x)\mathrm{d}y=0$ 的通解为 $\underline{\qquad}$

出现 $\dfrac{x}{y}$，往这个形式上靠

$$
\left(1+\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x}{y}}\right)\mathrm{d}x+\left(1-\frac{x}{y}\right)\mathrm{d}y=0
$$

令 $p=\dfrac{x}{y} \implies \mathrm{d}x=y\mathrm{d}p+p\mathrm{d}y$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(1+\mathrm{e}^{-p}\right)(y\mathrm{d}p+p\mathrm{d}y)+(1-p)\mathrm{d}y &= 0 \\
\left(1+\mathrm{e}^{-p}\right)y\mathrm{d}p+\left(\mathrm{e}^{-p}+1\right)\mathrm{d}y &= 0 \\
y'+\frac{\mathrm{e}^{p}+1}{\mathrm{e}^{p}+p}y &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

根据公式：

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= C\mathrm{e}^{\displaystyle-\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^{p}+1}{\mathrm{e}^{p}+p}\,\mathrm{d}p} \\
&= C\mathrm{e}^{\displaystyle-\int \frac{1}{\mathrm{e}^{p}+p}\,\mathrm{d}(\mathrm{e}^{p}+p)} \\
&= \frac{C}{\mathrm{e}^{p}+p}
\end{aligned}
$$

代回 $p$：$y\mathrm{e}^{\frac{x}{y}}+x=C$
