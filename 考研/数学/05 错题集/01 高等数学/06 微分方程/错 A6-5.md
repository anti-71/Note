> 微分方程 $yy'' + y'^{\,2} = 0$ 满足初始条件 $y|_{x=0}=1$，$y'|_{x=0}=\dfrac12$ 的特解是 $\underline{\qquad}$

方程不显含 $x$，令 $y' = P$，则 $y'' = P\dfrac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}y}$，代入方程

$$
yP\frac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}y} + P^2 = 0
$$

分离变量

$$
\frac{\mathrm{d}P}{P} = -\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{y}
$$

两边积分

$$
\ln P = -\ln y + C \implies P = \frac{C_1}{y}
$$

代入初始条件 $y'(0)=\dfrac12$，$y(0)=1$

$$
\frac12 = C_1 \cdot \frac{1}{1} \implies C_1 = \frac12
$$

$$
\therefore \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \frac{1}{2y} \implies 2y\,\mathrm{d}y = \mathrm{d}x
$$

积分得

$$
y^2 = x + C_2
$$

代入 $y(0)=1 \implies C_2 = 1$

$$
\therefore y^2 = x + 1
$$

由 $y(0)=1>0$，故

$$
y = \sqrt{x+1}
$$
