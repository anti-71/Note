> [!question]
>
> 设 $f(x)$ 为连续函数，且满足
> $$
> f(x) = \mathrm{e}^x + \mathrm{e}^x \int_{0}^{x} [f(t)]^2 \,\mathrm{d}t
> $$
> 试求 $f(x)$

两边求导：

$$
f'(x) = \mathrm{e}^x + \mathrm{e}^x \int_{0}^{x} [f(t)]^2 \,\mathrm{d}t + \mathrm{e}^x [f(x)]^2
$$

代入原方程：

$$
f'(x) = f(x) + \mathrm{e}^x [f(x)]^2
$$

变形：

$$
\frac{y'}{y^2} - \frac{1}{y} = \mathrm{e}^x
$$

令 $\displaystyle\frac{1}{y} = u$，则 $u' = -\dfrac{y'}{y^2}$

$$
u' + u = -\mathrm{e}^x
$$

一阶线性微分方程通解：

$$
\begin{aligned}
u &= \mathrm{e}^{-x}\left(-\int \mathrm{e}^x \cdot \mathrm{e}^{x}\,\mathrm{d}x + C\right) \\
&= \mathrm{e}^{-x}\left(-\frac12\mathrm{e}^{2x} + C\right) \\
&= -\frac12\mathrm{e}^x + C\mathrm{e}^{-x}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\therefore f(x) = \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{-\dfrac12\mathrm{e}^x + C\mathrm{e}^{-x}}
$$
