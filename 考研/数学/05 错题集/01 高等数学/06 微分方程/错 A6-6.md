> [!question]
>
> 设 $f(x)=\sin x - \displaystyle\int_{0}^{x}(x-t)f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t$，其中 $f(x)$ 为连续函数，求 $f(x)$

$$f(x)=\sin x - x\int_{0}^{x}f(t)\mathrm{d}t + \int_{0}^{x}tf(t)\mathrm{d}t$$

$$
\begin{align*}
f'(x)&=\cos x - \int_{0}^{x}f(t)\mathrm{d}t - xf(x) + xf(x) \\
&= \cos x - \int_{0}^{x}f(t)\mathrm{d}t
\end{align*}
$$

$$f''(x)=-\sin x - f(x) \implies f''(x)+f(x)=-\sin x$$

对于$y''+y=0$的通解：
$$r^2+1=0 \implies r=\pm i \ (\alpha=0,\beta=1)$$
$$y=C_1\cos x + C_2\sin x$$

设特解$y^*=x(A\cos x+B\sin x)$

$$(y^*)'= A\cos x+B\sin x + x(-A\sin x+B\cos x)$$

$$
\begin{align*}
(y^*)''&= -A\sin x+B\cos x -A\sin x+B\cos x - x(A\cos x+B\sin x) \\
&= -2A\sin x + 2B\cos x - x(A\cos x+B\sin x)
\end{align*}
$$

代入方程，$-2A\sin x + 2B\cos x = -\sin x$

$$
\begin{cases}
-2A=-1 \\\\
2B=0
\end{cases}
\implies
\begin{cases}
A=\dfrac{1}{2} \\\\
B=0
\end{cases}
$$
所以 $y^*=\dfrac{1}{2}x\cos x$

$$\therefore f(x)=C_1\cos x + C_2\sin x + \frac{1}{2}x\cos x$$
