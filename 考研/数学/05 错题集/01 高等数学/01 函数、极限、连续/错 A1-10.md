> [!question] 
> 设 $f''(a)$ 存在，$f'(a) \neq 0$，求
> $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} \left[ \dfrac{1}{f'(a)(x - a)} - \dfrac{1}{f(x) - f(a)} \right]$

通分：
$$
\text{原式} = \dfrac{1}{f'(a)} \lim_{x \to a} \dfrac{f(x) - f(a) - f'(a)(x - a)}{(x - a)[f(x) - f(a)]}
$$

$\dfrac{0}{0}$ 型，两次[[利用洛必达法则|洛必达]]：
$$
= \dfrac{1}{f'(a)} \lim_{x \to a} \dfrac{f'(x)}{f'(x) + f'(x) + (x - a)f''(x)}
= \dfrac{1}{f'(a)} \cdot \dfrac{f'(a)}{2f'(a)}
= \dfrac{f''(a)}{2[f'(a)]^2}
$$
