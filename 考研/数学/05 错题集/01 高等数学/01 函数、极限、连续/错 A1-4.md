> [!question] 
> $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{\sqrt{1+x} + \sqrt{1-x} - 2}{x^2} =$

**法一 [[利用泰勒公式|泰勒展开]]**

$$
(1+x)^{\dfrac{1}{2}} = 1 + \dfrac{1}{2}x + \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}(-\dfrac{1}{2})}{2}x^2 + O(x^3)
$$

$$
(1-x)^{\dfrac{1}{2}} = 1 - \dfrac{1}{2}x + \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}(-\dfrac{1}{2})}{2}x^2 + O(x^3)
$$

代入原式：
$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{-\dfrac{1}{4}x^2 + O(x^3)}{x^2} = -\dfrac{1}{4}
$$

**法二 洛必达 + 等价无穷小**

洛必达一次：
$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{1+x}} - \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{1-x}}}{2x}
= \dfrac{1}{4} \lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{\sqrt{1-x} - \sqrt{1+x}}{x}
$$

加一项减一项，用等价无穷小 $\sqrt{1+u} - 1 \sim \dfrac{1}{2}u$：
$$
= \dfrac{1}{4} \lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{(\sqrt{1-x} - 1) - (\sqrt{1+x} - 1)}{x}
= \dfrac{1}{4} \lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{-\dfrac{1}{2}x - \dfrac{1}{2}x}{x}
= -\dfrac{1}{4}
$$
