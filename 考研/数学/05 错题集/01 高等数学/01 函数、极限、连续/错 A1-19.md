> [!question] 
> 已知函数 $f(x)$ 在 $x = 0$ 的某邻域内可导，且
> $$
> \lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{\sin x}{x^2} + \frac{f(x)}{x} \right) = 2
> $$
> 试求 $f(0)$，$f'(0)$ 及 $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{f(x) + e^x}$

**法一（极限消去法）**

由题设乘以 $x$：
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{\sin x}{x} + f(x) \right) = 0 \implies 1 + f(0) = 0
$$
故 $f(0) = -1$

回代原式：
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x + f(x)x}{x^2}
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x[f(x) - f(0)] + \sin x - x}{x^2}
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x} + 0 = f'(0) = 2
$$

计算所求极限：
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{f(x) + e^x}
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{\dfrac{f(x) + 1}{x} + \dfrac{e^x - 1}{x}}
= \frac{1}{f'(0) + 1} = \frac{1}{3}
$$

**法二（泰勒展开）**

由题设：
$$
\frac{\sin x}{x} + f(x) = 2x + o(x)
$$
$$
\implies f(x) = 2x - \frac{\sin x}{x} + o(x)
= 2x - \frac{x - \frac{1}{6}x^3 + o(x^3)}{x} + o(x)
= 2x - 1 + o(x)
$$

故 $f(0) = -1$，$f'(0) = 2$

$$
\therefore \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{f(x) + e^x}
= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{2x + e^x - 1 + o(x)}
= \frac{1}{2 + 1} = \frac{1}{3}
$$

答案：$\boxed{f(0) = -1,\; f'(0) = 2,\; \displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{f(x) + e^x} = \frac{1}{3}}$
