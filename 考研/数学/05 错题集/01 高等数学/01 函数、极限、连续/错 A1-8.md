> [!question] 
> 设 $n$ 为正整数，求 $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to \infty} \left[ \dfrac{x^n}{(x-1)(x-2)\cdots(x-n)} \right]^x$

取自然对数后指数化：
$$
\text{原式} = \exp\left( \lim_{x \to \infty} x \left[ n\ln x - \sum_{i=1}^{n} \ln(x-i) \right] \right)
$$

$$
= \exp\left( \lim_{x \to \infty} x \sum_{i=1}^{n} \ln\dfrac{1}{1 - \frac{i}{x}} \right)
= \exp\left( \lim_{x \to \infty} x \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left[ -\ln\left(1 - \dfrac{i}{x}\right) \right] \right)
$$

令 $t = \dfrac{1}{x}$，[[利用泰勒公式|泰勒展开]] $-\ln(1 - it) = it + \dfrac{(it)^2}{2} + \cdots$：
$$
= \exp\left( \lim_{t \to 0^+} \dfrac{1}{t} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( it + O(t^2) \right) \right)
= \exp\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} i \right)
= e^{\frac{n(n+1)}{2}}
$$
