> [!question] 
> $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \left[ \sqrt{1+2+\cdots+n} - \sqrt{1+2+\cdots+(n-1)} \right]$

利用等差数列求和公式 $1+2+\cdots+n = \dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$：
$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \sqrt{\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}} - \sqrt{\dfrac{(n-1)n}{2}} \right)
= \lim_{n \to \infty} \sqrt{\dfrac{n}{2}} \left( \sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n-1} \right)
$$

分子有理化：
$$
= \lim_{n \to \infty} \sqrt{\dfrac{n}{2}} \cdot \dfrac{(n+1) - (n-1)}{\sqrt{n+1} + \sqrt{n-1}}
= \lim_{n \to \infty} \sqrt{\dfrac{n}{2}} \cdot \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{n}\left( \sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n}} + \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{n}} \right)}
$$

$$
= 2 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2}
= \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
$$
