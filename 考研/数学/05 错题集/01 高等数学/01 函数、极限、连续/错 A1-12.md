> [!question] 
> $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} \dfrac{\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt{k}}{\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt{n+k}}$

分子分母各提 $\sqrt{n}$：
$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{n \to \infty} \dfrac{\dfrac{1}{n} \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt{\dfrac{k}{n}}}{\dfrac{1}{n} \displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n} \sqrt{1 + \dfrac{k}{n}}}
$$

化为[[定积分的概念|定积分]]形式：
$$
= \dfrac{\displaystyle\int_0^1 \sqrt{x} \, dx}{\displaystyle\int_0^1 \sqrt{1+x} \, dx}
$$

计算积分：
$$
\int_0^1 \sqrt{x} \, dx = \dfrac{2}{3},\qquad
\int_0^1 \sqrt{1+x} \, dx = \dfrac{2}{3}(2\sqrt{2} - 1)
$$

故：
$$
\text{原式} = \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{2} - 1}
= \dfrac{2\sqrt{2} + 1}{7}
$$
