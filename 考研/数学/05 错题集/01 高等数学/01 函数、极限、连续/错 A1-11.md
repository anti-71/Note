> [!question] 
> 设函数 $y(x)$ 满足 $y'' + (x - 1)y' + x^2 y = e^x$，且 $y'(0) = 1$
> 若 $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{y(x) - x}{x^2} = a$，则 $a =$

解不出微分方程，从极限入手：
$$
a = \lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{y(x) - x}{x^2}
= \dfrac{1}{2} \lim_{x \to 0} \dfrac{y'(x) - 1}{x}
= \dfrac{1}{2} \lim_{x \to 0} y''(x)
= \dfrac{1}{2} y''(0)
$$

代入 $x = 0$ 到原方程：
$$
y''(0) + (0 - 1)y'(0) + 0^2 \cdot y(0) = e^0
\implies y''(0) - 1 = 1
\implies y''(0) = 2
$$

故 $a = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 2 = 1$
