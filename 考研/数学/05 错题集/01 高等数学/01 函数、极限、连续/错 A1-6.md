> [!question] 
> $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} n \left[ e - \left(1 + \dfrac{1}{2n}\right)^{2n} \right]$

**法一 [[利用等价无穷小代换|等价无穷小]]**

化为幂指形式后取对数：
$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{n \to \infty} n \left[ e - e^{2n \ln(1 + \frac{1}{2n})} \right]
= e \lim_{n \to \infty} n \left[ 1 - 2n \ln\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{2n}\right) \right]
$$

提 $2n$，凑等价无穷小 $\ln(1+u) \sim u - \dfrac{u^2}{2}$：
$$
= e \lim_{n \to \infty} 2n^2 \left[ \dfrac{1}{2n} - \ln\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{2n}\right) \right]
= e \lim_{n \to \infty} 2n^2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \left(\dfrac{1}{2n}\right)^2
= \dfrac{e}{4}
$$

**法二 [[利用洛必达法则|洛必达]]**

令 $\dfrac{1}{2x} = t$，则 $n \to \infty$ 时 $t \to 0$：
$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{e - (1+t)^{\frac{1}{t}}}{2t}
= \lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{e - e^{\frac{\ln(1+t)}{t}}}{2t}
$$

两次洛必达：
$$
\overset{L'H}{=} -\frac{e}{2} \lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{t - (t+1)\ln(1+t)}{t^2(t+1)}
\overset{L'H}{=} -\frac{e}{4} \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{1 - \ln(1+t) - 1}{t}
= -\frac{e}{4} \times (-1) = \dfrac{e}{4}
$$

**法三 [[拉格朗日中值定理]]**

$$
\text{原式} = \lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{e - e^{\frac{2\ln(1+\frac{1}{2}t)}{t}}}{t}
= \lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{e^{\xi} \left[1 - \dfrac{2\ln(1+\frac{1}{2}t)}{t}\right]}{t}
\quad (1 < \xi < \tfrac{2\ln(1+\frac{1}{2}t)}{t})
$$

由于 $\displaystyle\lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{2\ln(1+\frac{1}{2}t)}{t} = 1$，故 $\xi \to 1$：
$$
= e \lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{t - 2\ln(1+\frac{1}{2}t)}{t^2}
= 2e \lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{\frac{1}{2}t - \ln(1+\frac{1}{2}t)}{t^2}
$$

等价无穷小代换 $\ln(1+u) \sim u - \dfrac{u^2}{2}$：
$$
= 2e \lim_{t \to 0} \dfrac{\frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{1}{2}t\right)^2}{t^2}
= \dfrac{e}{4}
$$
