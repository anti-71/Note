> [!question] 
> 设数列 $\{x_n\}$ 满足 $x_1 = 1$，$x_{n+1} = \dfrac{x_n + 2}{x_n + 1}$（$n = 1,2,\cdots$），试证 $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = \sqrt{2}$

利用压缩映像估计：

$$
|x_{n+1} - \sqrt{2}|
= \left| \frac{x_n + 2}{x_n + 1} - \sqrt{2} \right|
= \frac{1}{x_n + 1} \left| x_n + 2 - \sqrt{2}(x_n + 1) \right|
$$

$$
= \frac{1}{(x_n + 1)(\sqrt{2} + 1)} \left| x_n - \sqrt{2} \right|
\leqslant \frac{1}{2} \left| x_n - \sqrt{2} \right|
$$

递推得：
$$
|x_{n+1} - \sqrt{2}| \leqslant \frac{1}{2^n} |x_1 - \sqrt{2}|
$$

由夹逼原理，$\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty} |x_n - \sqrt{2}| = 0$

故 $\displaystyle\boxed{\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = \sqrt{2}}$
