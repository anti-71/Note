>
> 设 $a,b,c \neq \boldsymbol{0}$，若 $\boldsymbol{a} = \boldsymbol{b} \times \boldsymbol{c}$，$\boldsymbol{b} = \boldsymbol{c} \times \boldsymbol{a}$，$\boldsymbol{c} = \boldsymbol{a} \times \boldsymbol{b}$，则 $|\boldsymbol{a}|+|\boldsymbol{b}|+|\boldsymbol{c}| = \underline{\qquad}$

由题设条件可知，$\boldsymbol{a},\boldsymbol{b},\boldsymbol{c}$ 互相垂直

对三个等式左右两边取模

$$
\begin{cases}
|\boldsymbol{a}| = |\boldsymbol{b}||\boldsymbol{c}|\sin\langle \boldsymbol{b},\boldsymbol{c}\rangle = |\boldsymbol{b}||\boldsymbol{c}| \\[4pt]
|\boldsymbol{b}| = |\boldsymbol{c}||\boldsymbol{a}|\sin\langle \boldsymbol{c},\boldsymbol{a}\rangle = |\boldsymbol{c}||\boldsymbol{a}| \\[4pt]
|\boldsymbol{c}| = |\boldsymbol{a}||\boldsymbol{b}|\sin\langle \boldsymbol{a},\boldsymbol{b}\rangle = |\boldsymbol{a}||\boldsymbol{b}|
\end{cases}
\implies |\boldsymbol{a}|=|\boldsymbol{b}|=|\boldsymbol{c}|=1
$$

$\therefore |\boldsymbol{a}| + |\boldsymbol{b}| + |\boldsymbol{c}| = 3$
