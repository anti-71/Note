> 已知三个向量 $\boldsymbol{a},\boldsymbol{b},\boldsymbol{c}$，其中 $\boldsymbol{c}\perp\boldsymbol{a}$，$\boldsymbol{c}\perp\boldsymbol{b}$，$\boldsymbol{a}$ 与 $\boldsymbol{b}$ 的夹角为 $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$，$|\boldsymbol{a}|=6$，$|\boldsymbol{b}|=|\boldsymbol{c}|=3$，则 $(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b})\cdot\boldsymbol{c}=\underline{\qquad}$

$$
|\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b}| = |\boldsymbol{a}||\boldsymbol{b}|\sin\langle\boldsymbol{a},\boldsymbol{b}\rangle = 6\cdot 3\cdot \frac12 = 9
$$

$\because \boldsymbol{c}\perp\boldsymbol{a},\ \boldsymbol{c}\perp\boldsymbol{b}$
$\therefore \boldsymbol{c}\parallel\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b} \implies \cos\alpha = \pm 1$

$$
\therefore (\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b})\cdot\boldsymbol{c} = |\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b}||\boldsymbol{c}|\cos\alpha = \pm 27
$$
