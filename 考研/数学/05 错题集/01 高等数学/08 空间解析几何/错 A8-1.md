> 设 $(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b})\cdot\boldsymbol{c}=2$，则 $[(\boldsymbol{a}+\boldsymbol{b})\times(\boldsymbol{b}+\boldsymbol{c})]\cdot(\boldsymbol{c}+\boldsymbol{a})=\boldsymbol{\underline{\qquad}}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{原式} &= \bigl[\boldsymbol{a}\times(\boldsymbol{b}+\boldsymbol{c})+\boldsymbol{b}\times(\boldsymbol{b}+\boldsymbol{c})\bigr]\cdot(\boldsymbol{c}+\boldsymbol{a}) \\[4pt]
&= (\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b}+\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{c}+\boldsymbol{b}\times\boldsymbol{b}+\boldsymbol{b}\times\boldsymbol{c})\cdot(\boldsymbol{c}+\boldsymbol{a}) \\[4pt]
&= (\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b})\cdot\boldsymbol{c}+(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b})\cdot\boldsymbol{a}+(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{c})\cdot\boldsymbol{c} \\
&\quad +(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{c})\cdot\boldsymbol{a}+(\boldsymbol{b}\times\boldsymbol{c})\cdot\boldsymbol{c}+(\boldsymbol{b}\times\boldsymbol{c})\cdot\boldsymbol{a}
\end{aligned}
$$

$\because \boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b}\perp\boldsymbol{a}$，$\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{c}\perp\boldsymbol{a}$，$\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{c}\perp\boldsymbol{c}$，$\boldsymbol{b}\times\boldsymbol{c}\perp\boldsymbol{c}$

$\therefore$ 含有 $\boldsymbol{a}$、$\boldsymbol{c}$ 的内积项均为零

$$
\text{原式}=(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b})\cdot\boldsymbol{c}+(\boldsymbol{b}\times\boldsymbol{c})\cdot\boldsymbol{a}
$$

由[[混合积]]性质，$(\boldsymbol{b}\times\boldsymbol{c})\cdot\boldsymbol{a}=(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b})\cdot\boldsymbol{c}=2$

所以原式 = 4
