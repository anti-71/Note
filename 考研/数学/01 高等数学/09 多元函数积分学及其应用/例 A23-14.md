> [!question] 
> 计算 $I = \displaystyle\int_{\overparen{AMB}} [\varphi(y)\cos x - \pi y]\mathrm{d}x + [\varphi'(y)\sin x - \pi]\mathrm{d}y$
> 
> 其中 $\overparen{AMB}$ 弧为连结 $A(\pi,2)$ 与点 $B(3\pi,4)$ 的线段 $\overline{AB}$ 的下方的任意分段光滑简单曲线，且该曲线与线段 $\overline{AB}$ 所围图形面积为 2

假设积分域如下图

![image.png|271x257](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/20260505160454437.png)

**法一**

补线段 $\overline{BA}$，

$$ I = \int_{\overparen{AMB}} =\oint_{\overparen{AMBA}} - \int_{\overline{BA}} $$

[[对坐标的线积分（第二类线积分）#3）补线用格林公式（利用线积分与路径无关）|补线用格林]]

$$ \oint_{\overparen{AMBA}} P\mathrm{d}x + Q\mathrm{d}y = \iint_{D} \left( \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y} \right)\mathrm{d}\sigma = \pi \iint_{D} \mathrm{d}\sigma = 2\pi $$

直线 $\overline{BA}$ 的方程为：$y = \dfrac{x}{\pi} + 1$，则

$$ \begin{aligned} \int_{\overline{BA}} &= \int_{3\pi}^{\pi} \left[ \varphi\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\cos x - \pi\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right) \right]\mathrm{d}x + \left[ \varphi'\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\sin x - \pi \right] \frac{1}{\pi}\mathrm{d}x\\
&=\int_{3\pi}^{\pi} \left[ \varphi\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\cos x +\varphi'\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\sin x\right]\mathrm{d}x - \int^{\pi}_{3\pi}x\mathrm{d}x+2\pi(\pi+1)\\
&=\int_{3\pi}^{\pi} \left[ \varphi\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\cos x +\varphi'\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\sin x\right]\mathrm{d}x+2\pi(1 + 3\pi)
\\ & \end{aligned} $$
对于第一个积分凑微分

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=\int^{\pi}_{3\pi}\left[\varphi\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\mathrm{d}\sin x+\sin x\mathrm{d}\varphi\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\right]\\
&=\left.\sin x\cdot\varphi\left( \frac{x}{\pi} + 1 \right)\right|^\pi_{3\pi}\\
&=0
\end{aligned}
$$

合并结果为

$$
= 2\pi(1 + 3\pi)
$$

故 $I = 2\pi - 2\pi(1 + 3\pi) = -6\pi^2$

**法二**

将带未知函数的积分分出来

$$ I = \int_{\overparen{AMB}} \varphi(y)\cos x\mathrm{d}x + \varphi'(y)\sin x\mathrm{d}y - \pi \int_{\overparen{AMB}} y\mathrm{d}x + \mathrm{d}y $$

根据法一，其中

$$ \int_{\overparen{AMB}} \varphi(y)\cos x\mathrm{d}x + \varphi'(y)\sin x\mathrm{d}y = \varphi(y)\sin x \Big|_{(\pi,2)}^{(3\pi,4)} = 0 $$

第二项积分[[对坐标的线积分（第二类线积分）#3）补线用格林公式（利用线积分与路径无关）|补线用格林]] 

$$ \begin{aligned} \int_{\overparen{AMB}} y\mathrm{d}x + \mathrm{d}y &= \oint_{\overparen{AMB} + \overline{BA}} y\mathrm{d}x + \mathrm{d}y - \int_{\overline{BA}} y\mathrm{d}x + \mathrm{d}y \\ &= -\iint_{D} \mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y - \int_{4}^{2} \pi y\mathrm{d}y + \mathrm{d}y \\&= 6\pi \end{aligned} $$

故 $I = -6\pi^2$
