> [!question] 
> 求底半径为 $R$，高为 $H$，密度为 $\mu$ 的均匀柱体对底面圆直径的转动惯量

**法一**

这里以 $y$ 轴计算

$$
I_y=\iiint\limits_\Omega\mu(x^2+z^2)\mathrm{d}v
$$

用[[三重积分#2）柱坐标|柱坐标]]

$$ \begin{aligned} &=\mu\int_0^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_0^R\mathrm{d}r\int_0^H(r^2\cos^2\theta+z^2)r\mathrm{d}z\\ &=\mu\int_0^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_0^R\left(Hr^3\cos^2\theta+\frac{1}{3}H^3r\right)\mathrm{d}r\\ &=\mu\int_0^{2\pi}\left(\frac{1}{4}HR^4\cos^2\theta+\frac{1}{6}H^3R^2\right)\mathrm{d}\theta\\ &=\frac{\pi\mu HR^2}{12}(3R^2+4H^2) \end{aligned} $$

**法二**

依旧以 $y$ 轴

$$ I_y=\iiint\limits_\Omega\mu(x^2+z^2)\mathrm{d}v, $$

拆成两个积分计算：

$$ \begin{aligned} \iiint\limits_\Omega x^2\mathrm{d}v&=\frac{1}{2}\iiint\limits_\Omega(x^2+y^2)\mathrm{d}v\\ &=\frac{1}{2}\int_0^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_0^R\mathrm{d}r\int_0^H r^3\mathrm{d}z\\ &=\frac{\pi HR^4}{4}, \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} \iiint\limits_\Omega z^2\mathrm{d}v&=\int_0^H\pi R^2 z^2\mathrm{d}z\\ &=\frac{\pi R^2 H^3}{3}, \end{aligned} $$

故

$$ I_y=\frac{\pi\mu HR^2}{12}(3R^2+4H^2) $$
