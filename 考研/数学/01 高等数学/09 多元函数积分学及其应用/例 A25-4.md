> [!question] 
> 设 $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$，$f(u)$ 有二阶连续导数，$\mathrm{div}[\mathrm{\boldsymbol{grad }}]f(r)] = 0$，试求 $f(u)$

[[场论初步#1）梯度|梯度公式]]如下

$$ \mathrm{\boldsymbol{grad }}f(r) = \frac{x}{r}f'(r)\boldsymbol{i} + \frac{y}{r}f'(r)\boldsymbol{j} + \frac{z}{r}f'(r)\boldsymbol{k}, $$

代入[[场论初步#2）散度|散度公式]]

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{div}[\text{grad }f(r)] &= \frac{x^2}{r^2}f''(r) + \left(\frac{1}{r} - \frac{x^2}{r^3}\right)f'(r) + \frac{y^2}{r^2}f''(r) + \left(\frac{1}{r} - \frac{y^2}{r^3}\right)f'(r) + \frac{z^2}{r^2}f''(r) + \left(\frac{1}{r} - \frac{z^2}{r^3}\right)f'(r) \\ &= f''(r) + \frac{2}{r}f'(r), \end{aligned} $$

根据题设条件

$$ f''(r) + \frac{2}{r}f'(r) = 0, $$

即

$$ r^2f''(r) + 2rf'(r) = 0, $$

于是

$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}r}\left(r^2f'(r)\right) = 0\implies r^2f'(r) = C_1, $$

两边同除 $r^2$

$$ f'(r) = \frac{C_1}{r^2}$$

两边对 $r$ 积分

$$
f(r) = -\frac{C_1}{r} + C_2
$$
