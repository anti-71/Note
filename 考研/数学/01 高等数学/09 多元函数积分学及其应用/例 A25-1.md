> [!question] 
> 函数 $u = \ln(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)$ 在点 $M(1,2,-2)$ 处的梯度 $\left.\mathrm{\boldsymbol{grad }}u\right|_{M} = \underline{\qquad}$

根据[[场论初步#1）梯度|梯度公式]]，先求三个偏导数

$$ \left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,2,-2)} = \left.\frac{2x}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}\right|_{(1,2,-2)} = \frac{2}{9}, $$

$$ \left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right|_{(1,2,-2)} = \left.\frac{2y}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}\right|_{(1,2,-2)} = \frac{4}{9}, $$

$$ \left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial z}\right|_{(1,2,-2)} = \left.\frac{2z}{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}\right|_{(1,2,-2)} = -\frac{4}{9}, $$

则

$$ \left.\mathrm{\boldsymbol{grad}}u\right|_{(1,2,-2)} = \frac{2}{9}\boldsymbol{i} + \frac{4}{9}\boldsymbol{j} - \frac{4}{9}\boldsymbol{k} = \frac{2}{9}\{1,2,-2\} $$
