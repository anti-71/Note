> [!question] 
> 设 
> 
> $$
> f(x) = \begin{cases}(1+x^2)^{\frac{1}{\sin x}}, & x \neq 0 \\ 1, & x = 0\end{cases}
> $$
> 
> ，则 $f'(0) = \underline{\quad\quad}$

$$ \begin{aligned} f'(0) &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x} \\&= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(1+x^2)^{\frac{1}{\sin x}} - 1}{x} \\
&=\lim_{x\to0} \frac{x^2}{x\sin x}
\\ &=\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x^2}{x^2} \\&= 1\end{aligned} $$ 

> [!note] 
> 分段函数在分界点处的导数一般都要用定义求
