> [!question] 
> 设 $f(x) = \ln\left(x + \sqrt{1 + x^2}\right)$，则 $f'(0) + f^{(100)}(0) = \underline{\quad\quad}$

由于 $f'(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + x^2}}$，则 $f'(0) = 1$

又 $f(x)$ 是奇函数，则 $f^{(2n)}(0) = 0$（$n = 1,2,\dots$），从而有 

$$ f'(0) + f^{(100)}(0) = 1$$
