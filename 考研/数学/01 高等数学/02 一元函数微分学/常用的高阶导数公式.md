1. $$(sinx)^{(n)}=sin(x+n·\frac{\pi}{2})$$
2. $$(cosx)^{(n)}=cos(x+n·\frac{\pi}{2})$$
3. $$(u\pm v)^{(n)}=u^{(n)}\pm v^{(n)}$$
4. $$(uv)^{(n)}=\sum^n_{k=0}C^k_nu^{(n)}v^{(n-k)}$$
