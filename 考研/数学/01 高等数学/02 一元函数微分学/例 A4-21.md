> [!question] 
> 设 $f(x) = \mathrm{e}^x \sin x$，求 $f^{(n)}(x)$

没有现成的公式，我们采用方法 2

$$ \begin{aligned} f'(x) &= \mathrm{e}^x \sin x + \mathrm{e}^x \cos x \\&= \mathrm{e}^x(\sin x + \cos x) \\ &= \mathrm{e}^x \cdot \sqrt{2}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\sin x + \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\cos x\right) \\ &= \sqrt{2}\mathrm{e}^x \sin\left(x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right), \end{aligned} $$

$$ f^{(n)}(x) = (\sqrt{2})^n \mathrm{e}^x \sin\left(x + \frac{n\pi}{4}\right)$$
