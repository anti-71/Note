> [!question] 
> 设 $y = f(x)$ 的反函数是 $x = \varphi(y)$，且 $f(x) = \displaystyle\int_{1}^{2x} \mathrm{e}^{t^2} \mathrm{d}t + 1$，则 $\varphi''(1) = \underline{\quad\quad}$

由[[求导法则#4）反函数的导数|反函数求导法]]得 

$$ \varphi'(y) = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}y} = \frac{1}{\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}} = \frac{1}{f'(x)}, $$

上式两端对 $y$ 求导得 

$$ \varphi''(y) = \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\left[\frac{1}{f'(x)}\right] \cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}y} = -\frac{f''(x)}{[f'(x)]^2} \cdot \frac{1}{f'(x)}$$

由 $f(x) = \displaystyle\int_{1}^{2x} \mathrm{e}^{t^2} \mathrm{d}t + 1$ 知，$x = \displaystyle\frac{1}{2}$ 时 $y = 1$，且 

$$ f'(x) = 2\mathrm{e}^{4x^2}, \quad f''(x) = 16x\mathrm{e}^{4x^2}, $$

则 

$$ \varphi''(1) = -\frac{f''\left(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\right)}{\left[f'\left(\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\right)\right]^3} = -\frac{8\mathrm{e}}{8\mathrm{e}^3} = -\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}^2}$$

> [!note] 
> 这里要注意 $\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}y} = \frac{1}{\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}}$，但 
> 
> $$\frac{\mathrm{d}^2 x}{\mathrm{d}y^2} \neq \frac{1}{\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{d}^2 y}{\mathrm{d}x^2}}
> $$
