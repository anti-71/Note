> [!question] 
> 设 $f''(t) \neq 0$，有 
> 
> $$ \begin{cases} x = f'(t), \\ y = t f'(t) - f(t), \end{cases} $$ 
> 
> 求 $\frac{\mathrm{d}^2 y}{\mathrm{d}x^2}$

$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \frac{y'(t)}{x'(t)} = \frac{f'(t) + t f''(t) - f'(t)}{f''(t)} = t, $$

 $$ \frac{\mathrm{d}^2 y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} = \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(t) = \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(t)\frac{\mathrm{d}t}{\mathrm{d}x} = 1 \times \frac{1}{x'(t)} = \frac{1}{f''(t)}$$

> [!note] 
> 本题中求二阶导数 $\frac{\mathrm{d}^2 y}{\mathrm{d}x^2}$ 不能套公式，条件不够
