> [!question] 
> （1）曲线 $y^2 = x$ 在点 $(0,0)$ 处的曲率圆方程为 $\underline{\quad\quad}$
> 
> （2）曲线 $\begin{cases}x = \cos^3 t, \\ y = \sin^3 t,\end{cases}$ 在 $t = \frac{\pi}{4}$ 对应点处的曲率为 $\underline{\quad\quad}$

（1）常规代公式显然代不进去，考虑到曲率圆的几何意义，$y^2 = x$ 和 $y = x^2$ 的曲率圆半径相同，转而去求 $y = x^2$ 的曲率半径

$\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}y}\right|_{y=0} = \left.2y\right|_{y=0} = 0$，$\left.\frac{\mathrm{d}^2x}{\mathrm{d}y^2}\right|_{y=0} = \left.2\right|_{y=0} = 2$，则 

$$ K = \frac{|x''(0)|}{[1 + x'^2(0)]^{\frac{3}{2}}} = 2, $$

则曲率半径为 $R = \frac{1}{2}$，曲率圆方程为 $\left(x - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + y^2 = \frac{1}{4}$

（2）

**法一**（公式法）

$$ x'\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \left.-3\cos^2 t\sin t\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = -\frac{3}{2\sqrt{2}}, $$

$$ x''\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \left.-3(-2\cos t\sin^2 t + \cos^3 t)\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{3}{2\sqrt{2}}, $$

$$ y'\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \left.3\sin^2 t\cos t\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{3}{2\sqrt{2}}, $$

$$ y''\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \left.3(2\sin t\cos^2 t - \sin^3 t)\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{3}{2\sqrt{2}}, $$

$$ K = \frac{|y''x' - x''y'|}{(x'^2 + y'^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}} = \frac{2}{3}$$

**法二**（参数方程求导代曲率公式）

$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = \frac{y'(t)}{x'(t)} = \frac{3\sin^2 t\cos t}{-3\cos^2 t\sin t} = -\tan t, $$

$$ \frac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2} = -\sec^2 t \cdot \frac{1}{x'(t)} = -\sec^2 t \cdot \frac{1}{-3\cos^2 t\sin t} = \frac{1}{3\cos^4 t\sin t}, $$

$$ \left.\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = -1,\quad \left.\frac{\mathrm{d}^2y}{\mathrm{d}x^2}\right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}$$

$$ K = \frac{|y''|}{(1 + y'^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}} = \frac{\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}}{[1 + (-1)^2]^{\frac{3}{2}}} = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}} = \frac{2}{3}$$
