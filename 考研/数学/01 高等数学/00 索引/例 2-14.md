> [!question] 
> 求极限
> $$
> \lim\limits_{x\to\infty}\left [x-x^2\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)\right]
> $$

**法一**

倒代换，令 $x=\frac{1}{t}$，则

$$
 \begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= \lim_{t\to0}\left [\frac{1}{t}-\frac{1}{t^2}\ln(1+t)\right] \\ &= \lim_{t\to0}\frac{t-\ln(1+t)}{t^2} \\ &= \lim_{t\to0}\frac{\frac{1}{2}t^2}{t^2} \\ &= \frac{1}{2} \end{aligned}
$$

**法二**

由 $\ln(1+x)$ 的[[利用泰勒公式|泰勒公式]]得

$$
\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)=\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{2x^2}+o\left(\frac{1}{x^2}\right),
$$

则

$$
 \begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= \lim_{x\to\infty}\left [x-x^2\left(\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{2x^2}+o\left(\frac{1}{x^2}\right)\right)\right] \\ &= \lim_{x\to\infty}\left [\frac{1}{2}-x^2\cdot o\left(\frac{1}{x^2}\right)\right] \\ &= \frac{1}{2} \end{aligned}
$$

 **法三**

联想到[[利用等价无穷小代换|等价代换]]，$x - \ln (1 + x) \sim \frac{1}{2}x^2$，凑出该形式

$$
\begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= \lim_{x\to\infty}x^2\left [\frac{1}{x}-\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)\right] \\ &= \lim_{x\to\infty}x^2\left(\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{x^2}\right) \\ &= \frac{1}{2} \end{aligned}
$$
