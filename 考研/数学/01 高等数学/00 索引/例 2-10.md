> [!question] 
> 求极限
> $$
> \lim\limits_{x\to 0}\frac{\cos x - \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}}{x^2\left[x+\ln(1-x)\right]}
> $$

> **法一** （[[利用泰勒公式|泰勒公式]]）
$$
 \begin{aligned} \ln(1-x) &= -x - \frac{x^2}{2} + o(x^2), \\ \cos x &= 1 - \frac{x^2}{2!} + \frac{x^4}{4!} + o(x^4), \\ \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{2}} &= 1 - \frac{x^2}{2} + \frac{x^4}{2^2 2!} + o(x^4) \end{aligned}
$$

$$
 \begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{-\frac{1}{12}x^4 + o(x^4)}{x^2\left[-\frac{x^2}{2} + o(x^2)\right]} = \frac{1}{6} \end{aligned}
$$

**法二**（[[利用等价无穷小代换|等价代换]]）

由于 $x+\ln(1-x)=\ln(1-x)-(-x)\sim -\frac{x^2}{2}$，则
$$
\begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\cos x - \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}}{-\frac{1}{2}x^4} \\
&= \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{-\sin x + x\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}}{-2x^3} \quad \text{（洛必达法则）} \\ &= -\frac{1}{2}\left[\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{x-\sin x}{x^3} + \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{x\left(\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}-1\right)}{x^3}\right] \\ &= -\frac{1}{2}\left[\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\frac{1}{6}x^3}{x^3} + \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{x\left(-\frac{x^2}{2}\right)}{x^3}\right] \\ &= \frac{1}{6} \end{aligned}
$$
