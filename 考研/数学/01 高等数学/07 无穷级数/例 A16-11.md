> [!question] 
> 设 $a_n = \displaystyle\int_{0}^{n\pi} x \lvert \sin x \rvert \mathrm{d}x (n = 1,2,\cdots)$，求极限 $\displaystyle\lim_{n \to \infty}\left(\frac{a_1}{2} + \frac{a_2}{2^2} + \cdots + \frac{a_n}{2^n}\right)$

原函数不好求，我们想到“区间再现”，令 $x = n\pi - t$

$$ \begin{aligned} a_n &= \int_{0}^{n\pi} x \lvert \sin x \rvert \mathrm{d}x\\ &= \int_{0}^{n\pi} (n\pi - t) \lvert \sin t \rvert \mathrm{d}t \\&= n\pi \int_{0}^{n\pi} \lvert \sin t \rvert \mathrm{d}t - a_n  \end{aligned} $$

故

$$
a_n = \frac{n\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{n\pi} \lvert \sin t \rvert \mathrm{d}t = \frac{n^2\pi}{2} \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin t \mathrm{d}t = n^2\pi
$$

代入所求极限式

$$ \lim_{n \to \infty}\left(\frac{a_1}{2} + \frac{a_2}{2^2} + \cdots + \frac{a_n}{2^n}\right) = \pi \lim_{n \to \infty}\left(\frac{1^2}{2} + \frac{2^2}{2^2} + \cdots + \frac{n^2}{2^n}\right) = \pi \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{n^2}{2^n}$$

将 $\dfrac{1}{2}$ 视为 $x$，令 $S(x) = \displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^2 x^n$，求和函数

观察后不能直接用公式，提一个 $x$

$$
S(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n^2 x^n = x\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n x^n\right)'
$$

转为求 $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n x^n$ 的和函数，也提一个 $x$

$$ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n x^n = x \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n x^{n-1} = x\left(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} x^n\right)' = x\left(\frac{1}{1-x} - 1\right)' = \frac{x}{(1-x)^2}, $$

代到 $S(x)$ 中

$$  = x\left(\frac{x}{(1-x)^2}\right)' = \frac{x + x^2}{(1-x)^3}$$

故

$$ \lim_{n \to \infty}\left(\frac{a_1}{2} + \frac{a_2}{2^2} + \cdots + \frac{a_n}{2^n}\right) = \pi S\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = 6\pi$$
