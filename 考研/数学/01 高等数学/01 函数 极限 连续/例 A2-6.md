> [!question] 
> 求极限
> $$
> \lim\limits_{x \to 0}\frac{\frac{x^2}{2} + 1 - \sqrt{1 + x^2}}{(\cos x - \mathrm{e}^{x^2})\sin^2 x}
> $$

**法一**

由于（[[利用泰勒公式|泰勒公式]]）
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{1 + x^2} &= 1 + \frac{1}{2}x^2 + \frac{\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2} - 1\right)}{2!}x^4 + o(x^4), \\
\cos x &= 1 - \frac{1}{2}x^2 + o(x^2), \\
\mathrm{e}^{x^2} &= 1 + x^2 + o(x^2)
\end{aligned}
$$
代入所求极限式得
$$
原式 = \lim\limits_{x \to 0}\frac{\frac{1}{8}x^4 + o(x^4)}{\left [-\frac{3}{2}x^2 + o(x^2)\right] x^2} = -\frac{1}{12}
$$

**法二**

先处理分母，
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\to0}(\cos x - \mathrm{e}^{x^2})\sin^2 x &= (cosx - 1 + 1 - e^{x^2})\cdot x^2 \\
&= (-\frac{1}{2}x^2-x^2)\cdot x^2 \\
&= -\frac{3}{2}x^4
\end{aligned}
$$
此时分子分母都趋于 0，且对分子形式求导可接受，对极限[[利用洛必达法则|洛必达]]
$$
\begin{aligned}
原式 &=\lim_{x\to0} \frac{x - \frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}}{-6x^3} \\
&= \lim_{x\to0} \frac{\frac{1}{2}x^2}{-6x^2} \\
&= -\frac{1}{12}
\end{aligned}
$$
**法三**

分母依旧如法二处理，转而对分子配方
$$
\begin{aligned}
原式 &= \lim_{x\to0} \frac{\frac{1}{2}(\sqrt{x^2+1}-1)^2}{-\frac{3}{2}x^4} \\
&= -\frac{1}{3}\lim_{x\to0} \frac{(\frac{1}{2}x^2)^2}{x^4} \\
&= -\frac{1}{12}
\end{aligned}
$$
