> [!question] 
> 求极限 $\lim\limits_{n\to\infty}\left(\frac{1}{n^2+1}+\frac{2}{n^2+2}+\cdots+\frac{n}{n^2+n}\right)$


$$
 \frac{1+2+\cdots+n}{n^2+n}\leqslant\frac{1}{n^2+1}+\frac{2}{n^2+2}+\cdots+\frac{n}{n^2+n}\leqslant\frac{1+2+\cdots+n}{n^2+1},
$$

$$
 \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1+2+\cdots+n}{n^2+n}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\frac{1}{2}n(n+1)}{n^2+n}=\frac{1}{2},
$$

$$
 \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1+2+\cdots+n}{n^2+1}=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{\frac{1}{2}n(n+1)}{n^2+1}=\frac{1}{2},
$$

 则
$$
原式 = \frac{1}{2}
$$


> [!note] 常用结论
> $$
> \lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt [n]{a^n_1+a^n_2+...+a^n_m}= \max{a_i}= a
> $$

> [!success] 
> 令 $\max_\limits{1\leqslant i\leqslant m}\{a_i\}=a$，则 
> 
> $$ a=\sqrt[n]{a^n}\leqslant\sqrt[n]{a_1^n+a_2^n+\cdots+a_m^n}\leqslant\sqrt[n]{ma^n}=\sqrt[n]{m}a. $$ 又 $\lim_\limits{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{m}=1$，则由夹逼准则知 
> 
> $$ \lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{a_1^n+a_2^n+\cdots+a_m^n}=a=\max_{1\leqslant i\leqslant m}\{a_i\} $$
