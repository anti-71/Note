1. 常用的基本极限

$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{sinx}{x}= 1,\lim_{x\to0}(1+x)^{\frac{1}{x}}= e,\lim_{x\to\infty}(1+\frac{1}{x})^x = e,\lim_{x\to0}\frac{a^x-1}{x}= \ln a,
\\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt [n] n = 1,\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt [n] a = 1(a > 0)
$$

$$
\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{a_nx^n+a_{n-1}{x^{n-1}}+...+a_1x+a_0}{b_mx^m+b_{m-1}{x^{m-1}}+...+b_1x+b_0}=\left\{
\begin{array}{l}
\frac{a_n}{b_m},\ n = m\\
0,\ n < m\\
\infty,\ n > m
\end{array}
\right.
$$

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}x^n =\left\{
\begin{array}{l}
0,\ |x|< 1\\
\infty,\ |x| > 1\\
1,\ x = 1\\
\text{不存在},\ x =-1
\end{array}
\right.
$$

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}e^{nx}=\left\{
\begin{array}{l}
0,\ x < 0\\
+\infty,\ x > 0\\
1,\ x = 0
\end{array}
\right.
$$

2. “$1^{\infty}$”型极限常用结论
对于 $1^{\infty}$ 型未定式，其极限结果 = e$^A$
	1. $\lim\limits_{x \to \square} [1 + f(x)]^{g(x)}$，其中 $f(x) \to 0$，$g(x) \to \infty$
  $$
   \lim\limits_{x \to \square} [1 + f(x)]^{g(x)} = \mathrm{e}^A, \quad A = \lim\limits_{x \to \square} f(x) \cdot g(x)
   $$
	2. $\lim\limits_{x \to \square} f(x)^{g(x)}$，其中 $f(x) \to 1$，$g(x) \to \infty$
	$$
   \lim\limits_{x \to \square} f(x)^{g(x)} = \mathrm{e}^A, \quad A = \lim\limits_{x \to \square} [f(x) - 1] \cdot g(x)
   $$
