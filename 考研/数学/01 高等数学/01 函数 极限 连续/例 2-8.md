> [!question] 
> 求极限
> $$
> \lim\limits_{x\to 0}\frac{\arcsin x - \sin x}{\arctan x - \tan x}
> $$

由[[利用等价无穷小代换|等价代换]]

$$
\begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{(\arcsin x - x) - (\sin x - x)}{(\arctan x - x) - (\tan x - x)} \\ &= \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\left(\frac{1}{6}x^3\right) - \left(-\frac{1}{6}x^3\right)}{\left(-\frac{1}{3}x^3\right) - \left(\frac{1}{3}x^3\right)} \\ &= \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\frac{1}{3}x^3}{-\frac{2}{3}x^3} = -\frac{1}{2} \end{aligned}
$$
