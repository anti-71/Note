> [!question] 
> 求极限
> $$
>  \lim_{x\to+\infty}\left(x^{\frac{1}{x}}-1\right)^{\frac{1}{\ln x}}
> $$

**法一**

令 $y = \left(x^{\frac{1}{x}} - 1\right)^{\frac{1}{\ln x}}$，则 

$$
\ln y = \frac{\ln\left(x^{\frac{1}{x}} - 1\right)}{\ln x} = \frac{\ln\left(\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}} - 1\right)}{\ln x},
$$

[[利用洛必达法则|洛必达]]

$$
 \lim_{x\to+\infty}\ln y = \lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{x\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}}}{\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}} - 1} \cdot \frac{1 - \ln x}{x^2},
$$

由于 $\lim_\limits{x\to+\infty}\frac{\ln x}{x} = 0$，则 

$$
= \lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{x\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}}}{\frac{\ln x}{x}} \cdot \frac{1 - \ln x}{x^2} = \lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{1 - \ln x}{\ln x} = -1, 
$$

故原式 = $e^{-1}$

**法二**

法一显然过于繁琐，取对数这一步不变，我们可以使用[[拉格朗日中值定理]]


$$
\begin{aligned} 
\ln y &= \frac{\ln\left(\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}} - 1\right)}{\ln x} \\
&= \frac{\ln\left(\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}} - \mathrm{e}^0\right)}{\ln x} \\ 
&= \frac{\ln\left(\mathrm{e}^{\xi}\frac{\ln x}{x}\right)}{\ln x} \\ 
&= \frac{\xi + \ln\ln x - \ln x}{\ln x}, 
\end{aligned}
$$

则 $\lim_\limits{x\to+\infty}\ln y = -1$，原式 = $e^{-1}$

**法三**

观察到 $\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}} - 1 \sim \frac{\ln x}{x}$，我们可以凑出 $\ln 1$ 以简化计算

$$
 \begin{aligned} 
 \lim_{x\to+\infty}\ln y &= \lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{\ln\left(\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}} - 1\right)}{\ln x} \\ 
 &= \lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{\ln\left(\frac{\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\ln x}{x}} - 1}{\frac{\ln x}{x}}\right) + \ln\frac{\ln x}{x}}{\ln x} \\
 &= \lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{\ln\ln x - \ln x}{\ln x} \\
 &= -1, 
 \end{aligned} 
$$

则原式 = $e^{-1}$
