> [!question] 
> 求极限
> $$
> \lim_{x \to 0} \left[ \frac{1}{\ln(1 + \tan^2 x)} - \frac{1}{\ln(1 + x^2)} \right]
> $$

首先通分，分母直接[[利用等价无穷小代换|等价代换]]
$$
\begin{aligned}
原式 &= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{\ln (1 + x^2) - \ln (1 + \tan^2 x)}{\ln (1 + x^2)\ln (1 + \tan^2 x)} \\
&= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{\ln (1 + x^2) - \ln (1 + \tan^2 x)}{x^2 \cdot x^2} \\
&= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{\ln \frac{1 + x^2}{1 + \tan^2 x}}{x^4}
\end{aligned}
$$

对 ln 要有敏感性，直接[[利用等价无穷小代换|等价代换]]

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{\frac{1 + x^2}{1 + \tan^2 x} - 1}{x^4} \\
&= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{x^2 - \tan^2 x}{x^4 \cdot (1 + \tan^2 x)} \\
&= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{(x + \tan x)(x - \tan x)}{x^4} \\
&= \lim_{x\to0}\frac{2x \cdot (-\frac{1}{3}x^3)}{x^4} \\
&= -\frac{2}{3}
\end{aligned}
$$
