> [!question] 
> 求极限 
> 
> $$
> \lim_{n\to\infty}\left[\frac{\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)^n}{e}\right]^n
> $$

先把原式指数化

$$
\begin{aligned}
原式 &=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left[\frac{e^{n\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)}}{e}\right]^n \\
&= \lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{e^{n^2\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)}}{e^n} \\
&=\lim_{n\to\infty}e^{n^2\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)-n},
\end{aligned}
$$ 

转而去求 e 的指数的极限

$$
\begin{aligned} \lim_{n\to\infty}\left[n-n^2\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)\right]&=\lim_{n\to\infty}n^2\left[\frac{1}{n}-\ln\left(1+\frac{1}{n}\right)\right]\\ &=\lim_{n\to\infty}n^2\cdot\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{n}\right)^2\\&=\frac{1}{2}, \end{aligned} $$ 
则原式 =  $e^{-\frac{1}{2}}$
