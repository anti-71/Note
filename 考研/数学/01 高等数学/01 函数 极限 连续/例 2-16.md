> [!question] 
> 求极限
> $$
> \lim\limits_{x \to +\infty} x \left(1 - \frac{\ln x}{x}\right)^x
> $$

**法一**

$x\to+\infty$，$\left(1 - \frac{\ln x}{x}\right)^x \sim e^{-\frac{\ln x}{x} \cdot x}\to0$，显然把两项指数化

$$
\begin{aligned}
原式 &= \lim_{x \to +\infty} e^{\ln x} \cdot e^{x\ln\left(1 - \frac{\ln x}{x}\right)} \\
&= \lim_{x \to +\infty} e^{\ln x + x\ln\left(1 - \frac{\ln x}{x}\right)},
\end{aligned}
$$

转为求指数的极限，提出一个 x 凑成[[利用等价无穷小代换|等价代换]] $x - \ln (1 + x) \sim \frac{1}{2} x^2$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left [\ln x + x\ln\left(1 - \frac{\ln x}{x}\right)\right]
&= \lim_{x \to +\infty} x \left [\frac{\ln x}{x} + \ln\left(1 - \frac{\ln x}{x}\right)\right] \\
&= \lim_{x \to +\infty} x \left [-\frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{\ln x}{x}\right)^2\right]\\
&= -\frac{1}{2} \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\ln^2 x}{x} \\
&= 0,
\end{aligned}
$$

故原式 $= e^0 = 1$

**法二**

用取对数法求，令 $y = x \left(1 - \frac{\ln x}{x}\right)^x$，法二我们用[[利用泰勒公式|泰勒公式]]，则
$$
\begin{aligned}
\ln y &= \ln x + x\ln\left(1 - \frac{\ln x}{x}\right) \\
&= \ln x + x \left [-\frac{\ln x}{x} - \frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{\ln x}{x}\right)^2 + o\left(\left(-\frac{\ln x}{x}\right)^2\right)\right] \\
&= -\frac{1}{2} \frac{\ln^2 x}{x} + x \cdot o\left(\left(-\frac{\ln x}{x}\right)^2\right), \end{aligned}
$$
 
 $\implies \lim_\limits{x \to +\infty} \ln y = 0,$

故原式 $= e^0 = 1$
