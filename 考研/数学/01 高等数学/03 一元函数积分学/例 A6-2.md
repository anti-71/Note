> [!question] 
> $$I = \int \frac{x\mathrm{e}^x}{\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x - 1}}\mathrm{d}x$$ 

把 $e^x$ [[第一换元积分法|凑微分]]

$$ 
\begin{aligned} I &= 2\int x\mathrm{d}\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x - 1} \\ &= 2x\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x - 1} - 2\int \sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x - 1}\mathrm{d}x 
\end{aligned} $$

[[第二换元积分法|换元]]，令 $\sqrt{\mathrm{e^x - 1}} = t$ 

$$
\begin{aligned} 
\int \sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x - 1}\mathrm{d}x &= \int \frac{2t^2}{1+t^2}\mathrm{d}t\\ &= 2t - 2\arctan t + C 
\end{aligned} 
$$

把 t 带回

$$
\begin{aligned} 
I &= 2x\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x - 1} - 4\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x - 1} + 4\arctan\sqrt{\mathrm{e}^x - 1} + C 
\end{aligned} 
$$
