> [!question] 
> 设 $f(x)$ 连续，且 $\lim_\limits{x\to+\infty}f(x)=1$，则
> 
> $$\lim_{x\to+\infty}\int_x^{x+2}t\sin\left(\frac{3}{t}\right)f(t)\mathrm{d}t = \underline{\quad\quad}$$

利用[[积分中值定理]]

$$ \begin{aligned} &\lim_{x\to+\infty}\int_x^{x+2}t\sin\left(\frac{3}{t}\right)f(t)\mathrm{d}t \\ &= \lim_{x\to+\infty}2\xi\sin\left(\frac{3}{\xi}\right)f(\xi) \quad (x<\xi<x+2) \\ &= 6 \end{aligned} $$
