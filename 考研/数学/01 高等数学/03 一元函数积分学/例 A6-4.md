> [!question] 
> $$I = \int \frac{1}{x+x^9}\mathrm{d}x$$ 

**法一**

凑 $x^8$

$$
\begin{aligned} I &= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x(1+x^8)} \\&= \int \frac{x^7\mathrm{d}x}{x^8(1+x^8)} \end{aligned} $$

令 $x^8 = u$ 

$$
\begin{aligned} &= \frac{1}{8}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}u}{u(1+u)} \\ &= \frac{1}{8}[\ln u - \ln(1+u)] + C \\ &= \frac{1}{8}\ln\frac{x^8}{1+x^8} + C \end{aligned} 
$$

**法二**

加项减项拆

$$ \begin{aligned} I &= \int \frac{[(1+x^8)-x^8]\mathrm{d}x}{x(1+x^8)} \\&= \int \left(\frac{1}{x} - \frac{x^7}{1+x^8}\right)\mathrm{d}x \\ &= \ln|x| - \frac{1}{8}\ln(1+x^8) + C \end{aligned} $$

**法三**

凑 $x^{-8}$

$$ \begin{aligned} I &= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^9\left(1+\frac{1}{x^8}\right)} \\&= -\frac{1}{8}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x^{-8}}{1+x^{-8}} \\ &= -\frac{1}{8}\ln|1+x^{-8}| + C \end{aligned} $$
