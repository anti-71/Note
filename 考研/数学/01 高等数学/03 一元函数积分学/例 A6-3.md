> [!question] 
> $$I = \int \frac{1}{(x+1)(x^2-2x+2)}\mathrm{d}x$$ 

**法一**

[[有理函数积分|部分分式法]]，令

$$\frac{1}{(x+1)(x^2-2x+2)} = \frac{A}{x+1} + \frac{Bx+C}{x^2-2x+2},$$

则 

$$ 1 = A(x^2-2x+2) + (x+1)(Bx+C) $$

比较同次幂系数得 $A+B=0$，$-2A+B+C=0$，$2A+C=1$，解得 

$$ A = \frac{1}{5},\ B = -\frac{1}{5},\ C = \frac{3}{5} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} I &= \frac{1}{5}\int \frac{1}{x+1}\mathrm{d}x - \frac{1}{5}\int \frac{(x-1)-2}{(x-1)^2+1}\mathrm{d}x \\ &= \frac{1}{5}\ln|x+1| - \frac{1}{10}\ln[(x-1)^2+1] + \frac{2}{5}\arctan(x-1) + C \end{aligned} $$

**法二**

加项减项拆，先列出 $x^2 - 2x + 2$，再凑出一个和 $x + 1$ 有关的式子相减，积分号外凑配系数

$$ \begin{aligned} I &= \int \frac{1}{(x+1)(x^2-2x+2)}\mathrm{d}x \\ &= \frac{1}{5}\int \frac{(x^2-2x+2)-(x+1)(x-3)}{(x+1)(x^2-2x+2)}\mathrm{d}x \\ &= \frac{1}{5}\int \frac{1}{x+1}\mathrm{d}x - \frac{1}{5}\int \frac{x-3}{x^2-2x+1}\mathrm{d}x \\ &= \frac{1}{5}\ln|x+1| - \frac{1}{10}\ln[(x-1)^2+1] + \frac{2}{5}\arctan(x-1) + C \end{aligned} $$
