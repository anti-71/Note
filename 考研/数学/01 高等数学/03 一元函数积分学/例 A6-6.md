> [!question] 
> $$\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1+\sin x+\cos x}$$

此题适合用[[三角有理式积分#1）一般方法（万能代换）|万能代换]]，令 $\tan\frac{x}{2} = t$，则 

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= \int \frac{\frac{2}{1+t^2}\mathrm{d}t}{1+\frac{2t}{1+t^2}+\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2}} \\ &= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}t}{1+t} \\ &= \ln|1+t| + C \\ &= \ln\left|1+\tan\frac{x}{2}\right| + C \end{aligned} $$
