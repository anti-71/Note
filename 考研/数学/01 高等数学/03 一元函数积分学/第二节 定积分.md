 ##### （2） **【例 2】** $$I = \int_{0}^{n\pi} \sqrt{1 - \sin 2x} \mathrm{d}x$$ **解** **（方法一）** $$ \begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= n\int_{0}^{\pi} \sqrt{1 - \sin 2x} \mathrm{d}x \\ &= n\int_{0}^{\pi} \sqrt{(\cos x - \sin x)^2} \mathrm{d}x \\ &= n\int_{0}^{\pi} |\cos x - \sin x| \ \mathrm{d}x \\ &= n\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} (\cos x - \sin x)\mathrm{d}x + n\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\pi} (\sin x - \cos x)\mathrm{d}x = 2\sqrt{2}n \end{aligned} $$ （周期性） **（方法二）** $$ \begin{aligned} \text{原式} &= n\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{5\pi}{4}} \sqrt{1 - \sin 2x} \mathrm{d}x = n\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{5\pi}{4}} \sqrt{(\cos x - \sin x)^2} \mathrm{d}x \\ &= n\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{5\pi}{4}} (\sin x - \cos x)\mathrm{d}x = 2\sqrt{2}n \end{aligned} $$ ##### （3） **【例 4】** 设 $n$ 为正整数，证明： $$\int_{0}^{2\pi} \cos^n x \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x = \begin{cases} 0, & n \text{ 为奇数}, \\ 4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x, & n \text{ 为偶数}. \end{cases} $$ **证明** $$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{2\pi} \cos^n x \mathrm{d}x &= \int_{0}^{2\pi} \sin^n\left(x + \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x + \frac{\pi}{2} = t} \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{2\pi + \frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n t \mathrm{d}t \\ &= \int_{0}^{2\pi} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x \quad (\sin^n x \text{ 以 } 2\pi \text{ 为周期}) \end{aligned} $$ 当 $n$ 为奇数时， $$\int_{0}^{2\pi} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x = \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x = 0$$ 当 $n$ 为偶数时， $$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{2\pi} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x &= \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x = 2\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x \\ &= 2\int_{-\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x = 4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x \end{aligned} $$ **注** 本题所证的结论是一个常用结论，如： $$\int_{0}^{2\pi} \cos^2 x \mathrm{d}x = 4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^2 x \mathrm{d}x = 4 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \pi$$ ##### （4） **【例 6】** 设 $f(x) = \int_{0}^{x} \frac{\sin t}{\pi - t} \mathrm{d}t$，计算 $\int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ **解** **（方法一）** $$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \mathrm{d}x &= \left. xf(x) \right|_{0}^{\pi} - \int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{x\sin x}{\pi - x} \mathrm{d}x \\ &= \pi\int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{\sin t}{\pi - t} \mathrm{d}t - \int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{x\sin x}{\pi - x} \mathrm{d}x \\ &= \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin x \mathrm{d}x = 2 \end{aligned} $$ ##### （5） **（方法二）** $$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \mathrm{d}x &= \int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \mathrm{d}(x - \pi) \\ &= \left. (x - \pi)f(x) \right|_{0}^{\pi} - \int_{0}^{\pi} \frac{(x - \pi)\sin x}{\pi - x} \mathrm{d}x \\ &= \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin x \mathrm{d}x = 2 \end{aligned} $$ **（方法三）** $$ \begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{\pi} f(x) \mathrm{d}x &= \int_{0}^{\pi} \mathrm{d}x \int_{0}^{x} \frac{\sin t}{\pi - t} \mathrm{d}t \\ &= \int_{0}^{\pi} \mathrm{d}t \int_{t}^{\pi} \frac{\sin t}{\pi - t} \mathrm{d}x = \int_{0}^{\pi} \sin t \mathrm{d}t = 2 \end{aligned} $$ （累次积分交换次序）
