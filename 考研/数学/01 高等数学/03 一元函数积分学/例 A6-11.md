> [!question] 
> 设 $f'(\mathrm{e}^x) = \sin x$，求 $f(x)$

**法一**

将括号内化为单元，令 $\mathrm{e}^x = t$，则 $f'(t) = \sin\ln t$，从而 

$$ \begin{aligned} f(t) &= \int \sin\ln t\,\mathrm{d}t = t\sin\ln t - \int t\cos\ln t \cdot \frac{1}{t}\mathrm{d}t \\ &= t\sin\ln t - t\cos\ln t - \int t\sin\ln t \cdot \frac{1}{t}\mathrm{d}t \end{aligned} $$

则 $f(t) = \frac{t}{2}(\sin\ln t - \cos\ln t) + C$ 

**法二**

等式 $f'(\mathrm{e}^x) = \sin x$ 两端对 $\mathrm{e}^x$ 积分得 

$$ \begin{aligned} f(\mathrm{e}^x) &= \int \sin x\,\mathrm{d}\mathrm{e}^x = \mathrm{e}^x\sin x - \int \mathrm{e}^x\cos x\,\mathrm{d}x = \mathrm{e}^x\sin x - \mathrm{e}^x\cos x - \int \sin x\,\mathrm{d}\mathrm{e}^x \end{aligned} $$

则 $f(\mathrm{e}^x) = \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{2}(\sin x - \cos x) + C$，从而 

$$ f(x) = \frac{x}{2}(\sin\ln x - \cos\ln x) + C $$
