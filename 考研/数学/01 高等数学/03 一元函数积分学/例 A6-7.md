> [!question] 
> $$I = \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sin x \cdot \cos^4 x}$$ 

**法一**

本题满足 $R(-\sin x,\cos x) = -R(\sin x,\cos x)$，所以应凑 $\mathrm{d}\cos x$ 

$$ \begin{aligned} I &= \int \frac{\sin x\mathrm{d}x}{\sin^2 x \cdot \cos^4 x} \\&= -\int \frac{\mathrm{d}\cos x}{(1-\cos^2 x)\cos^4 x}\end{aligned} $$

换元，令 $\cos x = u$，加项减项拆

$$
 \begin{aligned}
 &= -\int \frac{\mathrm{d}u}{(1-u^2)u^4} \\&= -\int \frac{(1-u^4)+u^4}{(1-u^2)u^4}\mathrm{d}u \\ &= -\int \left(\frac{1}{u^4} + \frac{1}{u^2}\right)\mathrm{d}u - \int \frac{\mathrm{d}u}{1-u^2} \\ &= \frac{1}{3u^3} + \frac{1}{u} - \frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{1+u}{1-u}\right| + C \\ &= \frac{1}{3\cos^3 x} + \frac{1}{\cos x} - \frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{1+\cos x}{1-\cos x}\right| + C \end{aligned} 
$$

**法二**

将 1 化为 $\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x$，再裂项，必要时多次进行
 
$$ \begin{aligned} I &= \int \frac{\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x}{\sin x \cdot \cos^4 x}\mathrm{d}x \\&= \int \frac{\sin x}{\cos^4 x}\mathrm{d}x + \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sin x\cos^2 x} \\ &= \frac{1}{3\cos^3 x} + \int \frac{\sin^2 x+\cos^2 x}{\sin x \cdot \cos^2 x}\mathrm{d}x \\ &= \frac{1}{3\cos^3 x} + \int \frac{\sin x}{\cos^2 x}\mathrm{d}x + \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sin x} \\ &= \frac{1}{3\cos^3 x} + \frac{1}{\cos x} - \ln|\csc x+\cot x| + C \end{aligned} $$
