> [!question] 
> 设方程 $F(\displaystyle\frac{x}{z},\frac{z}{y})=0$ 可确定函数 $z=z(x,y)$，求 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$ 和 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$

这种不给出具体式子的用[[隐函数求导法#方法|隐函数求导公式]]更方便

$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=-\frac{\frac{1}{z}F'_1}{-\displaystyle\frac{x}{z^2}F'_1+\frac{1}{y}F'_2}=\frac{yzF'_1}{xyF'_1-z^2F'_2}$$

$$\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=-\frac{-\displaystyle\frac{z}{y^2}F'_2}{-\displaystyle\frac{x}{z^2}F'_1+\frac{1}{y}F'_2}=\frac{-z^3F'_2}{y(xyF'_1-z^2F'_2)}$$
