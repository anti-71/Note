> [!question] 
> 设 $f(x,y)=\dfrac{2x+3y}{1+xy\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}$，求 $f'_x(0,0)$ 和 $f'_y(0,0)$

$$ f'_x(0,0)=\left.\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}f(x,0)\right|_{x=0}=\left.\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}(2x)\right|_{x=0}=2, $$

$$ f'_y(0,0)=\left.\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}y}f(0,y)\right|_{y=0}=\left.\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}y}(3y)\right|_{y=0}=3 $$

> [!note] 
> 求具体点处的偏导数可用先代后求的方法往往比较简单
