> [!question] 
> 设
> 
> $$f(x,y)=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\sqrt{|x|}}{x^2+y^2}\sin(x^2+y^2),&(x,y)\neq(0,0),\\0,&(x,y)=(0,0),\end{cases}$$
> 
> 求 $f'_x(0,0)$ 和 $f'_y(0,0)$

根据[[偏导数的定义]]，

$$\lim\limits_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{f(\Delta x,0)-f(0,0)}{\Delta x}=\lim\limits_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\dfrac{\sqrt{|\Delta x|}}{(\Delta x)^2}\sin(\Delta x)^2}{\Delta x}=\lim\limits_{\Delta x\to0}\dfrac{\sqrt{|\Delta x|}}{\Delta x}=\infty$$

则 $f'_x(0,0)$ 不存在，而

$$ f'_y(0,0)=\lim\limits_{\Delta y\to0}\dfrac{f(0,\Delta y)-f(0,0)}{\Delta y}=\lim\limits_{\Delta y\to0}\dfrac{0-0}{\Delta y}=0 $$

> [!note] 
> 求分段函数在分界点处的偏导数一般都是用定义
