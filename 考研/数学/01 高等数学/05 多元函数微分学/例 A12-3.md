> [!question] 
> 设 $z=\ln(1+xy^2)$，则 $\left.\dfrac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y}\right|_{(0,1)}=\underline{\qquad}$

$$\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial x}=\dfrac{y^2}{1+xy^2}$$

$$\left.\dfrac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y}\right|_{(0,1)}=\left.\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}y}\left(\dfrac{\partial z(0,y)}{\partial x}\right)\right|_{y=1}=\left.\dfrac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}y}(y^2)\right|_{y=1}=2$$
