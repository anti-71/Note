> [!question] 
> 设函数 $u=f(x,y)$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，且满足
> 
> $$4\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+12\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial y}+5\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=0$$
> 
> 确定 $a,b$ 的值，使等式在变换 $\xi=x+ay,\eta=x+by$ 下简化为 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}=0$

**法一**

 从已知条件出发，将 u 视为 $\xi$ 和 $\eta$ 的函数

变量之间的关系如下图

![image.png|225x137](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/20260410130040141.png)

条件涉及三个二阶偏导数，一一求出

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial u}{\partial\xi}\cdot 1+\frac{\partial u}{\partial\eta}\cdot 1=\frac{\partial u}{\partial\xi}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial\eta}$$

$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}&=\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi^2}\cdot 1+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}\cdot 1+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta\partial\xi}\cdot 1+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta^2}\cdot 1\\ &=\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi^2}+2\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta^2} \end{aligned} $$

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial u}{\partial\xi}\cdot a+\frac{\partial u}{\partial\eta}\cdot b=a\frac{\partial u}{\partial\xi}+b\frac{\partial u}{\partial\eta}$$

$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}&=a\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi^2}\cdot a+a\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}\cdot b+b\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta\partial\xi}\cdot a+b\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta^2}\cdot b\\ &=a^2\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi^2}+2ab\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}+b^2\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta^2} \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial y}&=\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi^2}\cdot a+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}\cdot b+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta\partial\xi}\cdot a+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta^2}\cdot b\\ &=a\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi^2}+(a+b)\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}+b\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta^2} \end{aligned} $$

将以上三个二阶偏导数代入等式 $4\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+12\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial y}+5\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=0$ 得

$$(5a^2+12a+4)\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi^2}+[10ab+12(a+b)+8]\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}+(5b^2+12b+4)\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\eta^2}=0$$

要 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}=0$，则 $\begin{cases}5a^2+12a+4=0\\\\5b^2+12b+4=0\end{cases}$，且 $10ab+12(a+b)+8\neq0$，解得

$$\begin{cases}a=-2\\\\b=-\dfrac{2}{5}\end{cases}\ 或\ \begin{cases}a=-\dfrac{2}{5}\\\\b=-2\end{cases}$$

**法二**

从所求出发，将 u 视为 x 和 y 的函数，变量关系如下

![image.png|235x168](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/20260410130549050.png)

由 $\xi=x+ay,\eta=x+by$ 反表示

$$\begin{cases}x=\dfrac{a\eta-b\xi}{a-b}\\\\y=\dfrac{\xi-\eta}{a-b}\end{cases}$$

只需求导得出 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}$

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial\xi}=\frac{-b}{a-b}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{1}{a-b}\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$$

$$ \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}&=\frac{-b}{a-b}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}\cdot\frac{a}{a-b}+\frac{-b}{a-b}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial y}\cdot\frac{-1}{a-b}+\frac{1}{a-b}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y\partial x}\cdot\frac{a}{a-b}+\frac{1}{a-b}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}\cdot\frac{-1}{a-b}\\ &=\frac{-ab}{(a-b)^2}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{a+b}{(a-b)^2}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial y}+\frac{-1}{(a-b)^2}\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2} \end{aligned} $$

欲使 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial\xi\partial\eta}=0$，即

$$-ab\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+(a+b)\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x\partial y}-\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=0$$

与已知关系式比较得

$$\frac{-ab}{4}=\frac{a+b}{12}=\frac{-1}{5}$$

由此解得

$$\begin{cases}a=-2\\\\b=-\dfrac{2}{5}\end{cases}\ 或\ \begin{cases}a=-\dfrac{2}{5}\\\\b=-2\end{cases}$$
