##### 定义

如果 $z = f(x,y)$ 在区域 $D$ 内的偏导函数 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$ 仍然存在偏导数，则称之为函数 $f(x,y)$ 的 **二阶偏导数**，常记为

$$
\begin{aligned}
 &\frac{\partial }{\partial x}\left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right) = \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} \ \text{或} \ f_{xx}'', \\ &\frac{\partial }{\partial y}\left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right) = \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} \ \text{或} \ f_{xy}'', \\ &\frac{\partial }{\partial x}\left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \right) = \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y \partial x} \ \text{或} \ f_{yx}'',  \\ &\frac{\partial }{\partial y}\left( \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \right) = \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} \ \text{或} \ f_{yy}''
 \end{aligned}
$$

常称 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y \partial x}$ 为 **混合偏导数**

##### 定理

如果函数 $z = f(x,y)$ 的两个二阶混合偏导数 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y}$ 及 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y \partial x}$ 在区域 $D$ 内 **连续**，则在该区域内这两个混合偏导数必定相等，即

$$
f_{xy}''(x,y)=f_{yx}''(x,y)
$$
