> [!question] 
> 设 $(r,\theta)$ 为极坐标，$u=u(r,\theta)$ 具有二阶连续偏导数，并满足 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial\theta}\equiv0$，且 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=0$，求 $u(r,\theta)$

由 $\displaystyle\frac{\partial u}{\partial\theta}\equiv0$ 知 $u$ 仅为 $r$ 的函数

故令 $u=\varphi(r)$，其中 $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$

利用题目所给条件，求两个二阶偏导

$$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\varphi'(r)\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}=\varphi'(r)\frac{x}{r}$$

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}=\varphi''(r)\frac{x^2}{r^2}+\varphi'(r)\frac{r-\frac{x^2}{r}}{r^2}=\varphi''(r)\frac{x^2}{r^2}+\varphi'(r)(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{x^2}{r^3})$$

由对称性知

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=\varphi''(r)\frac{y^2}{r^2}+\varphi'(r)(\frac{1}{r}-\frac{y^2}{r^3})$$

则

$$\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}+\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial y^2}=\varphi''(r)+\varphi'(r)\frac{1}{r}$$

从而得 $\varphi''(r)+\varphi'(r)\frac{1}{r}=0$，即 $r\varphi''(r)+\varphi'(r)=0$

判断方程为[[可降阶的高阶方程]]，但是有更快的方法

观察到 $[r\varphi'(r)]'=0$，则 $r\varphi'(r)=C_1\implies\varphi(r)=C_1\ln r+C_2$，故

$$u=C_1\ln r+C_2$$
