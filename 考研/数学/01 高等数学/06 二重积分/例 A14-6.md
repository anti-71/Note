> [!question] 
> 设$D$是全平面，$f(x) = \begin{cases} x, & -1 \leqslant x \leqslant 2, \\\\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{cases}$
> 
> 计算 $\displaystyle\iint\limits_D f(x)f(x^2-y)\mathrm{d}\sigma$

由题设知 $f(x^2-y) = \begin{cases} x^2-y, & -1 \leqslant x^2-y \leqslant 2, \\\\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{cases}$

则

$$ f(x)f(x^2-y) = \begin{cases} x(x^2-y), & -1 \leqslant x^2-y \leqslant 2,-1 \leqslant x \leqslant 2, \\\\ 0, & \text{其他} \end{cases} $$

那么积分域的有效区域为（下图阴影部分）

![image.png|286x248](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/20260415144948564.png)

[[利用直角坐标计算]]

$$ \text{原式} = \int_{-1}^2 \mathrm{d}x \int_{x^2-2}^{x^2+1} x(x^2-y)\mathrm{d}y = \frac{9}{4} $$
