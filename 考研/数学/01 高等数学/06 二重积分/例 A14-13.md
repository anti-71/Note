> [!question] 
> 设区域 $D$ 由 $x^2 + y^2 \leqslant y$ 和 $x \geqslant 0$ 所确定，$f(x,y)$ 为 $D$ 上的连续函数，且 $f(x,y) = \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2} - \dfrac{8}{\pi}\displaystyle\iint\limits_D f(u,v)\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v$
> 
> 求 $f(x,y)$

先画积分域

![image.png|233x229](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/20260416145232671.png)

**法一**

令

$$\displaystyle\iint\limits_D f(u,v)\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v = A\tag{1}$$

则 $f(x,y) = \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2} - \dfrac{8}{\pi}A$，代入 1 式得

$$\displaystyle\iint\limits_D \left(\sqrt{1-x^2-y^2} - \dfrac{8}{\pi}A\right)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = A$$

即 $\displaystyle\iint\limits_D \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y - A = A$（$S_D=\dfrac{\pi}{8}$），于是

$$A = \dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle\iint\limits_D \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \dfrac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{\sin\theta}\sqrt{1-r^2}r\mathrm{d}r = \dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$$

故 $f(x,y) = \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2} - \dfrac{4}{3\pi}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$

**法二**

回顾在定积分中对此类题的做法

等式 $f(x,y) = \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2} - \dfrac{8}{\pi}\displaystyle\iint\limits_D f(u,v)\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v$ 两端在区域 $D$ 上做二重积分得 

$$\displaystyle\iint\limits_D f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \iint\limits_D \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y - \iint\limits_D f(u,v)\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v$$

则 $\displaystyle\iint\limits_D f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \dfrac{1}{2}\iint\limits_D \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$（方法一中已算过）

故 $f(x,y) = \sqrt{1-x^2-y^2} - \dfrac{4}{3\pi}\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{2}{3}\right)$
