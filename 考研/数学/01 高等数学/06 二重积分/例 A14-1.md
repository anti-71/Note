> [!question] 
> 设区域 $D$ 为 $x^2+y^2 \leqslant R^2$，则 $\displaystyle\iint\limits_D\left(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}\right)\mathrm{d}\sigma =\underline{\qquad}$

**法一**

积分域 $D$ 是半径为 $R$ 的圆域，考虑[[利用极坐标计算]]（积分过程略）

$$ \begin{aligned} \iint\limits_D\left(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}\right)\mathrm{d}\sigma &= \int_0^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_0^R\left(\dfrac{\cos^2\theta}{a^2}+\dfrac{\sin^2\theta}{b^2}\right)r^3\mathrm{d}r \\ &= \dfrac{\pi R^4}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{a^2}+\dfrac{1}{b^2}\right) \end{aligned} $$

**法二**

由于积分域 $D$ 关于直线 $y=x$ 对称，[[利用变量的轮换对称性计算]]

$$ \iint\limits_D\left(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}\right)\mathrm{d}\sigma = \iint\limits_D\left(\dfrac{y^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{x^2}{b^2}\right)\mathrm{d}\sigma $$

从而有

$$ \begin{aligned} \iint\limits_D\left(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}\right)\mathrm{d}\sigma &= \dfrac{1}{2}\iint\limits_D\left[\left(\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}\right)+\left(\dfrac{y^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{x^2}{b^2}\right)\right]\mathrm{d}\sigma \\ &= \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{a^2}+\dfrac{1}{b^2}\right)\iint\limits_D(x^2+y^2)\mathrm{d}\sigma \\  \end{aligned} $$

此时被积函数[[利用极坐标计算]]更容易

$$
 \begin{aligned} &= \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{a^2}+\dfrac{1}{b^2}\right)\int_0^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_0^R r^3\mathrm{d}r \\ &= \dfrac{\pi R^4}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{a^2}+\dfrac{1}{b^2}\right) \end{aligned} 
$$
