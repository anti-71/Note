> [!question] 
> 设 $f(x,y)$ 在单位圆 $x^2 + y^2 \leqslant 1$ 上有连续一阶偏导数，且在边界上取值为零
> 
> 求证：
> 
> $$f(0,0) = \lim\limits_{\varepsilon\to 0^+} \dfrac{-1}{2\pi}\displaystyle\iint\limits_D \dfrac{x f'_x + y f'_y}{x^2 + y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$$
> 
> 其中：$D$ 为圆环域 $\varepsilon^2 \leqslant x^2 + y^2 \leqslant 1$，$\varepsilon > 0$

**证明**：

从积分域和被积函数不难看出，应[[利用极坐标计算]]，将本题中的重积分化为累次积分

$$
\iint\limits_D \dfrac{x f'_x + y f'_y}{x^2 + y^2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y = \int_{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{\varepsilon}^{1} \left[\cos\theta f'_x(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta) + \sin\theta f'_y(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)\right]\mathrm{d}r
$$

凑微分得

$$ \begin{aligned}  &= \int_{0}^{2\pi} \left[f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)\bigg|_{\varepsilon}^{1}\right]\mathrm{d}\theta \\&= -\int_{0}^{2\pi} f(\varepsilon\cos\theta,\varepsilon\sin\theta)\mathrm{d}\theta \\ &= -2\pi f(\varepsilon\cos\bar{\theta},\varepsilon\sin\bar{\theta}),\quad\bar{\theta} \in [0,2\pi] \end{aligned} $$

$f(x,y)$ 连续，则

$$\lim\limits_{\varepsilon\to 0^+} f(\varepsilon\cos\bar{\theta},\varepsilon\sin\bar{\theta}) = f(0,0)$$

证毕
