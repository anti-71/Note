取

$$
 \boldsymbol{\alpha}_1 = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} \\ a_{21} \\ \vdots \\ a_{m1} \end{pmatrix},\quad \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2 = \begin{pmatrix} a_{12} \\ a_{22} \\ \vdots \\ a_{m2} \end{pmatrix},\quad \dots,\quad \boldsymbol{\alpha}_n = \begin{pmatrix} a_{1n} \\ a_{2n} \\ \vdots \\ a_{mn} \end{pmatrix},\quad \boldsymbol{\beta} = \begin{pmatrix} b_1 \\ b_2 \\ \vdots \\ b_m \end{pmatrix} 
$$

则

$$
 A_{m \times n}\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{\beta} \iff x_1\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1 + x_2\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2 + \dots + x_n\boldsymbol{\alpha}_n = \boldsymbol{\beta} 
$$
