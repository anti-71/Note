> [!question] 
> 设 $A$ 是 3 阶矩阵，$\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3$ 线性无关，且 $A\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2$，$A\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3$，$A\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1$，则 $|A|=\underline{\qquad}$

**法一**

利用已知条件，得

$$A(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3)=(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1)=(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3)\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\1&1&0\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix}$$

记 $P=(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3)$，$B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\1&1&0\\0&1&1\end{pmatrix}$

由 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3$ 线性无关，知 $P$ 可逆，得 $P^{-1}AP=B$，即 $A$ 与 $B$ 相似

根据[[相似的性质#3）传递性|矩阵相似的性质]]

$$|A|=|B|=\begin{vmatrix}1&0&1\\1&1&0\\0&1&1\end{vmatrix}=2$$

或对 $A(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3)=(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3)B$ 左右同时求行列式，有

$$|A||\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3|=|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3||B|$$

由于 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3$ 线性无关，知 $(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3)$ 可逆，$|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3|\neq0$，故

$$|A|=|B|=\begin{vmatrix}1&0&1\\1&1&0\\0&1&1\end{vmatrix}=2$$

**法二**

由 $A(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3)=(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1)$，左右同时求行列式，得

$$ \begin{aligned} |A||\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3|&=|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|\\ &=2|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|\\
&=2|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2+\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,-\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,-\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2|\\
&=2|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,-\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,-\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2|\\
&=2|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3|, \end{aligned} $$

因为 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3$ 线性无关，所以 $|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3|\neq0$，从而有 $|A|=2$
