> [!question] 
> 设 $A=(a_{ij})_{3\times3}$，且 $|A|=2$，$B=\begin{pmatrix}a_{11}&a_{12}&3a_{11}+2a_{13}\\a_{21}&a_{22}&3a_{21}+2a_{23}\\a_{31}&a_{32}&3a_{31}+2a_{33}\end{pmatrix}$，求 $(A^*B)^{-1},|A^*B|$

观察矩阵 $B$，知 $B=AE_3(2)E_{31}(3)$，从而

$$A^*B=A^*AE_3(2)E_{31}(3)$$

根据[[方阵的伴随矩阵#2）重要结论|伴随矩阵]]

$$
=|A|E_3(2)E_{31}(3)=2E_3(2)E_{31}(3)
$$

故

$$
(A^*B)^{-1}=\left[2E_3(2)E_{31}(3)\right]^{-1}\\=\frac{1}{2}E_{31}^{-1}(3)E_3^{-1}(2)
$$

根据初等矩阵的公式

$$ \begin{aligned} \\ &=\frac{1}{2}E_{31}(-3)E_3\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=\frac{1}{2}\begin{pmatrix}1&0&-\dfrac{3}{2}\\0&1&0\\0&0&\dfrac{1}{2}\end{pmatrix}=\frac{1}{4}\begin{pmatrix}2&0&-3\\0&2&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix} \end{aligned} $$

而

$$|A^*B|=|2E_3(2)E_{31}(3)|=2^3|E_3(2)||E_{31}(3)|=2^4=16$$
