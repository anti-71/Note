对于齐次方程组

$$
 \begin{cases} a_{11}x_1 + a_{12}x_2 + \dots + a_{1n}x_n = 0\\ a_{21}x_1 + a_{22}x_2 + \dots + a_{2n}x_n = 0\\ \vdots \\ a_{m1}x_1 + a_{m2}x_2 + \dots + a_{mn}x_n = 0 \end{cases} \iff \boldsymbol{Ax} = \boldsymbol{0}
$$

通过观察可知，它的解 $\boldsymbol{x}$ 与系数矩阵 $A$ 的所有 $n$ 维行向量都正交

记 $A = \begin{pmatrix} \boldsymbol{\alpha}_1 \\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2 \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m \end{pmatrix}$（注意 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_i$ 是行向量），则

$$
A\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{0} \iff \begin{pmatrix} \boldsymbol{\alpha}_1 \\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_2 \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol{\alpha}_m \end{pmatrix}\boldsymbol{x} = \boldsymbol{0} \iff \boldsymbol{\alpha}_i\boldsymbol{x} = 0 \iff (\boldsymbol{\alpha}_i,\boldsymbol{x}) = 0,\ i = 1,2,\dots,m
$$
