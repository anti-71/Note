> [!question] 
> 设 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\beta},\boldsymbol{\gamma}$ 均是 4 维列向量，且 $|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\beta}|=C_1,|\boldsymbol{\beta}+\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|=C_2$，则 $|2\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3|=\underline{\qquad}$

将 $|\boldsymbol{\beta}+\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|$ 拆成两个行列式之和，得

$$ |\boldsymbol{\beta}+\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|=|\boldsymbol{\beta},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|+|\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|=C_2 $$

又因为 $|\boldsymbol{\beta},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|=|\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\beta}|=C_1$，所以 $|\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|=C_2-C_1$，从而有

$$ |2\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3|=-2|\boldsymbol{\gamma},\boldsymbol{\alpha}_3,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_2,\boldsymbol{\alpha}_1|=2(C_1-C_2) $$
