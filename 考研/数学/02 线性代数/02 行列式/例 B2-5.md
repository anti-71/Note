> [!question] 
> 请计算行列式
> 
> $$ \begin{vmatrix} x+m_1 & x & x \\ x & x+m_2 & x \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ x & x & & x+m_n \end{vmatrix} $$
> 
> 其中 $m_i \neq 0\ (i=1,2,\cdots,n)$

\* 的元素填 $x$，于是将第一行的元素的 -1 倍加到第 2 到 $n+1$ 行

$$ D = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & x & x & \cdots & x \\ 0 & x+m_1 & x & & x \\ 0 & x & x+m_2 & & \vdots \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \\ 0 & x & x & & x+m_n \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & x & \cdots & \cdots & x \\ -1 & m_1 & & & \\ -1 & & m_2 & & \\ \vdots & & & \ddots & \\ -1 & & & & m_n \end{vmatrix} $$

这个行列式显然是爪形行列式，我们比较熟悉了

$$ = \begin{vmatrix} \displaystyle1+\frac{x}{m_1}+\frac{x}{m_2}\cdots+\frac{x}{m_n} & & & & x \\ 0 & m_1 & & & \\ 0 & & \ddots & & \\ \vdots & & & \ddots & \\ 0 & & & & m_n \end{vmatrix} = \left(1+\frac{x}{m_1}+\frac{x}{m_2}+\cdots+\frac{x}{m_n}\right)(m_1m_2\cdots m_n) $$

或者在第一列填元素，思路差不多

$$ D = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ x & x+m_1 & x & & x \\ x & x & x+m_2 & & \vdots \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \\ x & x & x & & x+m_n \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & -1 & -1 & \cdots & -1 \\ x & m_1 & 0 & & 0 \\ x & 0 & m_2 & & \vdots \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \\ x & 0 & 0 & & m_n \end{vmatrix} $$
