##### 方法

对列分块矩阵的行列式，可利用行列式的线性性质对每一列拆开

$$
|\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_j+\beta_j,\dots,\alpha_n| = |\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_j,\dots,\alpha_n| + |\alpha_1,\dots,\beta_j,\dots,\alpha_n|
$$

也可将矩阵写成乘积形式，利用 $|AB| = |A||B|$ 计算

$$
\bigl|(\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n)K\bigr| = |\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_n| \cdot |K|
$$

由此可建立矩阵的相似关系 $AP = PB$ ，将抽象矩阵 $A$ 的行列式转化为具体矩阵 $B$ 的行列式计算

- 已知列向量 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ 的线性关系

  $$
  A(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3) = (\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)
  \begin{pmatrix}a_1 & b_1 & c_1 \\ a_2 & b_2 & c_2 \\ a_3 & b_3 & c_3\end{pmatrix}
  $$

  则 $A$ 与 $B = \begin{pmatrix}a_1 & b_1 & c_1 \\ a_2 & b_2 & c_2 \\ a_3 & b_3 & c_3\end{pmatrix}$ 相似

- 已知 $\alpha, A\alpha, A^2\alpha$ 及 $A^3\alpha = a\alpha + bA\alpha + cA^2\alpha$

  $$
  A(\alpha, A\alpha, A^2\alpha) = (\alpha, A\alpha, A^2\alpha)
  \begin{pmatrix}0 & 0 & a \\ 1 & 0 & b \\ 0 & 1 & c\end{pmatrix}
  $$

  则 $A$ 与 $B = \begin{pmatrix}0 & 0 & a \\ 1 & 0 & b \\ 0 & 1 & c\end{pmatrix}$ 相似

##### 例题 1

> [!question] 
> 设 $A = (\alpha_1,\alpha_2,2\alpha_3-\alpha_4+\alpha_2)$ ，$B = (\alpha_3,\alpha_2,\alpha_1)$ ，$C = (\alpha_1+2\alpha_2,\,2\alpha_2+3\alpha_4,\,\alpha_4+3\alpha_1)$ ，且 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\alpha_4$ 是 3 维列向量。若 $|B|=-1$ ，$|C|=-20$ ，求 $|A|$

对 $|A|$ 做初等列变换

$$
\begin{aligned}
|A| &= |\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\,2\alpha_3-\alpha_4+\alpha_2| \\[4pt]
&= |\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\,2\alpha_3-\alpha_4| \quad (\text{第 2 列的 }-1\text{ 倍加到第 3 列}) \\[4pt]
&= 2|\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3| - |\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_4|
\end{aligned}
$$

由 $B = (\alpha_3,\alpha_2,\alpha_1)$ 得

$$
|\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3| = -|\alpha_3,\alpha_2,\alpha_1| = -|B| = -(-1) = 1
$$

将 $C$ 写成矩阵乘积形式

$$
C = (\alpha_1+2\alpha_2,\,2\alpha_2+3\alpha_4,\,\alpha_4+3\alpha_1)
= (\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_4)
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 3 \\
2 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 3 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

故

$$
|C| = |\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_4| \cdot
\begin{vmatrix}
1 & 0 & 3 \\
2 & 2 & 0 \\
0 & 3 & 1
\end{vmatrix}
= |\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_4| \times 20
$$

代入 $|C| = -20$ 得 $|\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_4| = -1$

综上

$$
|A| = 2|\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3| - |\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_4| = 2 \times 1 - (-1) = \boxed{3}
$$

##### 例题 2

> [!question] 
> 设 $A$ 为 3 阶矩阵，向量 $\alpha, A\alpha, A^2\alpha$ 线性无关，且 $A^3\alpha + 2A^2\alpha = 3A\alpha$ ，求 $|A-E|$

令 $P = (\alpha, A\alpha, A^2\alpha)$ ，由 $\alpha, A\alpha, A^2\alpha$ 线性无关知 $|P| \neq 0$

$$
\begin{aligned}
AP &= A(\alpha, A\alpha, A^2\alpha) \\[4pt]
&= (A\alpha, A^2\alpha, A^3\alpha) \\[4pt]
&= (A\alpha, A^2\alpha, 3A\alpha - 2A^2\alpha) \\[4pt]
&= (\alpha, A\alpha, A^2\alpha)
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 0 & 0 \\
1 & 0 & 3 \\
0 & 1 & -2
\end{pmatrix}
= PB
\end{aligned}
$$

其中 $B = \begin{pmatrix}0 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 0 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 & -2\end{pmatrix}$ ，故 $P^{-1}AP = B$ ，即 $A$ 与 $B$ 相似

由[[相似的性质#4）其他性质|相似矩阵有相同的行列式]]，得

$$
|A-E| = |B-E| =
\begin{vmatrix}
-1 & 0 & 0 \\
1 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 1 & -3
\end{vmatrix}
= \boxed{0}
$$
