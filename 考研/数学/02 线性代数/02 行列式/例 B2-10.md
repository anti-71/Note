> [!question] 
> 请计算行列式
> 
> $$|A_{2n \times 2n}| = \begin{vmatrix} a_n & & & & & b_n \\ & \ddots & & & \ddots & \\ & & a_1 & b_1 & & \\ & & c_1 & d_1 & & \\ & \ddots & & & \ddots & \\ c_n & & & & & d_n \end{vmatrix}$$

可以先令 $n=3$ 找规律

按照第一行或第一列展开可得

$$ \begin{aligned} D_{2n} &= (a_nd_n - b_nc_n)D_{2(n-1)} \\ &= (a_nd_n - b_nc_n)(a_{n-1}d_{n-1} - b_{n-1}c_{n-1})D_{2(n-2)} \end{aligned} $$

从而可以此类推得到

$$ D_{2n} = (a_nd_n - b_nc_n)(a_{n-1}d_{n-1} - b_{n-1}c_{n-1}) \cdots (a_2d_2 - b_2c_2)(a_1d_1 - b_1c_1) = \prod_{i=1}^n (a_id_i - b_ic_i) $$
