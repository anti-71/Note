> [!question] 
> 设 $(X,Y)\sim N(0,1;4,9;0)$，则 $P\{X<Y-1\}= \underline{\qquad}$

由题意 $X\sim N(0,4)$，$Y\sim N(1,9)$，且 $\rho=0$，故 $X$ 与 $Y$ 独立

$$
X-Y\sim N(-1,\ 4+9)=N(-1,13)
$$

$$
P\{X<Y-1\} = P\{X-Y<-1\} = \Phi(0) = \frac12
$$

> [!note] 
> 若 $\rho=-\dfrac13$，结果仍为 $\dfrac12$，因为 $X-Y\sim N(-1,\ \sigma^2)$，$P\{X-Y<-1\}=\Phi(0)=\dfrac12$
