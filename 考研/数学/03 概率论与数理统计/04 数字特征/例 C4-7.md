> [!question] 
> 甲、乙两个盒子中各装有 2 个红球和 2 个白球，先从甲盒中任取一球，观察颜色后放入乙盒，再从乙盒中任取一球，令 $X,Y$ 分别表示从甲盒和乙盒中取到的红球的个数，则 $X$ 与 $Y$ 的相关系数为 $\underline{\qquad}$

由全概率公式计算联合分布

$$
\begin{aligned}
P\{X=0,Y=0\} &= P\{X=0\}P\{Y=0\mid X=0\} = \frac12\times\frac35 = \frac{3}{10} \\[4pt]
P\{X=0,Y=1\} &= \frac12\times\frac25 = \frac15 \\[4pt]
P\{X=1,Y=0\} &= \frac12\times\frac25 = \frac15 \\[4pt]
P\{X=1,Y=1\} &= \frac12\times\frac35 = \frac{3}{10}
\end{aligned}
$$

联合分布表如下

| $X\setminus Y$ | $0$ | $1$ | $p_{i\cdot}$ |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| $0$ | $\dfrac{3}{10}$ | $\dfrac15$ | $\dfrac12$ |
| $1$ | $\dfrac15$ | $\dfrac{3}{10}$ | $\dfrac12$ |
| $p_{\cdot j}$ | $\dfrac12$ | $\dfrac12$ | $1$ |

计算相关系数

$$
\begin{aligned}
E(X) &= \frac12,\quad E(Y) = \frac12,\quad E(XY) = 1\times1\times\frac{3}{10} = \frac{3}{10} \\[4pt]
D(X) &= \frac12\times\frac12 = \frac14,\quad D(Y) = \frac14 \\[4pt]
\rho_{XY} &= \frac{E(XY)-E(X)E(Y)}{\sqrt{D(X)}\sqrt{D(Y)}}
          = \frac{\dfrac{3}{10}-\dfrac14}{\dfrac14}
          = \frac{\dfrac1{20}}{\dfrac14} = \boxed{\frac15}
\end{aligned}
$$
