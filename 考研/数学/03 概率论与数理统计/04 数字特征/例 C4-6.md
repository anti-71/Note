> [!question] 
> 设随机变量 $X_1,X_2,\dots,X_n$（$n>1$）独立同分布，且其方差为 $\sigma^2>0$，令 $\overline{X}=\dfrac1n\sum\limits_{i=1}^n X_i$，则（ ）
> 
> （A）$\text{Cov}(X_1,\overline{X})=\dfrac{\sigma^2}{n}$
> 
> （B）$\text{Cov}(X_1,\overline{X})=\sigma^2$
> 
> （C）$D(X_1+\overline{X})=\dfrac{n+2}{n}\sigma^2$
> 
> （D）$D(X_1-\overline{X})=\dfrac{n+1}{n}\sigma^2$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cov}(X_1,\overline{X})
&= \text{Cov}\!\left(X_1,\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n X_i\right) \\
&= \frac1n\text{Cov}(X_1,X_1) + \frac1n\sum_{i=2}^n\text{Cov}(X_1,X_i) \\
&= \frac1n D(X_1) = \frac{\sigma^2}{n}
\end{aligned}
$$

（A）正确、（B）错误

$$
\begin{aligned}
D(X_1+\overline{X}) &= D\!\left[X_1+\frac1n(X_1+\cdots+X_n)\right] \\
&= D\!\left[\left(1+\frac1n\right)X_1+\frac1n X_2+\cdots+\frac1n X_n\right] \\
&= \left(1+\frac1n\right)^2 D(X_1) + \frac1{n^2}\sum_{i=2}^n D(X_i) \\
&= \frac{(n+1)^2}{n^2}\sigma^2 + \frac{n-1}{n^2}\sigma^2
   = \frac{n+3}{n}\sigma^2
\end{aligned}
$$

（C）（D）均不正确，故应选 A
