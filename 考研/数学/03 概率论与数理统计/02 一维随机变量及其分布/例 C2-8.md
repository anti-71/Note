> [!question] 
> 设 $X_1,X_2,X_3$ 是随机变量，且 $X_1 \sim N(0,1)$，$X_2 \sim N(0,2^2)$，$X_3 \sim N(5,3^2)$，$p_j = P\{-2 \leqslant X_j \leqslant 2\}(j=1,2,3)$，则（）
> 
> （A）$p_1 > p_2 > p_3$
> 
> （B）$p_2 > p_1 > p_3$
> 
> （C）$p_3 > p_1 > p_2$
> 
> （D）$p_1 > p_3 > p_2$

**法一**

$$
p_1 = P\{-2 \leqslant X_1 \leqslant 2\} = \Phi(2)-\Phi(-2)= 2\Phi(2)-1
$$

$$p_2 = P\{-2 \leqslant X_2 \leqslant 2\} = P\left\{-1 \leqslant \frac{X_2}{2} \leqslant 1\right\} = \Phi(1)-\Phi(-1)= 2\Phi(1)-1$$

因为 $\Phi(2) > \Phi(1)$，所以$p_1 > p_2$

$$ \begin{aligned} p_3 &= P\{-2 \leqslant X_3 \leqslant 2\} = P\left\{-\frac{7}{3} \leqslant \frac{X_3-5}{3} \leqslant -1\right\} \\ &= \Phi(-1)-\Phi\left(-\frac{7}{3}\right) \\ &= 1-\Phi(1)-\left[1-\Phi\left(\frac{7}{3}\right)\right] \\ &= \Phi\left(\frac{7}{3}\right)-\Phi(1) \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} p_2-p_3 &= 3\Phi(1)-1-\Phi\left(\frac{7}{3}\right) \\ &= 3\times 0.8413 -1-\Phi\left(\frac{7}{3}\right) > 0 \end{aligned} $$

得到 $p_2 > p_3$，故 $p_1 > p_2 > p_3$
