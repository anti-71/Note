> [!question] 
> X 服从参数为 $\dfrac12$ 的几何分布，Y 表 X 被 3 除的余数，求 Y 的分布律

$$
P\{X=k\}=\left(1-\frac12\right)^{k-1}\cdot\frac12=\frac1{2^k},\ k=1,2,3,\dots
$$

$$ \begin{aligned} \\ P\{Y=0\}&=P\{X=3\}+P\{X=6\}+P\{X=9\}+\dots\\ &=\frac1{2^3}+\frac1{2^6}+\frac1{2^9}+\dots\\ &=\frac{\dfrac1{2^3}}{1-\dfrac1{2^3}}=\frac17 \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} P\{Y=1\}&=P\{X=1\}+P\{X=4\}+P\{X=7\}+\dots\\ &=\frac12+\frac1{2^4}+\frac1{2^7}+\dots\\ &=\frac{\frac12}{1-\frac1{2^3}}=\dfrac12\times\dfrac87=\frac47 \end{aligned} $$

$$ \begin{aligned} P\{Y=2\}&=P\{X=2\}+P\{X=5\}+P\{X=8\}+\dots\\ &=\frac1{2^2}+\frac1{2^5}+\frac1{2^8}+\dots\\ &=\frac{\dfrac1{2^2}}{1-\dfrac1{2^3}}=\frac14\times\frac87=\frac27 \end{aligned} $$

所以 $Y\sim\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 2\\ \dfrac17 & \dfrac47 & \dfrac27 \end{pmatrix}$
