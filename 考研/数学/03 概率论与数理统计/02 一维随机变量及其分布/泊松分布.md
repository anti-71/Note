$X \sim P(\lambda)$：

$$
 P\{X = k\} = \dfrac{\lambda^k}{k!}\mathrm{e}^{-\lambda},\ k = 0,1,2,\cdots,
$$

参数 $\lambda > 0$

> [!note]
> $$
>  \sum_{k = 0}^{+\infty}\dfrac{\lambda^k}{k!}= 1+\lambda+\dfrac{\lambda^2}{2!}+\cdots+\dfrac{\lambda^n}{n!}+\cdots =\mathrm{e}^{\lambda}
> $$
