> [!question] 
> 设二维随机变量 $(X,Y)$ 的概率密度为
> 
> $$f(x,y)=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}\,\pi}\,\mathrm{e}^{-\dfrac{x^2-xy+y^2}{3}},\quad (x,y)\in\mathbb{R}^2$$
> 
> 则 $X$ 的边缘概率密度 $f_X(x)=\underline{\qquad}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_X(x) &= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(x,y)\,dy \\[4pt]
&= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}\,\pi}\,
   \mathrm{e}^{-\dfrac{x^2-xy+y^2}{3}}\,dy
\end{aligned}
$$

对指数部分配方

$$
\begin{aligned}
y^2-xy+x^2 &= \left(y-\frac{x}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{x^2}{4} + x^2 \\[4pt]
&= \left(y-\frac{x}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{3}{4}x^2
\end{aligned}
$$

于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_X(x) &= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}\,\pi}\,\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{4}}
   \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
   \mathrm{e}^{-\dfrac{(y-x/2)^2}{3}}\,dy \\[4pt]
&= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}\,\pi}\,\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{4}}
   \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}
   \mathrm{e}^{-\dfrac{(y-x/2)^2}{2\cdot(3/2)}}\,dy \\[4pt]
&= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}\,\pi}\,\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{4}}
   \cdot \sqrt{2\pi}\cdot\sqrt{\frac32} \\[4pt]
&= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi}}\,\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{x^2}{4}},\quad x\in\mathbb{R}
\end{aligned}
$$

也可写成正态分布标准形式

$$
f_X(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\cdot\sqrt{2}}\,
   \mathrm{e}^{-\dfrac{x^2}{2\cdot(\sqrt{2})^2}}
$$

即 $X\sim N(0,2)$，同理可得 $Y\sim N(0,2)$

> [!tip] 
> 配方的关键步骤：**配 $(y-a)^2$** → **凑 $2\sigma^2$** → **添 $\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma$**
