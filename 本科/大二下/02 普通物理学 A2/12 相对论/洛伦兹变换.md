##### 洛伦兹坐标变换

令 $\beta = \dfrac{v}{c}$，$\gamma = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}}$

**正变换**：
$$
x' = \dfrac{x - vt}{\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}},\quad y' = y,\quad z' = z,\quad t' = \dfrac{t - \dfrac{v}{c^2}x}{\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}}
$$

**逆变换**：
$$
x = \dfrac{x' + vt'}{\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}},\quad y = y',\quad z = z',\quad t = \dfrac{t' + \dfrac{v}{c^2}x'}{\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}}
$$

当 $v \ll c$ 时 $\beta \to 0$，洛伦兹变换退化为伽利略变换

##### 洛伦兹速度变换

**正变换**：
$$
u'_x = \dfrac{u_x - v}{1 - \dfrac{v}{c^2}u_x},\quad
u'_y = \dfrac{u_y}{1 - \dfrac{v}{c^2}u_x}\sqrt{1 - \beta^2},\quad
u'_z = \dfrac{u_z}{1 - \dfrac{v}{c^2}u_x}\sqrt{1 - \beta^2}
$$
