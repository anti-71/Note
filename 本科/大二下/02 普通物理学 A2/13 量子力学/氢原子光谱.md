##### 氢原子光谱的实验规律

![](https://shiyuzhe.oss-cn-hangzhou.aliyuncs.com/Tuchuang/f2156926c06c4248b8cc214f14e92747.png)

氢原子光谱是分立的线状光谱，每一条谱线具有确定的波长（频率）

**巴尔末公式**

1885 年，瑞士中学教师巴尔末发现了氢原子光谱在**可见光部分**的规律

$$
\lambda = B \dfrac{n^2}{n^2 - 4}, \quad n = 3, 4, 5, \dots
$$

$B = 365.47 \, \text{nm}$

当 $n = 3, 4, 5, \dots$ 时，分别为 $H_\alpha, H_\beta, H_\gamma, \dots$ 等谱线的波长

$$
\nu = \dfrac{c}{\lambda} = \dfrac{4c}{B} \left( \dfrac{1}{2^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right)
$$

令：$\tilde{\nu} = \dfrac{1}{\lambda}$，称为**波数**，即单位长度内完整波的个数

$$
\tilde{\nu} = \dfrac{1}{\lambda} = \dfrac{4}{B} \left( \dfrac{1}{2^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right) = R_H \left( \dfrac{1}{2^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right)\\R_H = 1.0973731 \times 10^7 \, \text{m}^{-1}
$$

$R_H$也称为**里德伯常数**

**里德伯公式**

$$
\tilde{\nu} = R_H \left( \dfrac{1}{k^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right), \quad \begin{cases} k = 1, 2, 3, \dots \\ n = k + 1, k + 2, \dots \end{cases}\\R_H = 1.0973731 \times 10^7 \, \text{m}^{-1}
$$

###### 光谱系列

**莱曼系（紫外光）**

$$
\tilde{\nu} = R_H \left( \dfrac{1}{1^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right), \quad n = 2, 3, \dots
$$

**巴尔末系（可见光）**

$$
\tilde{\nu} = R_H \left( \dfrac{1}{2^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right), \quad n = 3, 4, \dots
$$

**帕邢系（红外光）**

$$
\tilde{\nu} = R_H \left( \dfrac{1}{3^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right), \quad n = 4, 5, \dots
$$

**布拉开系（红外光）**

$$
\tilde{\nu} = R_H \left( \dfrac{1}{4^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right), \quad n = 5, 6, \dots
$$

**普丰德系（红外光）**

$$
\tilde{\nu} = R_H \left( \dfrac{1}{5^2} - \dfrac{1}{n^2} \right), \quad n = 6, 7, \dots
$$

**里兹并合原理**

其他原子，每一条谱线的波数都可以表示为两项之差

$$
\tilde{\nu} = \dfrac{1}{\lambda} = T(k) - T(n), \quad n > k
$$

$T(k)$或$T(n)$就被称为**光谱项**
